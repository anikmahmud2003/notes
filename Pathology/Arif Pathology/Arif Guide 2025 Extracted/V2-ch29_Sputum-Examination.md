Q. How sputum is collected? Write the tests for sputum examination. (SU-14Ju)
Y. Short note: Sputum collection and examination. (CU-14/12Ju)

Q. Short note: Sputum examination. (RU-13Ju, SU-10Ju)

Ans.

Sputum examination: (Sputum is mucus that is coughed up from the lower airways)

Collection of sputum: Sputum should be collected in a clean, dry, wide necked container. For culture it
should preferably be collected in sterile container. Sputum is best collected in the morning before any mouth
washed is used. The person should cough deeply to produce a sputum specimen.

Examinations:

1. NACKED EYE EXAMINATION:
a. Presence of blood (hemoptysis):

Pulmonary tuberculosis

Bronchiectasis

Mitral stenosis

Pneumonia

Lung abscess

Bronchogenic carcinoima

b. Amount
« Scanty in bronchitis, bronchial asthma, early stage of pneumonia.
= Copious in pulmonary edema, lung abscess, advanced TB.

c. Colour
= White & mucoid in bronchitis, bronchial asthma.
= Rusty in pneumonia.
« Yellow or green in purulent state.
= Pink frothy in pulmonary edema.
» Blood-stained in hemoptysis.

d.

Consistency

* Mucoid in chronic bronchitis, early TB.

* Purulent in acute and chronic bronchitis, bacterial pneumonia & bronchopneumonia, lung
abscess.

* Mucopurulent in all bacterial infection of upper respiratory tract.

* Viscid and rusty in pneumonia.

* Frothy sputum in pulmonary edema.

« Anchovy sauce in amoebic abscess.

Odour
= Sputum is usually odourless. It is offensive or putrid (foul smelling) in bronchiectasis, lung

abscess and gangrene.

MICROSCOPIC EXAMINATION:

Ziehl-Neelsen staining: Done for demonstration of tubercle bacilli.

Gram staining: Done for demonstration of common bacteria, actinomyces and fungi.

Leishman staining: It is done to identify eosinophils and other leukocytes and pus cells.
Unstained cover slip preparation: Done for demonstration of Entamoeba histolytica, hydatid
cyst etc.

KOH preparation: Done for demonstration of Aspergillus species.

Demonstration of malignant cells: Papinicolaou stain, or haematoxylin and eosin stain is used.

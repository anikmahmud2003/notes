Q. Short note on: VDRL test. (DU-03M)
Ans.
VDRL (Venereal Disease Research Laboratory) test:
* It is mostly used simple and rapid test for syphilis.
* It is non-specific but a useful screening test.
« It becomes positive within 3-4 weeks or primary infection.
* It generally becomes negative by 6 months after treatment.

Advantage:
ii) Simple and easy to perform.
ili) Inexpensive.
iv) It is suitable as screening test and is positive in about 70% of primary and 98% of secondary syphilis.

Disadvantage:
i) Non-specific, false positive results may occur in some diseases like malaria, measles, infectious
mononucleosis, leprosy, SLE, rheumatic disorders etc.
ii) May also become negative in late latent stage.
ili) The VDRL antigen must be prepared fresh daily.
(Ref: Prof. Akram/3"/241,242)

Q. Mention causes of hypoalbuminaemia. (RU-17Ja,11Ju)
Ans.

Causes of hvpoalbuminaemia:
1. Decreased protein intake: Protein-energy malnutrition (PEM)

2. Decreased protein absorption: Protein losing enteropathy
3. Decreased albumin synthesis: Chronic liver disease (CLD) & cirrhosis of liver
4. Increased protein loss: Nephrotic syndrome, acute glomerulonephritis (AGN)

Q. Short note on: Pregnancy test. (DU-23/06M)
Pregnancy test; Pregnancy test based on the presence of HCG in the urine and blood. In blood- as early as 6
days after conception. In urine- as early as 14 days after conception.

(Ref: Lecture SSMC)

Indications:
1. Diagnosis of pregnancy in an early stage
2. Confirmation when the foetus is suspected to be dead
3. Diagnosis of hydatidiform mole and choriocarcinoma
4. Diagnosis of pseudocyst.

Principle: The presence of HCG in the urine of pregnant woman forms the basis of all pregnancy tests.
Immunologically pregnancy can be diagnosed by the test involving the principle of antigen antibody reaction.

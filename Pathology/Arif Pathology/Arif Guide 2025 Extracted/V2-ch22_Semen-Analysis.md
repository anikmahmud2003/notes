Q. How semen is collected for analysis? (DU-15Ja)
Ans

Semen collection procedure:

1. Preparation of the patient:
* Patient should be instructed to collect semen with a sexual abstinence of 3-5 days.
* Should be obtained by masturbation (self-stimulation) or collect at home during the act of
intercourse (by coitus interruptus). Condom should be avoided, as it contains spermicidal agent.
* Patient should evacuate his bladder before ejaculation.
= Patient must wash hands before collection.

2. Collection of specimen and transport:

* Should be collected in a dry, clean plastic container provided by the laboratory.

* Condom collection is unacceptable.

=  Patient’s name, age, date and time of collection should be recorded.

* Sample should be taken to the laboratory within 1 hour of collection and kept at body temperature.
(Ref: Lecture SSMC, BSMMU Lab. Medicine & AFIP Clinical Pathology)

Q. Short note: Normozoospermia. (RU-18M)
Ans.

Semen analysis: Semen analysis measures the quality and quantity of the fluid secreted during ejaculation. It
evaluates both liquid portion called semen or seminal fluid, and microscopic moving cells called sperm.
It is often used to study the fertility status of a male.

Basic semen analysis measures:
1. Semen volume
2. Sperm concentration
3. Sperm motility
4. Sperm morphology

Additional pH & fructose levels (absence of fructose or high pH may be associated with ejaculatory, tract,
obstruction or seminal vesicle dysfunction) & WBC counts within semen can also be done.

Q. Short note: Oligoasthenozoospermia. (BUP-21N/M)
Ams.
Ofigeasthenozoospermia: It is a sperm disorder that involves two disorders at the same time-

a) Oligospermia: Low sperm count.

b) Asthenozoospermia: Abnormal sperm motility with more than 60% of sperm being immotile or
unable to move in a straight direction.

Causes:
a) In most cases causes are unknown / idiopathic.
b) Testicular diseases e.g. orchitis.
c) Some genetic disorders e.g. Klinefelter’s syndrome, immotile cilia syndrome.
4) Immunologic.

Treatment: Treatment of reversible cause, if any.

Q. Define pseudocyesis. (RMU-23M, 19Nv)

Ans.

Pseudocyesis (false pregnancy): Pseudocyesis is a syndrome when a woman shows all the symptoms of being
pregnant except for the presence of a fetus.

Their belly grows like a pregnant woman, and they can even go into false labour.

Q. How will you proceed to diagnose a case of male (RU-15/13Ju) and female infertility? (RU-13Ju)
Q. Short note: Infertility test.

Ans.

Infertility test: Infertility test are meant to establish the reason why a couple is not able to conceive naturally.
Overall, 20-30% of the cases of infertility are due to a problem with the male partner.

Diagnosis of male infertility:

1. Medical, sexual and social history
2. General physical examination (including an examination of genitals)

3. Specific fertility tests:
= Semen analysis
- Physical, chemical and microscopic test
- Immunological assay
- Anti-sperm antibody test
- Sperm function test
- Post ejaculatory urine analysis
= Hormone testing (testosterone and other male hormones)
*  Transrectal and scrotal ultrasound
= Genetic testing

Diagnosis of female infertility:
1. Medical, sexual and social history
2. General physical examination (including a regular gynaecological examination)
3. Specific fertility tests:

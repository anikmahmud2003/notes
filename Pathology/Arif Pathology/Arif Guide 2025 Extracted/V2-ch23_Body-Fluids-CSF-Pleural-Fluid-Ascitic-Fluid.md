Q. What is pleocytosis? (DU-10Ju)

Ans.

Pteocytosis: Pleocytosis is a term used to describe an increased cell count (Gk. pleion = more), usually
referring to an increase in white blood cell (WBC) count in a bodily fluid, such as cerebrospinal fluid (CSF). It
is often defined specifically as an increased WBC count in CSF.

Importance: It is indicative of an inflammatory, infectious, or malignant condition.

Q. A patient with pyogenic meningitis comes to you, but CSF finding shows that CSF glucose is
high. How it can be possible?

Ans.
The patients has uncontrolled DM.

We know,

CSF glucose = 60% of blood glucose level

If blood glucose level = 100 mg /dl

Then, normal CSF = 60 mg/dl

If the patient has > 200 mg/dl blood glucose level
Then his normal CSF level will be > 120 mg/dl.

So, although this patient has pyogenic meningitis, CSF glucose will be higher than normal.

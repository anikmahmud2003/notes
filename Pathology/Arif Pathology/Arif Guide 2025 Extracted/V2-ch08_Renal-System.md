HISTOLOGY: The parenchyma of each kidney is composed of approximately one million microstructures
called nephrons. A nephron, in turn, consists of 5 major parts, each having a functional role in the formation
of urine: the glomerular capsule (glomerulus and Bowman’s capsule), the proximal convoluted tubule (PCT),
the loop of Henle, the distal convoluted tubule (DCT), and the collecting ducts.

The major function of glomerulus is complex filtration from the capillaries to the urinary space. The barrier to
glomerular filtration consists of the following 3 components:
i. Fenestrated endothelial cells lining the capillary loops.

ii. Glomerular basement membrane (GBM) on which the endothelial cells rest.
iii. Filtration slit pores between the foot processes of the visceral epithelial cells (podocytes) external to

Q Write in short about lipoid nephrosis (RU-07Ja)/ minimal change disease. (RU- 18M)
Q Short note: Minimal change disease. (RU-17M,10Ja)

Ans.
Minimal change disease (lipoid nephrosis): Minimal change disease (lipoid nephrosis) is seen most often in

young children but can also occur in older children and adults. It is the prototype of the nephrotic syndrome.

h ws characterized by diffuse effacement of foot processes of epithelial cells (podocytes) in glomeruli that
appear Virtually normal by light microscopy.

The peak incidence is between 2 and 6 years of age. The most characteristic feature of this condition is the
dramatic response to corticosteroid therapy.

Etielogy & pathogenesis: It involves some immune dysfunction, eventually resulting in the elaboration of a
cytokine that damages visceral epithelial cells (podocytes) and cause proteinuria. The ultrastructural changes
pomt to a primary visceral epithelial cell injury, and loss of glomerular polyanions. Thus, defects in the
charge bamer may contribute to the proteinuria.

Morphology:
Macroscopic: Kidney tends to be pale or even yellowish in colour and slightly larger & heavier than normal.
Microscopic:

* No visible basement membrane changes;

* Fused foot processes of podocytes;

* Lipid accumulation in renal tubular cells.

Prognosis: Despite massive proteinuria, renal function remains good, and the long term prognosis is excellent
for children and adults.
(Ref: Robbins/10"/914; BRS Pathology/5"/265)

Q. Why minimal change disease is called lipoid nephrosis?

Ans.

Minimal change disease is called lipoid nephrosis: The cells of the proximal tubules are often laden with
lipid and protein, reflecting tubular reabsorption of lipoproteins passing through diseased glomeruli, thus the

Types of renal stone: There are four main types of renal calculi-

including cystine, leading to cystinuria.

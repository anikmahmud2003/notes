Preface

“Bismitlahin Rakmanin Raho” “ss

First of all, I am revealing my gratefulness to almighty Allah for his endless compassion & mercy
to complete and publish the completely new (15"") edition-2025 of this book in meantime.

This New Edition 2025 is an exclusive one which is unique compared to the previous editions. It
contains the solutions of all the Prof. questions of different Universities (DU, CU, CMU, RU, RMU,
SUST, BSFMMU’S & BUP) up to Prof. November 2024 as well as assessment questions of different
Medical Colleges of recent batches.

Recently Robbins & Cotran PATHOLOGIC BASIS OF DISEASE 10” (New) edition has
become available in Bangladesh which is hugely different from the previous (9") edition. Systemic and
General Pathology part has been totally re-written using that new (10") edition Robbins Pathology
and the South Asia (11") edition as well, that has made the book unique and totally updated.

Use of “BRS Pathology”-5" Edition, “Hoffbrand, Moss & Pettit”-6" Edition and “Dacie &
Lewis”-10" Edition along with “de Gruchy”-6" Edition has made the Hematology portion peerless.
Moreover use of “UNDERGRADUATE BASIC HEMATOLOGY by Dr. Mainuddin Ahmed” has
made this book special for Rajshahi Medical College (RMC) and other medical colleges under
Rajshahi University (RU). Clinical Pathology portion is totally written from “Davidson’s Medicine” —
24" & 23" Edition that is always preferable by the teachers. Systemic and General Pathology are totally
written from “Robbins Pathology”-10" Edition, “BRS Pathology”-5" Edition, “Harsh Mohan”-7"
Edition, “Pathoma Lecture Notes”-New Edition & “Davidson’s Medicine” — 24" & 23" Edition
with the help of the lectures of different Medical colleges of Bangladesh.

In fact, no pains have been spared to make the book as attractive & appealing to the students as
possible & I hope that I have been able to provide a complete guide to the students which will not only
be of help in their class work, but will also give them a better understanding of the subject. The content
of this book is enriched with huge amount of information that will help the post graduate entrance
examinees also (MD/MS/M.Phil/Diploma/FCPS Part-I). It is also equally effective for the BDS students.

I have tried to make this book so complete that the readers need not to travel here and there to
search this and that during examination, because everything is here that you are searching for. This will
save both your time and money. MCQ (updated up to Prof. November 2024) has also been added that
has made this book more effective.

I would like to thank our honorable Teachers for their proper guidance to perform best so far. I
would also like to thank my dear MBBS classmates of batch SS-34, SSMC, my present & past
colleagues, seniors & juniors, my friends, my students of arif’s Medical Academy especially the
students of “Pathology course” & also the students of different Medical Colleges in Bangladesh & all of
my well-wishers. It is requested that the discrepancies, if any noticed may please be intimated to me, so
that it may be taken care of in the next edition.

Frank opinions, critical comments & constructive suggestions for further improvement of the
book will be thankfully acknowledged.

Dhaka Dr. Md. Arif Hossain
24" February, 2025 Sir Salimullah Medical College

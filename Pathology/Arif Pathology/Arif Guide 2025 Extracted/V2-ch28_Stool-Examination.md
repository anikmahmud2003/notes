Indication of routine examination of stool:

To find out parasitic infestation e.g. detection of ova, cyst, larvae of different parasites.
To detect type of dysentery

To detect type of jaundice

To see amount of fat in cystic disease of pancreas and obstructive jaundice

Occult blood test to see any GIT bleeding.

Q. Write procedure and interpretation of occult blood test in stool.

Q. Short note: Occult blood test (OBT). (DU-06S, BUP-22Nv, RU-19/18/17Nv, 16/14Ju,13/12Ja,
SU-17M, 1 1Ja,09Ju, CU-16Ju,07Ja)
Ans.

Occult blood test: The test is done to detect altered blood or trace amount of blood which is not visible.
Methods:

1. Orthotoluidine test
2. Hema-Check slide test

A. Orthotoluidine test:
Procedure:

1) Make an emulsion of the stool (size of a pea) in distilled water in a test tube.
2) Boil for 5 minutes and cool.

3) Place 4 drops of orthotoluidine and 1 drop of H,0, in a clean test tube, and leave for 1 minute.

Common ova found in stool:

= Enterobius vermicularis.

Q. What is melaena? What are the causes of melaena?
Ans.

Melaena: Melaena is the passage of black, tarry stools containing altered blood which is usually caused by
bleeding from the upper gastrointestinal tract.

Causes of bleeding from upper gastrointestinal tract (melaena):
1) Peptic ulcer disease (PUD) (H. Pylori, NSAIDs)
2) Gastric erosion (NSAIDs, Alcohol)
3) Oesophageal varices due to liver diseases with portal vein thrombosis
4) Cancer of stomach and esophagus
5) Esophagitis with hiatus hemia
6) Vascular malformation
7) Mallory-Weiss tear.

(Ref: Davidson’s/23"'/781)

Q. Write four causes of fresh blood in stool.

Ans.

Causes of fresh blood in stool (haematocazia): It may be found in bleeding from lower G.I. tract.
= Dysentery

Bleeding piles

Malignant disease (Ca. rectum, Ca. colon)

Ulcerative colitis

Crohn’s disease

Anorectal fissure, haemorrhoids

Meckel’s diverticulum.

(Ref: Davidson’s/23"*/782; Lecture SSMC & DMC)

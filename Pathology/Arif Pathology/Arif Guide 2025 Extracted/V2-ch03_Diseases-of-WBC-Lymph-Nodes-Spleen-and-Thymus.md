Ans.

Hodgkin lymphoma: Malignant neoplasm of lymph node characterized by the presence of neoplastic giant
cells called Reed-Sternberg cells (RS cells) and pleomorphic infiltration by \ymphocyte, cosinophil,
macrophage, plasma cell, neutrophil and follicular dendritic cells.

Criteria:
* Characteristically affects young adults (20-40 age groups).
* Males are affected twice as commonly as female.
* Arises in a single node or group of nodes and spreads first to anatomically contiguous lymphoid
tissues.
* Histological hallmark is presence of RS (Reed-Sternberg) cells.

Clinical features:
= May be asymptomatic.
* May present with painless peripheral lymphadenopathy (usually rubbery and discrete, but may
become matted)
Hepatosplenomegally
Systemic features (“B’ symptoms): Fever (Pel-Ebstein fever), night sweats, weight loss, pruritus.

Q. Short note: Neonatal Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS).
Ans.

Neonatal Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS): The most common cause of respiratory distress in

newbom is Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS), also known as hyaline membrane disease because of the

deposition of a layer of hyaline proteinaceous material in the peripheral airspaces of infants who succumb to
this condition.

“Itis the serious pulmonary disease that develops in infants born before their surfactant system is functional”,

Pathophysiology: The fundamental defect in RDS is pulmonary immaturity and deficiency of surfactant.

Q. Short note: Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS).

Ans.

Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS): The National Institute of Child Health and Human Development
defines SIDS as-

“The sudden death of an infant under 1 year of age which remains unexplained after a through case

investigation, including performance of a complete autopsy examination of the death scene, and review of the
clinical history.”

Because these infants usually die while asleep its other names are crib death or cot death.

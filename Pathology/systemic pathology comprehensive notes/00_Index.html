💡 Abbreviation policy (read once)

Every abbreviation in these notes is expanded in full the first time it appears in each chapter (e.g., AAA = abdominal aortic aneurysm, PAN = polyarteritis nodosa, ADPKD = autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease, SLE = systemic lupus erythematosus, TNM = tumour–node–metastasis staging). If you ever meet an acronym without its expansion, it is a prior-chapter expansion carried forward.

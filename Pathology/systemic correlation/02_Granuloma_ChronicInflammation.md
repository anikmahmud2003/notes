## 🎯 Master — granuloma dekhlei konta bhabbe?

| Granuloma | Centre | Giant cell / hallmark cell | Kothay | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **TB caseating** | Caseous + AFB edge (ZN+) | Langhans horseshoe + epithelioid slipper | Lung Ghon/cavity/miliary, gut, meninges, urine, tube, Pott spine, pericardium | Item-4 Q13–Q14; Item-11 Q8; Item-21; Item-7 Q3; Item-22 Q2 |

## A. TB granuloma — sob organ e same structure (Item-4 Q14 same answer)

```text
Caseous centre (AFB edge) → epithelioid → Langhans → CD4 rim → fibrosis
```

📌 Mantoux correlation (Item-4 Q14): induration mm — ≥5 (HIV/contacts), ≥10 (Bangladesh/DM/CKD/staff), ≥15 (low-risk). False-neg: miliary/HIV/sarcoid/measles/steroid/malnutrition. False-pos: BCG/atypical myco.

## B. Aschoff body — 3 stage (Item-3 Q8 same answer)

| Stage | Histology |
|---|---|
| 1–2 wk exudative | Fibrinoid + lymphocytes/plasma around vessel |
| 2–4 wk granulomatous | 🔴 Caterpillar Anitschkow + Aschoff giant + T cells |
| 2–4 mo healing | Spindle fibrous scar |

📌 Site: LV/posterior wall perivascular + subendocardium MacCallum plaque. Marker of active carditis.

## C. Crohn vs intestinal TB — sobcheye important viva trap (Item-11 Q4–Q8)

| Feature | Crohn | Intestinal TB |
|---|---|---|
| Ulcer | 🔴 Longitudinal, cobblestone | 🔴 Transverse |
| Granuloma | Non-caseating | Caseating + AFB |
| Wall | Transmural, skip, creeping fat, fistula/stricture | Mucosal-submucosal, strictures |
| Perianal | Common | Rare |

## D. Foamy / defective-phagocytosis pair (Item-7 Q2 vs Item-8 Q5)

- **Xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis:** foamy macrophages, yellow nodules, **mimics RCC** — nephrectomy specimen trap.
- **Malakoplakia bladder:** Michaelis-Gutmann bodies (defective lysosome), chronic E. coli cystitis.

> 🎯 **"Foamy kidney = xanthogranulomatous (RCC mimic); MG body bladder = malakoplakia."**

## E. Breast IGM vs TB vs Hashimoto — chronic inflammation trap

- IGM breast: non-caseating, young parous, mimic TB/cancer → biopsy must (Item-18 Q3).
- Hashimoto: germinal centre + Hurthle, anti-TPO >90%, MALT lymphoma risk (Item-19 Q5).

## 📌 Viva traps

1. Langhans giant cell TB chara ar kothay? → GPA, Crohn (epithelioid), BCG prostatitis.
2. Caterpillar cell kothay? → Shudhu Aschoff/Anitschkow (Item-3 Q9) — owl-eye transverse cut.
3. Caseation thakleo TB na? → GPA geographic necrosis (ANCA+), fungal.
4. Sterile pyuria + granuloma? → Urinary TB (Item-7 Q3).
5. Cobblestone + creeping fat? → Crohn, TB na.

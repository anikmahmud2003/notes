## 🎯 A. Cardiac enzymes (Item-2 Q10–Q11 same)

| Marker | Rise / Peak / Fall | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Troponin-I | 2–4 h / 24–48 h / 7–10 d | Most specific/sensitive necrosis gold |
| Troponin-T | 3–4 h / 24–48 h / 10–14 d | Same, renal trend careful |
| 🔴 CK-MB | 4–6 h / 24 h / 48–72 h | 🔴 Reinfarction (troponin still high, CK-MB 2nd rise) |
| Myoglobin 1–3 h | Earliest, non-specific | Early exclude |
| LDH flip 24–48 h, AST | Obsolete / non-specific | Late / liver confusion |
| BNP | Failure, NOT necrosis | HF post-MI |

> 🎯 **"Troponin Tells (longest), CK-MB Marks re-MI (quick return), Myoglobin first but unsure."**

## 🎯 B. LFT patterns — pre vs hepato vs obstructive (Item-12 Q6/Q10–Q11 same)

| Pattern | Bilirubin | ALT/AST | ALP/GGT | Urine / stool | PT vitK |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre (hemolysis) | Indirect <5, direct N | Normal | Normal | Acholuric absent, urobilinogen+++ dark stool | Normal |
| Hepatocellular | Mixed 5–10 | 🔴 ALT king >10× ALT>AST (alcohol ≥2:1 exception) | Mild <2–3× | Dark early, urobilinogen up→down | 🔴 NOT corrected (synthesis fail) |
| Obstructive | 🔴 Direct 20–30 | Mild <5× | 🔴 ALP/GGT >3–4× dominant | Beer urine+, clay acholic, urobilinogen absent | 🔴 Corrected (cholestasis malabsorption) |

📌 Normals: bili total 0.3–1.2 direct 0–0.25; ALT/AST ≤40; ALP 30–120; GGT M11–50 F7–33; albumin 3.5–5; PT 12–14 s.

### Jaundice bedside without blood (Item-12 Q9 same)

Pre mild lemon + anemia/spleen no itch; hepato moderate + viral prodrome/spiders/ascites mild itch; obstructive deep green + marked itch + colic stone vs painless Courvoisier pancreas + bradycardia/xanthelasma.

### Viral markers (Item-12 Q2 same)

HAV IgM recent / IgG immune; HBV HBsAg first >6 mo chronic, anti-HBs recovery/vaccine-only, IgM anti-HBc window-only, HBeAg replication, DNA PCR; HCV ELISA + RNA genotype; HDV needs HBs; HEV IgM pregnancy.

## 🎯 C. RFT + uric + creat (Item-7 Q8–Q9/Q24–Q25 same)

- Creat M0.7–1.3 F0.6–1.1 ideal (steady muscle, filtered not reabsorbed); doubling ≈ halving GFR. Urea 15–45 confounded diet/GI bleed/dehydration.
- Uric M3.4–7 F2.4–5.7: gout podagra/tophus + radiolucent stones + tumour-lysis AKI + metabolic syndrome + preeclampsia severity.
- CKD G1≥90 G2 60–89 G3a45–59 G3b30–44 G4 15–29 G5<15 dialysis.

## 🎯 D. Lipid numbers 200-100-40-150 (Item-1 Q10 same)

TC <200 / LDL <100 (<70 CAD/diabetic) / HDL ≥40M ≥50F (≥60 protective) / TG <150 (≥500 pancreatitis) / VLDL TG/5 / non-HDL <130 / TC:HDL <4.5. Sample 9–12 h fast.
📌 HDL hauls home (reverse ABCA1→LCAT→SR-B1→bile + PON1 antioxidant); LDL leaves in wall (oxLDL→scavenger unregulated→foam).

## 🎯 E. Glucose: DM criteria + OGTT + HbA1c (Item-20 Q3–Q7 same)

- Any one: FPG ≥7.0 (126) / 2-h OGTT ≥11.1 (200) / HbA1c ≥6.5% / random ≥11.1 + symptoms.
- IFG 6.1–6.9 + IGT 7.8–11.0 prediabetes; GDM first-recognized pregnancy; HbA1c invalid anemia/Hbopathy.
- T1 young thin antibody+ HLA-DR3/4 insulitis DKA insulin-must vs T2 older obese antibody− no HLA amyloid HHS OHA (Item-20 Q2).
- Renal glycosuria: normal sugar + urine glucose (SGLT2 low threshold) vs DM hyperglycemic (Item-7 Q30; Item-20 Q9).
- Benedict: blue neg / green trace / yellow-green ++ / yellow-orange +++ / brick ++++ — all reducers + vitC false (Item-7 Q23; Item-20 Q14).

## 🎯 F. TFT + PSA (Item-19 + Item-15 Q12 same)

- TSH best screen suppressed hyper (except TSH adenoma↑) + FT4/T3↑ + TRAb/TSI Graves diffuse vs focal hot vs low thyroiditis/factitious (Tg low)/struma.
- PSA organ-specific NOT cancer-specific: cancer+BPH/prostatitis/infarct/instrumentation/ejaculation; serial monitoring excellent, screening poor (overdiagnosis).

## 📌 Viva traps

1. Troponin high + CK-MB 2nd rise day 5? → reinfarction.
2. ALT 1200 + PT not corrected vitK? → hepatocellular failure; ALP 800 + PT corrected? → obstructive.
3. Creat double mane? → GFR half.
4. HDL 32 male? → low = major CAD risk.
5. Urine glucose + normal FBS? → renal glycosuria, DM na.

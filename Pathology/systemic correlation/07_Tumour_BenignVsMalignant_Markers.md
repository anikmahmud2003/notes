## 🎯 Master — benign vs malignant rule (sob organ e same)

| Feature | Benign | Malignant |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Slow, expansile, capsule | Rapid, infiltrative, no capsule |
| Cells | Bland, no atypia/mitosis, resembles parent | Pleomorphic, prominent nucleoli, mitoses, necrosis/hemorrhage |
| Spread | No mets | Lymphatic (carcinoma) / hematogenous (sarcoma) / transcoelomic (ovary) |
| Example pair | PA salivary vs mucoepidermoid; fibroadenoma vs NST; leiomyoma vs leiomyosarcoma; adenoma thyroid vs PTC; oncocytoma vs RCC | — |

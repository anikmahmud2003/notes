## A. Hydronephrosis vs hydrosalpinx vs bronchiectasis — same pathology, onno pipe

- Sob khetrei: obstruction + pooling → chronic infection → wall destruction → permanent dilatation.
- Kidney: pressure atrophy first concentrating defect; tube: cilia loss infertility; bronchus: elastic/SM destruction foul sputum.

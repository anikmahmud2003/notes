## A. PUD core (Item-10 Q4–Q11 same, ekhane condensed)

- Defence: mucus-bicarb + prostaglandin + NO + rapid turnover vs aggression acid-pepsin.
- H. pylori: urease ammonia + VacA/CagA → antral gastritis → ulcer + gastric Ca/MALT.
- Morphology benign: round punched, overhanging mucosa, clean base, radiating folds; malignant: heaped everted, necrotic, infiltrative.
- Complications: bleed (commonest), perforation (anterior duodenal), penetration (posterior → pancreas), obstruction (pyloric), malignant change (gastric only).

## B. Gastric vs duodenal (Item-10 Q16 same)

| Feature | Gastric | Duodenal |
|---|---|---|
| Site | Antrum/lesser curvature | Anterior bulb |
| Acid | Normal/low | High |
| Pain | Food-aggravated | Night, food-relief |
| Blood group | — | O+ |
| Malignant risk | Yes (biopsy must) | Almost never |

## C. IBD vs IBS (Item-11 Q14 same)

- IBD: organic inflammation, blood/mucus, weight loss, ESR/CRP↑, calprotectin↑.
- IBS: functional Rome criteria, no blood, no weight loss, normal labs/colonoscopy, stress-related.

## D. Genital ulcer pair (Item-16)

- Herpes: multiple painful vesicle→ulcer + Tzanck, recurrent, cesarean if active term, HIV risk↑.
- Chancre: single painless, darkfield+, heals even untreated → secondary rash.

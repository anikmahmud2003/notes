## 🎯 Master — size diye mone rakho

| Calibre | Disease | Key clue |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Large | **Giant cell/temporal (>50 yr)** + **Takayasu (<50 yr, pulseless)** | T-cell mediated, steroids |
| 🔴 Medium | **PAN** (HBsAg 30%), **Kawasaki** (child coronary aneurysm), **Buerger** (young male smoker) | PAN spares lung; Kawasaki IVIG |
| 🔴 Small | **ANCA trio:** GPA/PR3-cANCA, MPA/MPO-pANCA, Churg-Strauss + **IgA/HSP** immune-complex | Pauci-immune; HSP palpable purpura |
| Variable | Behçet, infectious (Aspergillus/Mucor need antifungal not steroid!) | Direct invasion |

> 🎯 **"Large = Giant-Taka; Medium = PAN-Kawasaki-Buerger; Small = ANCA trio + IgA."**

## A. Hypertension vessel lesion pair (Item-1 Q2 + Item-2 Q13 same)

| Feature | Benign HTN | Malignant HTN |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel | 🔴 Hyaline arteriolosclerosis (pink homogeneous) | 🔴 Hyperplastic onion-skin + fibrinoid necrosis |
| BP | <200/120 slow decades | >200/120 rapid + papilledema, renal failure 1 yr untreated |
| Heart | Concentric LVH >500 g wall >2 cm | Same + fibrinoid |

📌 Primary 90–95% (reset pressure natriuresis, salt/obesity/age) vs secondary 5–10%: **Kidney parenchymal (commonest) / renal artery stenosis / Conn / pheo / Cushing / coarctation / OCP-steroid-NSAID**.

## B. Aneurysm — true vs false vs dissection (Item-1 Q3 same)

- 🔴 Definition: >1.5× normal diameter permanent dilation.
- **True:** all 3 layers — atherosclerotic AAA, syphilitic ascending, berry, mycotic.
- **False/pseudo:** wall defect + pulsating hematoma (post-angiography leak, contained ventricular rupture).
- **Dissecting:** intimal tear → split media → blood channel in wall.
- Shape: saccular one-side (berry) vs fusiform full-circle (AAA).

### Berry aneurysm (Item-1 Q1 same)

🔴 Congenital medial defect at **Circle of Willis branching** (ACoA commonest, multiple 20–30%) → rupture → **SAH worst headache**. Association: ADPKD, HTN, smoking, EDS, coarctation.
> 🎯 **"Berry = Birth defect at Branching of Brain → Bleeds (SAH)."**

### Aortic dissection (Item-1 Q9 same)

🔴 Intimal tear usually **ascending within 10 cm of valve**, HTN >90%, Marfan/EDS/pregnancy young. Tearing chest pain back-radiation, BP differs arms. **Stanford A ascending = surgery; B distal to subclavian = medical.** Complication: tamponade, MI (coronary), stroke, renal/mesenteric infarct.

## C. Atherosclerosis sites bottom-up (Item-1 Q5 same)

**Lower abdominal aorta > coronary LAD > popliteal > carotid sinus > Circle of Willis.** Focal eccentric at ostia/branch points (turbulent flow); laminar flow protective via KLF2 (statin raises KLF2).

## D. Pulmonary vs portal vs systemic HTN correlation

| HTN | Cause → heart |
|---|---|
| Systemic → concentric LVH → IHD/SCD | Item-2 Q13 |
| Pulmonary (PE/recurrent, Eisenmenger L→R reversal) → RVH/cor pulmonale | Item-3 Q1–Q2; Item-4 Q2 |
| Portal (cirrhosis) → varices/hemorrhoids/caput, splenomegaly | Item-13 Q7/Q10 |

## 📌 Viva traps

1. Onion-skin kothay? → malignant HTN; hyaline? → benign + diabetes.
2. Berry vs AAA shape? → saccular vs fusiform.
3. Dissection MI mimic? → BP asymmetry + tearing back pain + widened mediastinum — anticoagulate korar age exclude.
4. Pulseless disease? → Takayasu; >50 temple pain? → giant cell.
5. Lumen narrow kore na kintu X-ray te dekha jay? → Mönckeberg pipestem (Item-1 Q2).

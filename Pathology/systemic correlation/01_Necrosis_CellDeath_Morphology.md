## A. Coagulative necrosis — MI timeline vs renal/papillary (comparison kore mone rakho)

### MI myocardium (Item-2 Q8/Q15 — same answer, ekhane regrouped)

| Time | Gross | Micro |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 min | None | EM only: glycogen loss, swelling |
| 2–4 hr | Dark mottling | 🔴 **Wavy fibers** |
| 4–12 hr | Dark mottling | 🔴 **Coagulative necrosis** — hypereosinophilic, pyknosis, loss of striation |
| 12–24 hr | Pale mottled | Necrosis + 🔴 neutrophil start |
| 1–3 d | Yellow-brown soft, hyperemic border | Neutrophil peak, karyolysis |
| 3–7 d | Yellow soft depressed | 🔴 Macrophage + granulation at border — **rupture danger window** |
| 7–14 d | Red-purple border grey centre | Granulation matures, collagen |
| 2–8 wk | 🔴 **Thin white fibrous scar** | Dense collagen, non-contractile |

📌 Heart **regenerate korte pare na** — healing only by scar (Item-2 Q8). Ejonnoi chronic IHD te diffuse white scar + flabby dilated heart (Item-2 Q9).

### Kidney papillary necrosis (Item-7 Q2 — same concept, onno organ)

- 📌 Diabetes + obstruction + sickle + analgesic → **coagulative necrosis of papilla → slough → obstruction + sterile pyuria + hydronephrosis**.
- 🔴 Correlation trap: MI te 3–7 din rupture-prone, kidney te sloughed papilla obstruction-prone — dutoi **coagulative necrosis er fate**.

> 🎯 **"MI 3–7 din = norom heart = rupture; papilla necrosis = khose pora papilla = block."**

## B. Caseous necrosis — TB sob jaygay same granuloma (Item-4 + Item-11 + Item-21 + Item-7 + Item-16 + Item-22)

🔴 **Structure sob jaygay same** (Item-4 Q14):

```text
Centre: amorphous granular eosinophilic CASEOUS debris + AFB at edge
→ palisaded EPITHELIOID cells (slipper nuclei)
→ LANGHANS giant cells (horseshoe nuclei ring)
→ CD4 T rim → fibroblast capsule
= Type-IV delayed hypersensitivity
```

| Site | Lesion name | Special point |
|---|---|---|
| Lung primary | 🔴 **Ghon focus (1–1.5 cm subpleural) + hilar nodes = Ghon complex → Ranke (healed calcified)** (Item-4 Q12) | Child, mid-zone, 95% heal |
| Lung secondary | Apical fibrocaseous + 🔴 **cavity draining to bronchus** + satellite foci (Item-4 Q13) | Adult, apex, AFB sputum+ |
| Miliary | 1–2 mm millet-seed granulomas lung+liver+spleen+kidney+marrow+meninges (Item-4 Q11) | Immunosuppression, anergy Mantoux negative |
| Intestine | Transverse TB ulcer + strictures (Item-11 Q8) | Crohn longitudinal vs TB transverse — viva trap |
| Meninges | Basal exudate, spider-web clot (Item-21 Q14–23) | TB CSF: lymphocyte, protein high, glucose low |
| Urinary | Sterile pyuria classic (Item-7 Q3) | Pus cells but culture negative |
| Tube | Caseating salpingitis → infertility (Item-16 Q7–10) | 1–2% PID, endemic |
| Spine | Pott disease + psoas abscess (Item-22 Q2) | Vertebral body |
| Pericardium | Caseous → calcific constrictive (Item-3 Q7) | Pericardiectomy |

> 🎯 **"Caseation dekhlei bolo TB — Ghon (lung), transverse (gut), spider-web (CSF), sterile pyuria (urine), Pott (spine)."**

## C. Fibrinoid — Aschoff vs malignant HTN vs vasculitis (Item-3 + Item-1)

- 🔴 **Aschoff body (Item-3 Q8):** fibrinoid centre + **Anitschkow caterpillar cells** + Aschoff giant cells + lymphocytes → perivascular interstitium → spindle scar 2–4 mo. **Pathognomonic of RF.**
- 🔴 **Malignant HTN (Item-1 Q2):** hyperplastic **onion-skin + fibrinoid necrosis**; benign HTN = hyaline arteriolosclerosis.
- 🔴 **Vasculitis (Item-1 Q9):** PAN/Kawasaki/Buerger medium + ANCA small-vessel fibrinoid.

> 🎯 **"Fibrinoid tin jaygay — Aschoff (heart), onion-skin (malignant HTN), vasculitis wall."**

## D. Fat necrosis — pancreas vs breast (Item-14 vs Item-18)

| Feature | Pancreatitis (Item-14 Q9) | Breast (Item-18 Q3) |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | Lipase saponification | Trauma/biopsy/surgery |
| Lesion | Chalky white foci, 🔴 hypocalcemia (Ca soaps) | Oil cysts + dystrophic calc |
| Trap | Amylase early, 🔴 lipase specific longer | Mammography te cancer mimic |

## 📌 Viva traps — "ekhane eta hole okhane ki?"

1. Coagulative necrosis heart e hole kidney te kothay hoy? → papillary necrosis / renal infarct.
2. Caseous necrosis lung chara ar kothay? → gut, meninges, urine (sterile pyuria), tube, spine, pericardium.
3. Fibrinoid Aschoff chara ar kothay? → malignant HTN onion-skin, vasculitis, Libman-Sacks.
4. Liquefactive brain chara ar kothay? → lung abscess, breast abscess, Brodie abscess, pyonephrosis.
5. White scar heart e 6–8 wk, ar kothay white scar? → chronic RHD fish-mouth fibrosis, cirrhosis bridging fibrosis, chronic pyelo scar.

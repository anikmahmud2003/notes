## C. GB pathogenesis pair (Item-14 Q2–Q3 condensed)

- Cholesterol: supersaturation (estrogen↑ secretion) + stasis (GB hypomotility) + nucleation.
- Black: unconjugated bilirubin↑ (hemolysis/cirrhosis) → Ca bilirubinate.
- Brown: infected stagnant bile → bacterial β-glucuronidase deconjugates → Ca soaps.
- Complications common: biliary colic (fatty RUQ + Murphy), acute cholecystitis, empyema, mucocele, CBD stone → cholangitis/Charcot, pancreatitis, porcelain GB → carcinoma.

## D. Renal stone pathogenesis (Item-8 Q7 same)

Supersaturation (low volume/high solute/abnormal pH) + low inhibitors (citrate/Mg/pyrophosphate) → nucleation → growth/aggregation + retention → colic UPJ/iliac/UVJ loin-to-groin + hematuria → hydronephrosis → infection/pyonephrosis → SCC chronic metaplasia.

Continuous culture:

It is a special type of culture, which supports the bacterial growth in a particular phase (e.g., log phase)
for longer period continuously without depletion of nutrients or without accumulation of toxic products.
Special devices are used which continuously supply the nutrients and remove the toxic products.
These device are-

1. Turbidostat: turbidity of the medium is constant.

2. Chemostat: Chemical composition of the medium is constant.

Why anaerobic bacteria cannot grow in presence of oxygen (O2)?
The obligate anaerobes, which cannot grow in the presence of oxygen because of -
a. Anaerobic bacteria do not have cytochrome systems for oxygen (O2) metabolism.
b. Anaerobic bacteria lack either superoxide dismutase or catalase, or both. That’s why oxygen
(O2) generated two toxic molecules: hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) and superoxide (O2°) are not
neutralized by anaerobic bacteria-
Superoxide dismutase which catalyzes the following reaction:

lethal effects of oxygen or toxic oxygen radicals).

X factor and V factor:

X factor (Heme) and V factor (NAD) are growth factors required by certain bacteria, particularly
members of genus Haemophilus.

Haemopilus species cannot synthesize their own X and or V factors and must obtain them from
the environment.

X factor and V factor are released from red blood cells'when they are lysed (in chocolate agar

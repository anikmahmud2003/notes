I. Bacterial classification according to flagellar arrangement:
There are four types of flagellar arrangement,» _

1. Monotrichous:
Single polar flagellum e.g. Vibrio cholerae and Campylobacter jejuni.

2. Amphitrichous:
Single flagellum at both ends e.g. Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

3. Lophotrichous:
Tuft of flagella at one or both ends e.g. Helicobacter pylori.
4. Peritrichous:
Flagella surrounding the bacterial cell.
Some enterobacteriaceae, e.g., Salmonella typhi, Escherichia coli, Proteus.

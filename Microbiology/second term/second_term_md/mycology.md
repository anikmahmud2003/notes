# Mycology

## M1: What is fungus? Mention the characteristics of fungus. Fungus vs. Bacteria. ********

> 📌 Hint:Eukaryotic, chitin cell wall, 80S ribosomes, exospore, etc.

### ✅ Answer

**Fungus:** Fungi are chlorophyll-free eukaryotic organisms that may be unicellular or multicellular and reproduce by sexual and asexual spores. The term comes from Greek *mykos* or *mykes* meaning fungus/mushroom. **Medical mycology** is the branch dealing with medically important fungi, and **mycosis** is any fungal infection.

**Characteristics of Fungus:**

- **Cell type:** Eukaryote with a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles (mitochondria, ER).
- **Cell number:** May be unicellular (yeast) or multicellular (mold).
- **Cell wall:** 90% polysaccharide, 10% protein. Contains **chitin** (polymer of N-acetyl glucosamine), **chitosan** (soluble derivative of chitin), and **β-glucan** (polymer of D-glucose).
- **Cytoplasmic membrane:** Contains sterols — ergosterol and zymosterol.
- **Ribosomes:** 80S (40S + 60S).
- **Chlorophyll:** Absent — they require an organic carbon source.
- **Reproduction:** By budding (asexual) or by sexual and asexual spores.
- **Oxygen requirement:** Mostly strict aerobes; some facultative anaerobes. No obligate anaerobes.
- **Natural habitat:** Mostly in the environment (soil, decaying organic matter).

| # | Point | Fungus | Bacteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cell type | Eukaryotes | Prokaryotes |
| 2 | Cell number | Unicellular or multicellular | Unicellular |
| 3 | Cell wall | Chitin, chitosan, β-glucan | Peptidoglycan |
| 4 | Cell membrane | Sterol present (ergosterol, zymosterol) | Sterol absent (except Mycoplasma) |
| 5 | Cytoplasm-bound organelles | Present | Absent |
| 6 | Ribosomes | 80S (40S + 60S) | 70S (30S + 50S) |
| 7 | Nucleus | True (eukaryotic) | Primitive / nucleoid (prokaryotic) |
| 8 | Spore | Exospore (reproduction) | Endospore (survival) |
| 9 | Reproduction | Budding or sexual & asexual spores | Binary fission |
| 10 | Treatment | Antifungal drug | Antibacterial drug |
| 11 | Organic carbon requirement | Required (no obligate anaerobes) | Not required by all |
| 12 | Thermal dimorphism | Yes (some) | No |

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Antifungal drugs-কে mode of action ধরে কীভাবে classification করা যায়?**
A: ৩ ভাবে করা যায় — (১) **cell wall**‑এ act করে: echinocandin β‑glucan synthesis inhibit করে; (২) **cell membrane**‑এ act করে: amphotericin B ও nystatin ergosterol‑এর সাথে bind করে, আর azole ও terbinafine ergosterol‑এর synthesis inhibit করে; (৩) **nucleic acid**‑এ act করে: flucytosine DNA synthesis inhibit করে ও griseofulvin mitotic spindle‑কে disrupt করে।

**Q: Amphotericin B আর azole—দুটোই ergosterol‑এর সাথে সম্পর্কিত, তাহলে action-এ পার্থক্যটা কী?**
A: পার্থক্য আছে। Amphotericin B সরাসরি ergosterol‑এর সাথে **bind** করে cell membrane‑এ pore তৈরি করে; কিন্তু azole ergosterol‑এর **synthesis**‑কে inhibit করে। অর্থাৎ একজন bind করে, আরেকজন তৈরিই হতে দেয় না।

**Q: Fungus-কে eradicate করা এত কঠিন কেন?**
A: দুই কারণে — প্রথমত, animal host‑এর কাছে fungal cell wall‑এর polysaccharide degrade করার মতো enzyme নেই; দ্বিতীয়ত, **selective toxicity** অর্জন কঠিন, কারণ fungal cell আর human cell দুটোই **eukaryote**।

**Q: Fungi-র কিছু উপকারী দিক বলুন?**
A: অনেক আছে — decomposition‑এর মাধ্যমে nutrient recycling করে; **mushroom, cheese, bread, wine, beer** তৈরি করে (food industry); **drug ও antibiotic** production‑এর biosynthetic factory; আর research‑এ model organism হিসেবে কাজ করে।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, মূল কথা হলো — **fungus** হলো chlorophyll‑মুক্ত, **eukaryotic** organism, যা unicellular বা multicellular হতে পারে এবং sexual ও asexual spore‑এর মাধ্যমে reproduce করে। Greek শব্দ *mykos/mykes* মানে fungus/mushroom। **Medical mycology** হলো সেই শাখা যা medically important fungi নিয়ে কাজ করে, আর **mycosis** হলো যেকোনো fungal infection।

**Characteristics** গুলো বলি — প্রথমত, cell type **eukaryote**, অর্থাৎ true nucleus, mitochondria, ER—মembranr ও membrane‑bound organelle আছে। দ্বিতীয়ত, cell number অনুযায়ী unicellular (yeast) বা multicellular (mold)। তৃতীয়ত, cell wall ৯০% polysaccharide ও ১০% protein — সেখানে থাকে **chitin** (N‑acetyl glucosamine‑এর polymer), **chitosan** (chitin‑এর soluble derivative) ও **β‑glucan** (D‑glucose‑এর polymer)।

Cytoplasmic membrane‑এ **ergosterol** ও **zymosterol** (sterol) থাকে — এটাই antifungal treatment‑এর target। Ribosome **80S (40S+60S)**। **Chlorophyll absent**, তাই organic carbon source দরকার। Reproduction হয় budding দিয়ে বা sexual ও asexual spore দিয়ে। Oxygen requirement — বেশিরভাগ **strict aerobe**, কিছু facultative anaerobe; কিন্তু কোনো obligate anaerobe নেই। Natural habitat — soil, decaying organic matter, অর্থাৎ বেশিরভাগ environment‑এ।

এবার **fungus vs bacteria** তুলনা করি — fungus **eukaryote**, bacteria **prokaryote**; fungus unicellular বা multicellular, bacteria শুধু unicellular; fungus‑এর cell wall **chitin, chitosan, β‑glucan**, bacteria‑র **peptidoglycan**; fungus‑এর membrane‑এ sterol (ergosterol) আছে, bacteria‑তে নেই (Mycoplasma ছাড়া); ribosome fungus‑এ **80S**, bacteria‑তে 70S; fungus‑এ true nucleus, bacteria‑তে primitive nucleoid; fungus‑এর spore **exospore (reproduction)**, bacteria‑র **endospore (survival)**; fungus budding বা spore দিয়ে, bacteria binary fission দিয়ে; treatment antifungal vs antibacterial; fungus‑এ organic carbon লাগবেই (obligate anaerobe নেই), bacteria‑র সবসময় লাগে না; আর কিছু fungus‑এ **thermal dimorphism** আছে, bacteria‑তে নেই।

## M2: Mention the composition of cell wall and cell membrane of fungi. ****

> 📌 Hint:Cell wall: chitin, D glucan; Cell membrane: ergosterol

### ✅ Answer

**Cell Wall of Fungi:**

- Composed of **90% polysaccharide** and **10% protein**.
- **Chitin:** A polysaccharide composed of long chains of **N-acetyl glucosamine (NAG)**. Chitin is insoluble in most solvents and has no free amino groups.
- **Chitosan:** A soluble derivative of chitin that has free amino groups.
- **β-D-Glucan:** A polymer of D-glucose. Target of echinocandin antifungals.

**Cell Membrane (Cytoplasmic Membrane) of Fungi:**

- Contains **ergosterol** and **zymosterol** (sterol components).
- Ergosterol is the key target of many antifungal drugs (amphotericin B, nystatin bind directly; azoles and terbinafine inhibit its synthesis).

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Ergosterol clinically এত গুরুত্বপূর্ণ কেন?**
A: কারণ human cell membrane‑এ ergosterol থাকে না—থাকে **cholesterol**। এই পার্থক্যের উপরেই selective toxicity নির্ভর করে — **amphotericin B** ergosterol‑এর সাথে bind করে pore তৈরি করে, আর **azole** ergosterol synthesis inhibit করে। তাই fungus‑এর জন্য toxic, human cell‑এ কম ক্ষতি।

**Q: কোন antifungal cell wall‑এর উপর কাজ করে?**
A: **Echinocandin** (caspofungin, micafungin) β‑glucan synthase inhibit করে। এটা বিশেষ, কারণ bacteria‑র cell wall‑এ তো **peptidoglycan** থাকে, β‑glucan নেই।

**Q: *P. jiroveci*‑র ক্ষেত্রে antifungal‑এ কী exception দেখা যায়?**
A: *P. jiroveci*‑র cell membrane‑এ ergosterol নেই—থাকে **cholesterol**। তাই echinocandin ও conventional antifungal এই organism‑এর বিরুদ্ধে কম কার্যকর।

**Q: Fungal cytoplasm‑এ কী কী structure থাকে?**
A: Cytoplasm‑এ **80S ribosome** (40S+60S) থাকে, আর membrane‑bound organelle হিসেবে **mitochondria** ও **endoplasmic reticulum** থাকে।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **cell wall** দিয়ে শুরু করি — এটা ৯০% polysaccharide ও ১০% protein দিয়ে তৈরি। Polysaccharide গুলো হলো **chitin**, **chitosan** ও **β‑D‑glucan**। Chitin হলো N‑acetyl glucosamine (NAG)‑এর long polymer, যা বেশিরভাগ solvent‑এ insoluble এবং free amino group থাকে না। Chitosan হলো chitin‑এর soluble derivative—এর free amino group থাকে। β‑D‑glucan হলো D‑glucose‑এর polymer, আর এটাই **echinocandin** antifungal‑এর target।

এবার **cell membrane (cytoplasmic membrane)** — এখানে থাকে **ergosterol** ও **zymosterol** (sterol component)। Ergosterol হলো অনেক antifungal drug‑এর key target — **amphotericin B ও nystatin** সরাসরি bind করে, আর **azole ও terbinafine** এর synthesis inhibit করে।

মনে রাখতে হবে — cell wall‑এ chitin ও β‑glucan, cell membrane‑এ ergosterol; এই দুটোই drug target। আর clinically গুরুত্বপূর্ণ হলো—human membrane‑এ cholesterol থাকে, ergosterol নয়, তাই amphotericin B ও azole selective toxicity দেখায়।

## M3: Difference between fungal and bacterial spore. **** (bacterial endospore for survival, fungal exospore for reproduction)

> 📌 Hint:bacterial endospore for survival, fungal exospore for reproduction

### ✅ Answer

| Point | Fungal Spore | Bacterial Spore |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Exospore | Endospore |
| Purpose | **Reproduction** (both sexual and asexual) | **Survival** under harsh conditions (heat, desiccation, radiation) |
| Formation | Formed externally from specialized structures (conidiophore, sporangium, or fragmentation of hyphae) | Formed intracellularly within a vegetative bacterial cell |
| Number | Many spores produced at once from a single parent | Typically one endospore per vegetative cell (one spore = one cell) |
| Structure | Simple: single- or few-celled; no specialized cortex | Complex: core, cortex, and thick protein coat (spore coat) |
| Resistance | Resistant to some environmental stresses but far less than bacterial endospores | Extremely resistant to heat, chemicals, UV radiation, desiccation |
| Germination | Germinates to form new fungal hyphae or yeast | Germinates back into a single vegetative cell |
| Examples | Zygospore, ascospore, basidiospore, conidia, sporangiospore, arthrospore, chlamydospore, blastospore | Bacillus, Clostridium endospores |

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Fungal sexual spore গুলো কী কী?**
A: তিনটি — **zygospore** (single, বড়, thick‑wall স্পোর), **ascospore** (ascus নামক থলির ভিতরে তৈরি হয়), আর **basidiospore** (basidium নামক pedestal‑এর উপরে তৈরি হয়)।

**Q: আর asexual spore (conidia) গুলো?**
A: Asexual conidia গুলো হলো — arthrospore, blastospore, chlamydospore, sporangiospore, microconidia ও macroconidia।

**Q: যে fungus sexual spore তৈরি করে না, তাকে কী বলে?**
A: তাকে বলে **fungi imperfecti** (Deuteromycotina)—অর্থাৎ imperfect fungus, কারণ এর sexual form পাওয়া যায় না।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, মূল পার্থক্যটা হলো — fungal spore হলো **exospore**, অর্থাৎ **reproduction**‑এর জন্য, sexual ও asexual দুটোই; আর bacterial spore হলো **endospore**, অর্থাৎ **survival**‑এর জন্য — harsh condition (heat, desiccation, radiation)‑এ বাঁচতে সাহায্য করে।

Formation ধরে বলি — fungal spore **বাইরে**, specialized structure (conidiophore, sporangium, বা hyphae fragmentation) থেকে তৈরি হয়; bacterial endospore **ভিতরে**, vegetative bacterial cell‑এর intracellular‑এ তৈরি হয়। Number অনুযায়ী — fungus‑এ একটি parent থেকে একসাথে অনেকগুলো spore তৈরি হয়; bacteria‑তে একটি vegetative cell থেকে সাধারণত একটি মাত্র endospore (one spore = one cell)।

Structurally fungal spore simple — single বা few cell, কোনো specialized cortex নেই; bacteria‑র endospore complex — core, cortex ও thick protein coat (spore coat) থাকে। Resistance — fungal spore কিছুটা resistant, কিন্তু bacterial endospore‑এর মতো নয়; endospore heat, chemicals, UV, desiccation‑এ **extremely resistant**। Germination — fungal spore থেকে নতুন fungal hyphae বা yeast হয়; bacterial endospore থেকে single vegetative cell।

Example — fungal: zygospore, ascospore, basidiospore, conidia, sporangiospore, arthrospore, chlamydospore, blastospore; bacteria: *Bacillus*, *Clostridium* endospore। মনে রাখার শর্টকাট — exospore = reproduction, endospore = survival; many spores vs one spore।

## M4: Classify fungus morphologically with example. *********

> 📌 Hint:Yeast, yeast-like, mold, dimorphic — with examples

### ✅ Answer

Fungi are morphologically classified into two main forms — **yeast** and **mold** — plus two sub-categories: **yeast-like** and **dimorphic**.

| Morphological Type | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **a. Yeast** | Unicellular, round or oval cells. Reproduce by **budding**. In the body, buds remain attached or detach as new cells. | *Cryptococcus neoformans* (pathogenic), *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* (non-pathogenic) |
| **b. Yeast-like (pseudohyphae)** | Unicellular yeast that forms **pseudohyphae** — elongated cells remaining attached in chains after budding. | *Candida* species (especially *C. albicans*) |
| **c. Mold** | Multicellular, elongated filaments called **hyphae**. Hyphae grow continuously by tip extension, forming a tangled mass called **mycelium**. Hyphae may be septate or non-septate (coenocytic). Molds grow at room temperature (25°C). | Dermatophytes, *Aspergillus*, *Rhizopus*, *Mucor*, *Penicillium*, *Rhinosporidium seeberi* |
| **d. Dimorphic** | Two forms: **mold at room temperature (25°C)** in environment; **yeast at body temperature (37°C)** in the host. | *Histoplasma capsulatum*, *Blastomyces dermatitidis*, *Coccidioides immitis*, *Paracoccidioides brasiliensis*, *Sporothrix schenckii*, *Penicillium marneffei*, *Malassezia furfur* |

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Septate আর non-septate hyphae—এই দুটো কোন ধরনের fungus‑এ দেখা যায়?**
A: **Septate hyphae** — cross wall থাকে, বেশিরভাগ **pathogenic**, dermatophytes ও systemic fungi‑তে দেখা যায়। **Non-septate (coenocytic)** — cross wall নেই, multinucleated, বেশিরভাগ **saprophytic**, যেমন *Mucor*, *Penicillium*।

**Q: Hyphae কীভাবে grow করে?**
A: Hyphae **tip‑এর extension** দিয়ে grow করে — filament‑এর খুঁএ cell division দিয়ে নয়।

**Q: Mycelium কী?**
A: Hyphae‑র visible tangled mass‑কে mycelium বলে — culture‑এ cotton ball‑এর মতো দেখা যায়।

**Q: Fungi imperfecti কাকে বলে?**
A: যে fungus sexual spore তৈরি করে না, তাকে **fungi imperfecti** বা Deuteromycotina বলে।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, morphological classification‑এ মূলত ৪টি group আছে — **(ক) yeast**, **(খ) yeast‑like (pseudohyphae)**, **(গ) mold** ও **(ঘ) dimorphic**।

প্রথম **yeast** — unicellular, round বা oval cell, **budding** দিয়ে reproduce করে। শরীরে bud attached থাকে বা নতুন cell হয়ে আলাদা হয়। Example — *Cryptococcus neoformans* (pathogenic), *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* (non‑pathogenic)। দ্বিতীয় **yeast‑like** — unicellular yeast যা **pseudohyphae** তৈরি করে, অর্থাৎ budding‑এর পর elongated cell chain আকারে attached থাকে। Example — *Candida*, বিশেষ করে *C. albicans*।

তৃতীয় **mold** — multicellular, elongated filament **hyphae**। Hyphae tip extension দিয়ে grow করে এবং tangled mass হয়ে **mycelium** তৈরি করে। Hyphae septate বা non‑septate (coenocytic) হতে পারে। Mold 25°C (room temperature)‑এ grow করে। Example — dermatophytes, *Aspergillus*, *Rhizopus*, *Mucor*, *Penicillium*, *Rhinosporidium seeberi*।

চতুর্থ **dimorphic** — দুটি form: environment‑এ 25°C‑এ **mold**, host‑এ 37°C‑এ **yeast**। Example — *Histoplasma capsulatum*, *Blastomyces dermatitidis*, *Coccidioides immitis*, *Paracoccidioides brasiliensis*, *Sporothrix schenckii*, *Penicillium marneffei*, *Malassezia furfur*।

মনে রাখবেন — yeast unicellular + budding, mold multicellular + hyphae/mycelium; dimorphic মানে temperature অনুযায়ী দুই রকম। আর septate hyphae সাধারণত pathogenic, non‑septate saprophytic।

**Structure** — yeast single cell, pseudohyphae তৈরি করতে পারে; mold hyphae দিয়ে **mycelium** (tangled mass) গঠন করে। **Colony appearance** — yeast smooth, creamy, wet‑looking (bacteria‑র মতো); mold cottony, powdery, velvety বা granular। **Growth temperature** — yeast 37°C (body temperature), mold 25°C (room temperature)। **Microscopy** — yeast‑এ budding yeast cell; mold‑এ branched septate বা non‑septate hyphae।

Example হিসেবে remember করুন — yeast: *Cryptococcus*, *Saccharomyces*; mold: *Aspergillus*, *Mucor*, *Rhizopus*, *Penicillium*, dermatophytes। আর yeast‑like *Candida* pseudohyphae তৈরি করতে পারে, আর dimorphic fungi 25°C‑এ mold, 37°C‑এ yeast হয়।

## M6: What do you mean by hyphae, pseudohyphae and mycelium? ***

> 📌 Hint:Hyphae = filaments; pseudohyphae = chained buds; mycelium = tangled mass of hyphae

### ✅ Answer

**Hyphae:** Long, branching filamentous structures formed by mold. Growth occurs by **extension of the tip** (not by cell division along the filament). Two types: **septate** (with cross walls) and **non-septate / coenocytic** (multinucleated, no cross walls).

- **Septate hyphae:** Usually pathogenic — dermatophytes, systemic fungi.
- **Non-septate hyphae:** Mostly saprophytic — *Mucor*, *Rhizopus*.

**Pseudohyphae:** Formed in some yeasts (especially *Candida*) when buds remain attached to the mother cell, forming a chain of elongated cells. They are NOT true hyphae because they lack the continuous cytoplasmic flow seen in true hyphae.

**Mycelium:** When hyphae grow continuously and form a **tangled mass or mat** of growth, it is called mycelium. This is visible to the naked eye and gives mold colonies their characteristic cottony/powdery appearance.

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Pseudohyphae *Candida albicans*‑এর জন্য কেন characteristic?**
A: কারণ *C. albicans* budding‑এর পর elongated cell‑এর chain আকারে pseudohyphae তৈরি করে — এটা এর একটি distinctive morphological feature।

**Q: Culture‑এ mycelium terminology কীভাবে ব্যবহৃত হয়?**
A: SDA plate‑এ যে visible **"fuzzy" বা cottony growth** দেখা যায়, সেটাই **aerial mycelium**।

**Q: Hyphae microscope‑এ কীভাবে পরীক্ষা করা হয়?**
A: **KOH preparation** বা **Lactophenol Cotton Blue** staining‑এর পরে পরীক্ষা করা হয়।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, তিনটি term পরিষ্কার করে বলছি।

**Hyphae** — mold‑এর তৈরি long, branching filamentous structure। Hyphae **tip‑এর extension** দিয়ে grow করে, cell division দিয়ে নয়। দুই প্রকার — **septate** (cross wall আছে) ও **non‑septate / coenocytic** (multinucleated, cross wall নেই)। Septate সাধারণত pathogenic — dermatophytes, systemic fungi; non‑septate বেশিরভাগ saprophytic — *Mucor*, *Rhizopus*।

**Pseudohyphae** — কিছু yeast‑এ (বিশেষত *Candida*) bud মাদার cell‑এ attached থাকলে elongated cell‑এর chain তৈরি হয় — এটাই pseudohyphae। এটা **true hyphae নয়**, কারণ true hyphae‑র মতো continuous cytoplasmic flow থাকে না।

**Mycelium** — hyphae যখন ক্রমাগত grow করে **tangled mass বা mat** তৈরি করে, তখন তাকে mycelium বলে। এটা naked eye‑এ visible, আর mold colony‑র cottony/powdery appearance‑এর কারণ।

Microscopically hyphae KOH preparation বা Lactophenol Cotton Blue staining দিয়ে দেখা হয়।

## M7: Classify fungus according to habitat. ***

> 📌 Hint:Superficial cutaneous, subcutaneous, systemic, opportunistic

### ✅ Answer

Clinical classification based on **site of infection / habitat**:

**A. Superficial Cutaneous Fungi** — affect skin, hair, nails; confined to body surfaces.

- *Malassezia furfur* — tinea versicolor
- Dermatophytes (*Epidermophyton*, *Trichophyton*, *Microsporum*) — dermatophytosis / tinea / ringworm
- *Trichosporon beigelii* — white piedra
- *Piedraia hortae* — black piedra
- *Hortaea werneckii* — tinea nigra

**B. Subcutaneous Fungi** — inoculated through skin by cuts, scratches, or thorns.

- *Rhinosporidium seeberi* — rhinosporidiosis
- *Madurella mycetomatis* — mycetoma / madura foot
- Dematiaceous fungi (*Fonsecaea*, *Phialophora*, *Exophiala*, *Cladosporium*) — chromoblastomycosis & pheohyphomycosis

**C. Systemic / Deep Fungi** — usually dimorphic; affect internal organs.

- *Blastomyces dermatitidis* — blastomycosis
- *Coccidioides immitis* — coccidioidomycosis
- *Paracoccidioides brasiliensis* — paracoccidioidomycosis

**D. Opportunistic Fungi** — cause disease in immunocompromised individuals.

- *Candida albicans* — candidiasis
- *Cryptococcus neoformans* — cryptococcal meningitis
- *Pneumocystis jiroveci* — pneumocystis pneumonia
- *Aspergillus fumigatus* — aspergillosis
- *Mucor* & *Rhizopus* — mucormycosis (black fungus)
- *Penicillium marneffei* — penicilliosis

**Q: কোন fungi নিঃসন্দেহে superficial, আর কোনগুলো cutaneous?**

### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

**A. Superficial Cutaneous Fungi** — skin, hair, nail‑কে প্রভাবিত করে, body surface‑এ সীমাবদ্ধ। Example — *Malassezia furfur* (tinea versicolor), dermatophytes (*Epidermophyton*, *Trichophyton*, *Microsporum*) — dermatophytosis/tinea/ringworm, *Trichosporon beigelii* (white piedra), *Piedraia hortae* (black piedra), *Hortaea werneckii* (tinea nigra), আর কখনো *Candida albicans* (superficial candidiasis)।

## M8: Define dimorphic fungus with example. *********

> 📌 Hint:Mold at 25°C, yeast at 37°C — remember the main dimorphic fungi

### ✅ Answer

**Dimorphic fungi** are fungi that exist in two forms depending on temperature: they grow as **molds (hyphae) at room temperature (25°C)** in the environment and as **yeast at body temperature (37°C)** in human tissues. This property is called **thermal dimorphism**.

The mnemonic: **"Mold at 25, Yeast at 37 — the lung fungi!"**

**Examples of dimorphic fungi:**

- *Histoplasma capsulatum* — histoplasmosis
- *Blastomyces dermatitidis* — blastomycosis
- *Coccidioides immitis* — coccidioidomycosis
- *Paracoccidioides brasiliensis* — paracoccidioidomycosis
- *Sporothrix schenckii* — sporotrichosis
- *Penicillium marneffei* — penicilliosis
- *Malassezia furfur* — tinea versicolor

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Dimorphic fungus‑এর pathogenesis কীভাবে?**
A: Environment থেকে mold spore **inhale** হয় → lung‑এ গিয়ে spore **yeast**‑এ differentiate করে → বেশিরভাগ infection asymptomatic ও self‑limited, কিন্তু disseminate করলে রোগ হতে পারে।

**Q: কোন fungi‑তে dimorphism বেশি দেখা যায়?**
A: সব **systemic/deep fungi** সাধারণত dimorphic হয়ে থাকে।

**Q: Mold থেকে yeast‑এ রূপান্তর কেন virulence factor?**
A: কারণ yeast form শরীরে **macrophage‑এর ভিতরেও survive** করতে পারে — এটাই আসল virulence factor।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **dimorphic fungi** হলো সেই fungus যারা temperature অনুযায়ী দুই রকম form‑এ থাকে — environment‑এ **25°C‑এ mold (hyphae)**, আর human tissue‑এ **37°C‑এ yeast**। এই বৈশিষ্ট্যকে বলে **thermal dimorphism**। Mnemonic — **"Mold at 25, Yeast at 37 — the lung fungi!"**

Example — *Histoplasma capsulatum* (histoplasmosis), *Blastomyces dermatitidis* (blastomycosis), *Coccidioides immitis* (coccidioidomycosis), *Paracoccidioides brasiliensis* (paracoccidioidomycosis), *Sporothrix schenckii* (sporotrichosis), *Penicillium marneffei* (penicilliosis), আর *Malassezia furfur* (tinea versicolor)।

Pathogenesis‑উলটা বললে — environment থেকে mold spore inhale হয়, lung‑এ গিয়ে yeast‑এ convert হয়। বেশিরভাগ infection asymptomatic, self‑limited; তবে disseminate করলে নানা disease হতে পারে। সব systemic/deep fungi সাধারণত dimorphic। আর mold→yeast এই transition‑টা একটা key **virulence factor**, কারণ yeast form macrophage‑এর ভিতরে survive করতে পারে।

## M9: What do you mean by dermatophytes? Mention the dermatophytes and their site of infection. *****

> 📌 Hint:Keratinophilic fungi; infect skin, hair, nails

### ✅ Answer

**Dermatophytes** are a group of fungi that infect only superficial keratinized tissues — the **skin** and skin appendages such as **hair and nails**. They are **keratinophilic**, meaning they thrive on keratin.

**Keratin types in different structures:**

- Skin — soft keratin
- Hair — hard and soft keratin
- Nail — hard keratin

| Genus | Species | Site of Infection |
|---|---|---|
| **Epidermophyton** | *E. floccosum* | Skin & Nail |
| **Trichophyton** | *T. rubrum* | Skin, Hair & Nail |
| *T. tonsurans* | Skin, Hair & Nail |  |
| *T. mentagrophytes* | Skin, Hair & Nail |  |
| *T. schoenleinii, T. verrucosum* | Skin, Hair & Nail |  |
| **Microsporum** | *M. canis* | Skin & Hair |
| *M. gypseum* | Skin & Hair |  |

**Key point:** *Epidermophyton* does NOT infect hair; *Microsporum* does NOT infect nails; *Trichophyton* infects all three.

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Dermatophytosis কীভাবে ছড়ায় (transmission)?**

**Q: Tinea কীভাবে body area অনুযায়ী নাম পায়?**
A: Tinea capitis (head hair), tinea corporis (trunk), tinea cruris (groin), tinea pedis (foot), tinea unguium (nail), tinea manuum (hand), tinea barbae (beard), tinea faciei (face)।

**Q: Microconidia‑র shape কী কী?**
A: *T. rubrum* — single, tear drop; *T. mentagrophytes* — cluster, oval; *T. tonsurans* — elongated।

**Q: আর macroconidia‑র shape?**
A: *Epidermophyton* — club shaped; *Trichophyton* — cylindrical; *Microsporum* — spindle shaped।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **dermatophytes** হলো সেই fungi যারা শুধু superficial keratinized tissue‑কে infect করে — **skin** আর তার appendage, যেমন **hair ও nail**। এরা **keratinophilic**, অর্থাৎ keratin‑এ বেঁচে থাকে।

Keratin type — skin‑এ **soft keratin**, hair‑এ **hard ও soft keratin**, nail‑এ **hard keratin**। তিনটি genus — **Epidermophyton**, **Trichophyton**, **Microsporum**। *E. floccosum* শুধু skin & nail‑এ infect করে। *Trichophyton*‑এর species গুলো (rubrum, tonsurans, mentagrophytes, schoenleinii, verrucosum) skin, hair ও nail — তিনটিতেই infect করে। *Microsporum* (canis, gypseum) শুধু skin & hair infect করে।

Key point — *Epidermophyton* **hair infect করে না**, *Microsporum* **nail infect করে না**, *Trichophyton* তিনটিই infect করে।

Transmission — direct contact: infected person (anthropophilic) বা infected animal (zoophilic — যেমন *Microsporum*), আর indirect — shared clothing, comb, towel। রোগের নাম dermatophytosis/tinea/ringworm। Site অনুযায়ী — tinea capitis (head), corporis (trunk), cruris (groin), pedis (foot), unguium (nail), manuum (hand), barbae (beard), faciei (face)। Identification — microconidia: *T. rubrum* single tear drop, *T. mentagrophytes* cluster oval, *T. tonsurans* elongated; macroconidia: *Epidermophyton* club, *Trichophyton* cylindrical, *Microsporum* spindle।

## M10: Write down the methods of laboratory diagnosis of fungal infections/dermatophytoses/ring worm infections. ***********

> 📌 Hint:KOH prep, culture on SDA, staining, PCR, Wood's lamp, hair perforation test

### ✅ Answer

**1. Specimen Collection:**

- Skin scraping (from margin of lesion and redness area)
- Nail clipping
- Hair plucking
- Swabs (oral, throat, vaginal) for Candida
- Blood (for serology in systemic mycosis)

**2. Microscopical Examination:**

- **KOH wet mount preparation** (most common): KOH 10–40% dissolves keratin → fungi visualized after 30 min. Findings: skin/nail — branching septate hyphae (± arthrospore); hair — ectothrix or endothrix.
- **Staining:** Calcofluor white (most common), Lactophenol cotton blue (for culture mount), Indian ink (for *Cryptococcus* capsule), Gram stain (only for *Candida* — shows gram-positive yeast), Methenamine silver, PAS, Giemsa, H&E.

**3. Culture / Isolation and Identification:**

- **SDA (Sabouraud's Dextrose Agar)** — most common media.
- Mold: incubate at **25°C for 2–4 weeks**. Yeast: at **37°C for 2–4 weeks**.
- Naked eye: colony color (white, brown, greenish-yellow), texture (powdery, cottony, velvety, granular).
- Microscopy of culture: stain with lactophenol cotton blue → macroconidia & microconidia morphology.
- Other media: SDA + chloramphenicol & cycloheximide, dermatophyte test agar, potato dextrose agar, corn meal agar, mycobiotic agar.

**4. Immunological Tests:** Antigen detection & antibody detection (mainly for systemic mycosis; not usually for dermatophytosis).

**5. Molecular Detection:** PCR.

**6. Other Tests:**

- **Wood's lamp:** *Microsporum* species show fluorescence under UV — used for tinea capitis.
- **Hair perforation test:** For *Microsporum* & *Trichophyton*.
- **Skin test:** For systemic fungi, dermatophytes, *Sporothrix*, and *Candida*.
- **Germ tube test:** Differentiates *C. albicans* from other *Candida* species.

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Superficial cutaneous fungus‑এ KOH কেন, আর অন্য body fluid‑এ কী ব্যবহার হয়?**
A: Superficial cutaneous fungus‑এ KOH keratin dissolve করে, তাই fungus স্পষ্ট দেখা যায়; অন্য body fluid‑এ (যেমন blood, CSF) **normal saline wet mount** ব্যবহার হয়।

**Q: Macroconidia দিয়ে কীভাবে identify করা হয়?**
A: *Epidermophyton* — club shaped; *Trichophyton* — cylindrical; *Microsporum* — spindle shaped।

**Q: আর microconidia দিয়ে?**
A: *T. rubrum* — single tear drop; *T. mentagrophytes* — cluster oval; *T. tonsurans* — elongated।

**Q: সব fungus‑এ Gram stain ব্যবহার করা যায় কি?**
A: না — শুধু *Candida*‑র জন্য Gram stain useful; অন্য fungus‑এ নয়।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, fungal infection‑এর lab diagnosis ধাপে ধাপে বলছি।

**১. Specimen collection:** skin scraping (lesion margin ও redness area থেকে), nail clipping, hair plucking; *Candida*‑র জন্য oral/throat/vaginal swab; systemic mycosis‑এ serology‑র জন্য blood।

**২. Microscopical examination:** সবচেয়ে common **KOH wet mount** — KOH 10–40% keratin dissolve করে, 30 min পরে fungus দেখা যায়। Finding — skin/nail‑এ branching septate hyphae (± arthrospore), hair‑এ ectothrix বা endothrix। Staining — **Calcofluor white** (most common), Lactophenol cotton blue (culture mount), Indian ink (*Cryptococcus* capsule), Gram stain (শুধু *Candida* — gram‑positive yeast), Methenamine silver, PAS, Giemsa, H&E।

**৩. Culture/isolation:** common media **SDA**। Mold 25°C‑এ 2–4 week, yeast 37°C‑এ 2–4 week। Naked eye‑এ colony color/texture দেখি, তারপর lactophenol cotton blue দিয়ে macro‑ ও microconidia morphology। Onno media — SDA + chloramphenicol & cycloheximide, dermatophyte test agar, potato dextrose agar, corn meal agar, mycobiotic agar।

**৪. Immunological test** — antigen ও antibody detection, মূলত systemic mycosis‑এ। **৫. Molecular** — PCR। **৬. Other tests** — **Wood's lamp**: *Microsporum* UV‑তে fluoresce করে (tinea capitis); **hair perforation test**: *Microsporum* & *Trichophyton*; **skin test**: systemic fungi, dermatophytes, *Sporothrix*, *Candida*; **germ tube test**: *C. albicans* আর অন্য *Candida* আলাদা করে।

## M11: S/N: Mycotoxin. ***

> 📌 Hint:Fungal toxins that cause mycotoxicosis

### ✅ Answer

**Mycotoxins** are toxins released by certain fungi as they grow. **Mycotoxicosis** is the condition caused by ingestion of these fungal toxins.

| Mycotoxin | Producing Fungus | Effect (Mycotoxicosis) |
|---|---|---|
| **Amanitin** | *Amanita* mushrooms | Liver damage |

A: Ergotism **vascular spasm** ও **neurological** — দুই ধরনের damage‑ই করতে পারে।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **mycotoxin** হলো সেই toxin যা কিছু fungus grow করার সময় release করে। **Mycotoxicosis** হলো সেই অবস্থা যা এই fungal toxin খাওয়ার ফলে হয়।

## M12: Name five fungal culture media. ***** (SDA, mycobiotic media, dermatophyte test media, corn meal media)

> 📌 Hint:SDA, mycobiotic media, dermatophyte test media, corn meal media

### ✅ Answer

**Five important fungal culture media:**

1. **Sabouraud's Dextrose Agar (SDA)** — most commonly used fungal culture media.
2. **Mycobiotic (Fungobiotic) Agar Media**
3. **Dermatophyte Test Agar (DTA) Media**
4. **Corn Meal Agar Media**
5. **Potato Dextrose Agar Media**

**SDA details:**

- High dextrose content — excellent carbon & energy source.
- Peptone — source of nitrogen, vitamins, and minerals.
- **Acidic pH (5.6)** — optimal for fungi, not bacteria.
- Antibiotics frequently added: penicillin (kills gram-positive bacteria), streptomycin (kills gram-negative bacteria), cycloheximide (inhibits saprophytic/non-pathogenic fungi).

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: আর কোন কোন fungal media আছে?**
A: SDA + chloramphenicol & cycloheximide, inhibitory mold agar, blood agar with antibiotic, brain heart infusion agar।

**Q: SDA‑তে bacteria কেন grow করে না?**

## M14: Why KOH is used in fungal wet mount preparation? ********* (to dissolve the keratin layer, easy visualization of fungus)

> 📌 Hint:To dissolve the keratin layer, easy visualization of fungus

### ✅ Answer

**KOH (Potassium Hydroxide)** is used in fungal wet mount preparation because:

- **Dissolves keratin:** KOH (10–40%) dissolves the keratin present in skin, hair, and nail specimens. This removes the obscuring host tissue debris that would otherwise hide the fungal elements.
- **Easy visualization of fungi:** After keratin dissolution, the refractile fungal hyphae, spores, and other structures become clearly visible under the microscope.
- **KOH does NOT dissolve chitin** — the fungal cell wall component — so fungi remain intact while the surrounding host tissue is cleared.

**Procedure:** Place specimen on slide → add KOH (10–40%) → wait 30 minutes → examine under microscope with or without staining.

**Note:** For specimens from non-skin sites (e.g. blood, CSF), normal saline wet mount is used instead of KOH.

**Q: KOH preparation‑এ কী finding দেখা যায়?**

**Q: Wait time কত, আর কী দিয়ে কমানো যায়?**
A: Typical wait time **30 minutes**; slide গরম করলে clearing দ্রুত হয়।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **KOH (Potassium Hydroxide)** fungal wet mount‑এ ব্যবহার হয় কারণ —

## M15: Why SDA media is used to diagnosed fungus? *** (acidic pH optimum for fungus not for bacteria, high sugar conc.: fungus is a sugar loving organism, added antibiotics: prevent bacterial growth)

> 📌 Hint:Acidic pH optimum for fungus not for bacteria, high sugar conc., added antibiotics

### ✅ Answer

**Sabouraud's Dextrose Agar (SDA)** is the most commonly used media for fungal diagnosis because of three key properties:

1. **Acidic pH (5.6):** Optimal for fungal growth. Most bacteria prefer neutral pH (~7.0) and cannot grow well at this acidic pH — so fungi are selectively encouraged while bacteria are inhibited.
2. **High dextrose (glucose) concentration:** Provides an excellent source of carbon and energy. Fungi are sugar-loving organisms (saccharophilic). The high sugar also raises osmotic pressure, which inhibits many bacteria.
3. **Added antibiotics:** Penicillin (inhibits gram-positive bacteria), streptomycin (inhibits gram-negative bacteria), and cycloheximide (inhibits saprophytic/non-pathogenic fungi) are frequently added to further ensure only pathogenic fungi grow.

**Additional components:** Peptone provides nitrogen, vitamins, and minerals.

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: SDA‑তে culture time কত?**
A: Mold 25°C‑এ 2–4 weeks; yeast 37°C‑এ 2–4 weeks।

**Q: অন্য fungal media গুলো কী কী?**
A: SDA + chloramphenicol & cycloheximide, mycobiotic agar, dermatophyte test agar, potato dextrose agar, corn meal agar।

**Q: Cycloheximide‑এর বিশেষ কাজ কী?**
A: Cycloheximide বিশেষভাবে **saprophytic (non‑pathogenic) fungi**‑কে inhibit করে, যাতে clinical specimen থেকে pathogenic fungus select হয়।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, SDA fungal diagnosis‑এর সবচেয়ে common media, আর এর কারণ তিনটি key property।

1. **Acidic pH (5.6):** fungal growth‑এর optimum। Most bacteria neutral pH (~7.0) পছন্দ করে, তাই এই acidic pH‑এ গজাতে পারে না — fungi selectively বাড়ে, bacteria inhibit হয়।
2. **High dextrose concentration:** carbon ও energy‑র excellent source। Fungi হলো **sugar‑loving (saccharophilic)** organism। High sugar osmotic pressure‑ও বাড়ায়, ফলে অনেক bacteria‑ও inhibit হয়।
3. **Antibiotic যোগ:** penicillin (gram‑positive bacteria inhibit), streptomycin (gram‑negative bacteria inhibit), cycloheximide (saprophytic/non‑pathogenic fungus inhibit) — যাতে শুধু pathogenic fungus‑ই grow করে।

আরেকটি component — peptone, যা nitrogen, vitamin ও mineral দেয়। Culture time — mold 25°C 2–4 week, yeast 37°C 2–4 week। আরও media আছে — SDA + chloramphenicol & cycloheximide, mycobiotic agar, DTA, PDA, corn meal agar। Cycloheximide‑র ভূমিকা — saprophytic fungus inhibit করে pathogenic fungus select করা।

## M24: S/N: Germ tube test. ** (used to differentiate Candida albicans to other candida spp)

> 📌 Hint:Used to differentiate Candida albicans from other Candida species

### ✅ Answer

**Germ Tube Test** is a specific test for *Candida albicans*. It distinguishes *C. albicans* (which produces germ tubes) from other *Candida* species (which do not).

**Procedure:**

1. Take a small amount of specimen from a colony of culture.
2. Mix with **0.5 ml of human or sheep serum**.
3. Incubate at **37°C for 2 hours**.
4. Examine under microscope after wet mount preparation.

**Result:** Tube-like projections or outgrowths of yeasts (germ tubes) are found → **positive** → confirms *C. albicans*.

**Significance:** This is a rapid, simple, and inexpensive test widely used in clinical microbiology labs. *C. albicans* is the only *Candida* species that reliably produces germ tubes in serum.

**Q: Candida species differentiate করার অন্য পদ্ধতি?**

**Fungal ball (Aspergilloma)** is a lung lesion caused by *Aspergillus fumigatus*. It is a mass of fungal hyphae, mucus, and cellular debris that colonizes a pre-existing lung cavity (e.g., from tuberculosis, sarcoidosis, or bronchiectasis).

**Key features:**

- Appears as a **radiopaque structure** on chest X-ray.
- **Changes position** when the patient is moved from erect to supine posture — this mobility is diagnostic.
- Usually non-invasive but can cause hemoptysis (coughing up blood).

**Classification of *Aspergillus* lung diseases:**

1. Allergic aspergillosis (asthma, ABPA)
2. **Fungal ball / Aspergilloma** (colonization of cavity)
3. Invasive aspergillosis (in immunocompromised)

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: *Aspergillus*‑এর morphology কী?**
A: Mold, septate hyphae, **V‑shaped (dichotomous)** branch।

**Q: Transmission কী?**
A: **Airborne conidia**।

**Q: Microscopy ও culture finding?**
A: Microscopy — septate hyphae, regular V‑shaped branch; culture — mold, **green conidia radiating chain**।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **fungal ball (aspergilloma)** হলো lung lesion, যা *Aspergillus fumigatus* ঘটায়। এটা fungal hyphae, mucus আর cellular debris‑এর mass, যা **আগে থেকে থাকা lung cavity**‑তে colonize করে (যেমন TB, sarcoidosis, bronchiectasis‑এর cavity)।

Key features — (১) chest X‑ray‑এ **radiopaque structure**; (২) patient erect থেকে **supine** হলে **position বদলায়** — এই mobility diagnostic; (৩) সাধারণত non‑invasive, কিন্তু **hemoptysis** (কাশিতে রক্ত) হতে পারে।

*Aspergillus* lung disease‑এর classification — (১) allergic aspergillosis (asthma, ABPA); (২) **fungal ball/aspergilloma** (cavity‑র colonization); (৩) invasive aspergillosis (immunocompromised‑এ)।

Morphology note — mold, septate hyphae, V‑shaped branching; transmission airborne conidia; culture green conidia radiating chain।

## M30: What do you mean by rose handler diseases? * (Sporothrix schenckii)

> 📌 Hint:Sporothrix schenckii

### ✅ Answer

**Rose handler disease (Rose gardener's disease / Sporotrichosis)** is a subcutaneous mycosis caused by *Sporothrix schenckii*.

- When *S. schenckii* is introduced into the skin (usually by a **rose thorn prick**), it causes a **local painless pustule or ulcer with nodules** along the draining lymphatics.
- It occurs most often in **gardeners**, especially those who prune roses — hence the name "rose handler disease."
- *Sporothrix schenckii* is a **dimorphic fungus** (mold at 25°C, yeast at 37°C).

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: *S. schenckii*‑এর classification কী?**
A: Subcutaneous fungus — minor cut, scratch বা thorn‑এর মাধ্যমে **skin inoculation** হয়ে acquired হয়।

**Q: Nodule কোন পথ ধরে spread করে?**
A: Nodule **lymphatic drainage**‑এর পথ ধরে — charakteristic **lymphocutaneous spread** pattern।

**Q: Diagnosis ও treatment?**
A: Diagnosis — **SDA**‑তে culture, dimorphism 25°C (mold) vs 37°C (yeast) দিয়ে confirm; treatment — antifungal **itraconazole, potassium iodide**।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **rose handler disease** (rose gardener's disease / sporotrichosis) হলো একটি **subcutaneous mycosis**, যা *Sporothrix schenckii* ঘটায়।

যখন *S. schenckii* skin‑এ প্রবেশ করে (সাধারণত **rose thorn prick** দিয়ে), তখন **local painless pustule বা ulcer** তৈরি হয়, আর draining lymphatic‑এর পথ ধরে **nodule** থাকে। এটি বেশি হয় **gardener**‑দের মধ্যে, বিশেষ করে যারা rose prune করে — তাই "rose handler disease" নাম।

*S. schenckii* হলো **dimorphic fungus** — 25°C‑এ mold, 37°C‑এ yeast।

Classification — subcutaneous fungus, skin inoculation (cut/scrat/thorn) দিয়ে। Spread pattern — lymphocutaneous। Diagnosis — SDA culture + dimorphism (25 vs 37°C)। Treatment — itraconazole, potassium iodide।

## M31: What is "id reaction"? ** (hypersensitivity reaction by circulating fungal antigen but not by fungus)

> 📌 Hint:Hypersensitivity reaction by circulating fungal antigen but not by fungus

### ✅ Answer

**"Id reaction" (Dermatophytid reaction)** is a **hypersensitivity reaction** that occurs in response to **circulating fungal antigens** — without the actual presence of fungus (hyphae) at the site of the reaction.

- It is a systemic immune response — the fungal antigens from the primary infection site travel via the blood and trigger an allergic reaction elsewhere on the skin.
- The "id" reaction sites do NOT contain fungal organisms on microscopy or culture.
- It is a **Type IV hypersensitivity** reaction.
- Commonly seen in dermatophytosis (ringworm) — e.g., vesicular eruptions on hands when feet are infected.

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Id reaction চেনা কেন important?**
A: কারণ এটা clinically **fungal infection‑এর মতো দেখায়**, কিন্তু আসলে immunological phenomenon — এটা বুঝলে secondary site‑এ ভুল diagnosis/unnecessary treatment এড়ানো যায়।

**Q: Treatment কীভাবে?**
A: Primary infection‑এর জন্য antifungal + id reaction‑এর জন্য **antihistamine বা steroid**।

**Q: Id reaction বুঝলে কী লাভ?**
A: Secondary site‑এর **misdiagnosis ও unnecessary treatment** prevent হয়।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **"id reaction" (dermatophytid reaction)** হলো একটি **hypersensitivity reaction** যা **circulating fungal antigen**‑এর প্রতিক্রিয়ায় হয় — reaction‑এর জায়গায় আসলে fungus (hyphae) থাকে না।

- এটা systemic immune response — primary infection site‑এর fungal antigen রক্তে ভ্রমণ করে অন্য skin site‑এ allergic reaction trigger করে।
- "id" reaction site‑এ **microscopy/culture‑তে fungal organism থাকে না**।
- এটা **Type IV hypersensitivity**।
- Common — dermatophytosis (ringworm)‑এ; যেমন foot infected থাকলে হাতে vesicular eruption।

Important — এটা clinically fungal infection‑এর মতো, কিন্তু আসলে immunological; treatment — primary infection antifungal + id reaction‑এ antihistamine/steroid; চিনলে misdiagnosis/unnecessary treatment prevent হয়।

## M32: What do you mean by dematiaceous fungi? * (having melanin or melanin like pigment. Exophiala, Cladosporium or Hortaea etc. but Mucor is not dematiaceous fungi)

> 📌 Hint:Having melanin or melanin-like pigment. Exophiala, Cladosporium or Hortaea etc. but Mucor is not dematiaceous

### ✅ Answer

**Dematiaceous fungi** are fungi that have **melanin or melanin-like pigment** in the wall of their hyphae and/or spores. Their conidia or hyphae appear **dark-colored (gray or black)**, which is why they are called "dematiaceous" (from Latin *dematiaceous* = dark-pigmented).

**Important dematiaceous fungi:**

1. *Fonsecaea* — *F. pedrosoi*, *F. compacta*
2. *Phialophora* — *P. verrucosa*
3. *Cladophialophora* — *C. carrionii*, *C. bantiana*
4. *Exophiala* — *E. dermatitidis*, *E. jeanselmei*, *E. spinifera*, *E. moniliae*
5. *Cladosporium* / *Hortaea* — *C. werneckii* (= *Hortaea werneckii*)

**Important: *Mucor* is NOT a dematiaceous fungus** — it does not contain melanin pigment.

**Diseases caused by dematiaceous fungi:**

- **Chromoblastomycosis:** Chronic granulomatous infection of cutaneous and subcutaneous tissues.
- **Pheohyphomycosis:** Primary or opportunistic infection ranging from superficial tissue to deep organs.

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Tinea nigra কোন fungus‑এর কারণে, আর কী দেখায়?**
A: *Hortaea werneckii* / *Cladosporium werneckii*‑এর কারণে; melanin‑like pigment‑এর জন্য skin‑এ **brownish spot** দেখা যায়।

**Q: *Phialophora verrucosa* কোন disease করে?**
A: **Chromoblastomycosis**।

**Q: *Exophiala dermatitidis* কোন disease করে?**
A: **Pheohyphomycosis**।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, **dematiaceous fungi** হলো সেইসব fungus যাদের hyphae/spore‑এর wall‑এ **melanin বা melanin‑like pigment** থাকে। তাই এরা **dark‑colored (gray বা black)** দেখায় — "dematiaceous" শব্দটি Latin থেকে এসেছে, মানে dark‑pigmented।

Important dematiaceous fungi — *Fonsecaea* (pedrosoi, compacta), *Phialophora* (verrucosa), *Cladophialophora* (carrionii, bantiana), *Exophiala* (dermatitidis, jeanselmei, spinifera, moniliae), *Cladosporium/Hortaea* (werneckii)।

**Important:** *Mucor* dematiaceous **নয়** — এতে melanin pigment থাকে না।

Disease — **chromoblastomycosis** (cutaneous/subcutaneous‑এর chronic granulomatous infection) ও **pheohyphomycosis** (superficial tissue থেকে deep organ পর্যন্ত, primary বা opportunistic)। Extra — tinea nigra *H. werneckii*‑এর কারণে brownish spot; *P. verrucosa* → chromoblastomycosis; *E. dermatitidis* → pheohyphomycosis।

## M33: Mention the name of sexual and asexual spore.* (sexual spore: zygospore, ascospore, basidiospore, asexual spore: arthrospore, blastospore, chlamydospore, sporangiospore, microconidia and macroconidia)

> 📌 Hint:Sexual: zygospore, ascospore, basidiospore; Asexual: arthrospore, blastospore, chlamydospore, sporangiospore, microconidia, macroconidia

### ✅ Answer

**A. Sexual Spores:**

1. **Zygospore:** Single large spore with thick walls.
2. **Ascospore:** Formed inside a sac called an **ascus**.
3. **Basidiospore:** Formed externally on the tip of a pedestal called a **basidium**.

**B. Asexual Spores (Conidia):**

1. **Arthrospore:** Arises by fragmentation of the ends of hyphae. Example: *Coccidioides immitis*.
2. **Blastospore (Blastoconidia):** Formed by budding. Multiple buds that do not detach form pseudohyphae. Example: *Candida albicans*.
3. **Chlamydospore:** Rounded, thick-walled, quite resistant. Example: *Candida albicans*.
4. **Sporangiospore:** Formed within a sac (sporangium) on a stalk by molds. Example: *Rhizopus* and *Mucor*.
5. **Microconidia:** Small conidia. Example: *Aspergillus*, *Microsporum*.
6. **Macroconidia:** Large conidia. Example: *Microsporum*.

**Note:** Fungi that do NOT form sexual spores are termed **fungi imperfecti** (Deuteromycotina).

### ❓ Cross Questions (Bangla)

**Q: Macroconidia‑র shape genus অনুযায়ী কী কী?**
A: *Epidermophyton* — club shaped; *Trichophyton* — cylindrical; *Microsporum* — spindle shaped।

**Q: আর microconidia species অনুযায়ী?**
A: *T. rubrum* — single tear drop; *T. mentagrophytes* — cluster oval; *T. tonsurans* — elongated।

**Q: Sporangiospore আর conidia‑র পার্থক্য কী?**
A: Sporangiospore **sporangium‑এর ভিতরে enclosed**; conidia **naked** (enclosed নয়)।


### 🗣️ Viva Explanation (Bangla)

স্যার, sexual ও asexual spore‑এর নাম বলছি।

**A. Sexual spore (৩টি):** **Zygospore** — single, বড়, thick‑walled spore; **Ascospore** — **ascus** নামক থলির ভিতরে তৈরি হয়; **Basidiospore** — **basidium** নামক pedestal‑এর tip‑এ externally তৈরি হয়।

**B. Asexual spore (conidia) (৬টি):** **Arthrospore** — hyphae‑এর প্রান্ত fragmentation‑এর ফলে; example *Coccidioides immitis*। **Blastospore (blastoconidia)** — budding‑এর ফলে; একাধিক bud detach না হলে pseudohyphae; example *Candida albicans*। **Chlamydospore** — rounded, thick‑walled, বেশ resistant; example *Candida albicans*। **Sporangiospore** — stalk‑এর উপর **sporangium** নামক থলির ভিতরে; example *Rhizopus*, *Mucor*। **Microconidia** — ছোট conidia; example *Aspergillus*, *Microsporum*। **Macroconidia** — বড় conidia; example *Microsporum*।

Note — যেসব fungus sexual spore তৈরি করে না, তাদের বলে **fungi imperfecti** (Deuteromycotina)। Shape মনে রাখুন — macroconidia: Epidermophyton club, Trichophyton cylindrical, Microsporum spindle; microconidia: rubrum single tear drop, mentagrophytes cluster oval, tonsurans elongated; sporangiospore enclosed, conidia naked।

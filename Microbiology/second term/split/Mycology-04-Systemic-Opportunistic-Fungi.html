M16

Name four (04) systemic mycotic agents. **** (remember the main dimorphic fungus)

Four systemic (deep) mycotic agents — all are dimorphic fungi:

Systemic FungusDiseaseMicroscopyTransmission
Histoplasma capsulatumHistoplasmosisYeast within macrophagesInhalation
Blastomyces dermatitidisBlastomycosisYeast with single broad-based budInhalation of microconidia
Coccidioides immitisCoccidioidomycosisSpherule filled with endosporesInhalation of arthrospores

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Systemic mycosis‑এর pathogenesis কীভাবে?

Spore inhalation → lung‑এ yeast differentiation → বেশিরভাগ asymptomatic ও self‑limited; disseminate করলে tissue destruction এমনকি death‑ও হতে পারে।

H. capsulatum macrophage‑এর ভিতর কীভাবে বাঁচে?

Phagolysosome‑এর ভিতরে bicarbonate ও ammonia দিয়ে pH বাড়িয়ে survive করে।

B. dermatitidis আর C. neoformans‑এর bud দিয়ে পার্থক্য কী?

B. dermatitidis‑এর broad‑based bud, আর C. neoformans‑এর narrow‑based bud — এটাই key differentiator।

C. immitis‑এর tissue ও environment form কী?

Lung tissue‑এ spherule with endospores; environment‑এ arthrospore।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, systemic (deep) mycotic agent চারটি, আর সবগুলোই dimorphic fungi।

  1. Histoplasma capsulatum — histoplasmosis; microscopy — yeast within macrophage; transmission — inhalation।
  2. Blastomyces dermatitidis — blastomycosis; single broad‑based bud –যুক্ত yeast; transmission — inhalation of microconidia।
  3. Coccidioides immitis — coccidioidomycosis; spherule filled with endospores; transmission — inhalation of arthrospores।
  4. Paracoccidioides brasiliensis — paracoccidioidomycosis; multiple buds ("ship captain's wheel"); transmission — inhalation।

Pathogenesis — spore inhalation → lung‑এ yeast differentiation → বেশিরভাগ asymptomatic/self‑limited; disseminate করলে tissue destruction বা death। বিশেষ কথা — H. capsulatum phagolysosome‑এর ভিতরে bicarbonate ও ammonia দিয়ে pH বাড়িয়ে বাঁচে; bud morphologically B. dermatitidis broad‑based, C. neoformans narrow‑based; C. immitis tissue‑এ spherule, environment‑এ arthrospore।

M17

Name five (05) opportunistic mycotic agents. *****

📌 Hint: Candida, Cryptococcus, Aspergillus, Pneumocystis, Mucor/Rhizopus
✅ Answer

Opportunistic fungi normally do not cause disease in healthy individuals but cause infection when the immune system is weakened or impaired.

Five important opportunistic mycotic agents:

Opportunistic FungusDiseaseMorphology
Candida albicansCandidiasisYeast with pseudohyphae
Cryptococcus neoformansCryptococcal meningitis / cryptococcosisYeast with large capsule
Aspergillus fumigatusAspergillosisMold with septate hyphae, V-shaped branches
Mucor & RhizopusMucormycosis (black fungus)Mold with non-septate hyphae
Pneumocystis jiroveciPneumocystis pneumoniaCysts (difficult to culture)

Penicillium marneffei is also an important opportunistic agent (penicilliosis).

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Most common opportunistic fungus কোনটি?

C. albicans — সবচেয়ে common opportunistic fungus, আর nosocomial fungal infection‑এর সবচেয়ে common কারণও।

C. neoformans‑এর বিশেষত্ব কী?

এটাই একমাত্র capsulated fungus; transmission — inhalation; source — pigeon droppings।

Aspergillus‑এর morphology?

Septate hyphae, dichotomous (V‑shaped) branching; green conidia radiating chain‑এ।

Mucor/Rhizopus‑এর morphology?

Non‑septate hyphae, right‑angle branching; spore sporangium‑এর ভিতরে।

P. jiroveci‑র culture ও diagnosis note কী?

Culture difficult; methenamine silver বা Giemsa stain দিয়ে diagnosis; ELISA শুধু epidemiological উদ্দেশ্যে।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, opportunistic fungi হলো সেইসব fungus যারা healthy মানুষে disease করে না, কিন্তু immune system weakened/impaired হলে infection করে।

পাঁচটি important agent — Candida albicans (candidiasis; yeast with pseudohyphae), Cryptococcus neoformans (cryptococcal meningitis/cryptococcosis; yeast with large capsule), Aspergillus fumigatus (aspergillosis; mold, septate hyphae, V‑shaped branch), Mucor & Rhizopus (mucormycosis/black fungus; mold, non‑septate hyphae), Pneumocystis jiroveci (pneumocystis pneumonia; cysts, culture difficult)। Penicillium marneffei‑ও গুরুত্বপূর্ণ opportunistic agent (penicilliosis)।

High‑yield দিক — C. albicans সবচেয়ে common opportunistic fungus ও nosocomial fungal infection‑এর কারণ; C. neoformans একমাত্র capsulated fungus, source pigeon droppings, transmission inhalation; Aspergillus‑এর dichotomous V‑shaped branching ও green conidia; Mucor/Rhizopus‑এর non‑septate hyphae ও right‑angle branching; P. jiroveci diagnosis methenamine silver/Giemsa, ELISA শুধু epidemiological উদ্দেশ্যে।

M18

Enumerate the fungi that can be present as normal flora. ** (Candida spp)

📌 Hint: Candida spp
✅ Answer

Candida species (especially C. albicans) are the only fungi found as normal flora in the human body. They colonize:

  • Gastrointestinal tract (GIT)
  • Upper Respiratory Tract
  • Female genital tract

Candida albicans is the most common opportunistic fungus. As a normal commensal, it causes disease when host defenses are weakened — making it an opportunistic pathogen.

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

C. albicans‑কে কেন "white fungus" বলা হয়?

কারণ oral thrush‑এ white patches (pseudomembrane) দেখা যায়।

Infant‑এ transmission কীভাবে হয়?

Vertical transmission — birth‑এর সময় heavily colonized birth canal দিয়ে।

অন্য কোন Candida species আছে?

C. tropicalis, C. parapsilosis, C. glabrata, C. krusei, C. dubliniensis।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, মানবদেহে Candida species (বিশেষ করে C. albicans)‑ই একমাত্র fungus যা normal flora হিসেবে থাকে। এরা colonize করে — GIT, উপরের respiratory tract আর female genital tract‑এ।

Candida albicans হলো সবচেয়ে common opportunistic fungus। Normal commensal হিসেবে host defense দুর্বল হলে disease করে — তাই এটা opportunistic pathogen।

"White fungus" বলা হয় oral thrush‑এর white patches (pseudomembrane)‑এর কারণে। Infant‑এ transmission vertical — birth canal দিয়ে। আরও species আছে — C. tropicalis, parapsilosis, glabrata, krusei, dubliniensis।

M19

Enumerate the fungi that can take gram stain. ** (Candida take gram positive stain)

📌 Hint: Candida takes gram positive stain
✅ Answer

Candida species (especially C. albicans) are the only fungi that take up Gram stain. They stain Gram-positive, appearing as gram-positive oval budding yeast cells and pseudohyphae.

Gram stain is generally NOT useful for other fungi — dermatophytes, Aspergillus, Mucor, Cryptococcus, etc. do not take Gram stain reliably.

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Gram stain‑এ gram‑positive yeast with pseudohyphae দেখলে এর তাৎপর্য কী?

এটা candidiasis‑এর জন্য suggestive — অর্থাৎ Candida infection থাকতে পারে।

অন্য fungus‑এর জন্য কোন stain ব্যবহার হয়?

Specialized stain — calcofluor white, Lactophenol cotton blue, বা Indian ink।

Cryptococcus‑এর diagnosis কোন stain দিয়ে?

Indian ink (negative stain) দিয়ে — Gram stain নয়।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, শুধু মাত্র Candida species (বিশেষ করে C. albicans)‑ই Gram stain গ্রহণ করে, আর এরা Gram‑positive stain হয় — gram‑positive oval budding yeast cell ও pseudohyphae হিসেবে দেখা যায়।

অন্য fungus — dermatophytes, Aspergillus, Mucor, Cryptococcus ইত্যাদি — Gram stain নির্ভরযোগ্যভাবে গ্রহণ করে না। তাই Gram stain‑এ gram‑positive yeast with pseudohyphae দেখলে candidiasis suggestive। অন্য fungus‑এর জন্য specialized stain — calcofluor white, LPCB, বা Indian ink (Cryptococcus‑এর জন্য negative stain)।

Candidiasis & Candida

Properties of Candida, lesions of C. albicans, predisposing factors, diagnosis, germ tube test
M20

Mention some important properties of Candida. ***

📌 Hint: White fungus, yeast-like, normal flora, gram-positive, germ tube positive
✅ Answer

Important properties of Candida albicans:

  1. Also known as "white fungus" (due to white patches in oral thrush).
  2. Yeast-like fungus — oval yeast with a single bud. In tissues, appears as yeasts or pseudohyphae. True hyphae also form when invading tissues.
  3. Only fungus found as normal flora in the human body (GIT, upper respiratory tract, female genital tract).
  4. Only fungus that takes Gram stain — shows Gram positivity.
  5. Germ tube positive — forms germ tubes in serum at 37°C (specific for C. albicans).
  6. Can be isolated on blood agar media.
  7. Most common opportunistic fungus.
  8. Morphology: budding yeast with pseudohyphae in tissue; chlamydospores and pseudohyphae in culture at 20–25°C.

M21

List the lesions produced by C. albicans. *****

📌 Hint: Mucosal, cutaneous, chronic mucocutaneous, systemic
✅ Answer

C. albicans causes candidiasis (white fungus) with the following lesions:

A. Mucosal & Cutaneous Candidiasis:

  • Oral thrush — white patches (pseudomembrane) on tongue, mouth, or throat.
  • Vulvovaginal candidiasis (moniliasis) — vaginal discharge, itching.
  • Esophageal candidiasis
  • Onychomycosis — nail infection.
  • Diaper rash — in infants' diaper area.

B. Chronic Mucocutaneous Candidiasis:

  • Rare condition in early childhood associated with reduced cell-mediated immunity (CMI).

C. Systemic Candidiasis:

  • Candidemia (Candida in blood)
  • Meningitis
  • Endocarditis
  • Endophthalmitis
  • Subcutaneous nodules

M22

State the predisposing factors for candidiasis. ****

📌 Hint: Physiological, immunodeficiency, drugs, trauma, endocrine, others
✅ Answer

Predisposing factors for candidiasis:

  1. Physiological: Infancy, pregnancy, old age.
  2. Immunodeficiency diseases: AIDS, malignancy (leukemia, lymphoma), chronic granulomatous disease, aplastic anemia.
  3. Drugs (Iatrogenic): Antibiotics (disrupt normal flora), steroids, oral contraceptive pills, anti-cancer drugs.
  4. Traumatic: Burns, infection.
  5. Endocrine: Diabetes mellitus.
  6. Others: Catheterization, IV drug abuse, conditions causing neutropenia.

M23

Write down the laboratory diagnosis of candidiasis. *****

📌 Hint: Specimen, microscopy (wet mount, Gram stain), culture (SDA, blood agar), germ tube test
✅ Answer

Laboratory Diagnosis of Candidiasis:

1. Specimen Collection: Swabs from site of infection — oral swab, throat swab, vaginal swab.

2. Microscopical Examination:

  • Wet mount preparation: Finding — budding yeast with pseudohyphae.
  • Gram stain: Gram-positive budding yeast cells.

3. Culture / Isolation and Identification:

  • Smooth, white, creamy colonies.
  • Media: SDA (Sabouraud's Dextrose Agar) and Blood Agar (containing antibiotic).

4. Immunological Tests: Usually not done.

5. Molecular Detection: PCR.

6. Other Tests:

  • Germ tube test: To differentiate C. albicans from other Candida species.
  • Carbohydrate fermentation test: To differentiate various Candida species.

C. albicans germ tube তৈরি করে, অন্য Candida কেন না?

C. albicans serum‑এ 37°C‑এ germ tube তৈরি করে; most other pathogenic Candida করে না — এটাই পার্থক্যের ভিত্তি।

Colony থেকে sample → 0.5 ml human/sheep serum‑এ mix → 37°C‑এ 2 hours incubate → wet mount → tube‑like projection (germ tube) দেখা যায়।

SDA ছাড়া অন্য কোন media‑তে Candida isolate হয়?

Blood agar‑এও isolate করা যায়।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, candidiasis‑এর lab diagnosis বলছি।

১. Specimen: infection site থেকে swab — oral, throat, vaginal।

২. Microscopy: wet mount‑এ budding yeast with pseudohyphae; Gram stain‑এ gram‑positive budding yeast।

৩. Culture: smooth, white, creamy colony; media SDA ও Blood Agar (antibiotic সহ)।

৪. Immunological: সাধারণত করা হয় না। ৫. Molecular: PCR।

৬. Other tests: germ tube test — C. albicans‑কে অন্য species থেকে আলাদা করে; carbohydrate fermentation — species differentiate করে।

Extra detail — germ tube test: colony + 0.5 ml serum → 37°C 2 hr → wet mount → tube‑like projection; শুধু C. albicans করে, অন্যরা করে না। Blood agar‑এও isolate করা যায়।

M24

S/N: Germ tube test. ** (used to differentiate Candida albicans to other candida spp)

📌 Hint: Used to differentiate Candida albicans from other Candida species
✅ Answer

Germ Tube Test is a specific test for Candida albicans. It distinguishes C. albicans (which produces germ tubes) from other Candida species (which do not).

Procedure:

  1. Take a small amount of specimen from a colony of culture.
  2. Mix with 0.5 ml of human or sheep serum.
  3. Incubate at 37°C for 2 hours.
  4. Examine under microscope after wet mount preparation.

Result: Tube-like projections or outgrowths of yeasts (germ tubes) are found → positive → confirms C. albicans.

Significance: This is a rapid, simple, and inexpensive test widely used in clinical microbiology labs. C. albicans is the only Candida species that reliably produces germ tubes in serum.

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Pseudohyphae আর germ tube কি আলাদা জিনিস?

হ্যাঁ — pseudohyphae হলো budding‑এর পর attached থাকা elongated cell‑এর chain (germ tube থেকে আলাদা); germ tube হলো true tube‑like projection।

Chlamydospore কী, আর C. albicans‑এ কখন হয়?

Chlamydospore হলো rounded, thick‑walled, resistant spore — C. albicans‑এ culture 20–25°C‑এ তৈরি হয়।

Candida species differentiate করার অন্য পদ্ধতি?

Carbohydrate fermentation test।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, germ tube test হলো Candida albicans‑এর জন্য specific test — এটা C. albicans (যে germ tube produce করে)‑কে অন্য Candida species (যারা produce করে না) থেকে আলাদা করে।

Procedure — (১) culture colony থেকে সামান্য specimen; (২) 0.5 ml human বা sheep serum‑এ mix; (৩) 37°C‑এ 2 ঘণ্টা incubate; (৪) wet mount preparation করার পর microscope‑এ examine। Result — germ tube (tube‑like projection) দেখা গেলে → positive → C. albicans confirm।

Significance — rapid, simple, inexpensive test, clinical lab‑এ বহুল ব্যবহৃত। C. albicans‑ই একমাত্র Candida যা serum‑এ নির্ভরযোগ্যভাবে germ tube produce করে।

Clarification — pseudohyphae (Candida‑তেও হয়, elongated cell‑এর chain) germ tube নয়; chlamydospore C. albicans culture 20–25°C‑এ rounded thick‑walled resistant spore; carbohydrate fermentation অন্য differentiate পদ্ধতি।

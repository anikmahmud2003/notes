Cryptococcus, Pneumocystis & Aspergillus

C. neoformans, Pneumocystis jiroveci, aspergillosis, black/white fungus, fungal ball, rose handler disease
M25

Mention the important properties, diseases & laboratory diagnosis of C. neoformans. **** (properties: only capsulated fungus, morphology yeast, causes meningitis)

📌 Hint: Only capsulated fungus, morphology yeast, causes meningitis
✅ Answer

Properties of Cryptococcus neoformans:

  • Only capsulated fungus — unique among all pathogenic fungi.
  • Morphology: Yeast — round or oval budding yeast with a narrow-based bud.
  • Opportunistic fungus.
  • Transmission: inhalation.
  • Source: Bird (especially pigeon) droppings.

Diseases:

  • Cryptococcal meningitis — especially in AIDS / immunocompromised patients.
  • Cryptococcal pneumonia.
  • Cryptococcosis (general term).

Laboratory Diagnosis:

  1. Specimen: CSF, sputum.
  2. Indian ink preparation (negative stain): round/oval yeast with narrow-based bud surrounded by a wide unstained capsule (clear halo against dark background).
  3. Culture on SDA at 37°C for 2–3 days: cream-colored mucoid colonies.
  4. CRAG test (Cryptococcal antigen): capsular antigen detection by latex agglutination test.
  5. PCR — molecular detection.

Capsule‑এর কাজ কী (virulence)?

Capsule polysaccharide দিয়ে তৈরি, এটাই main virulence factor — এটি phagocytosis inhibit করে।

কোন কোন fungus meningitis করতে পারে?

Highly sensitive ও specific — diagnosis ও treatment response monitoring দুটোতেই ব্যবহৃত হয়।

M26

Write down the peculiar properties of Pneumocystis jiroveci/ Explain- "what type of organism is Pneumocystis jiroveci"? *** (at morphology: like parasites and at molecular level: like fungus)

📌 Hint: At morphology: like parasites; at molecular level: like fungus
✅ Answer

Pneumocystis jiroveci is a unique organism that has features of both fungi and parasites. It is now classified as a fungus based on molecular evidence.

Peculiar Properties:

  • Human species: Pneumocystis jiroveci; Rat species: Pneumocystis carinii.
  • Transmission: Inhalation of airborne organism.
  • Disease: Atypical pneumonia (plasma cell pneumonia) — ground glass opacity on imaging.
  • Mortality: Approximately 100% if untreated.

Why it looks like a parasite:

  • Cell membrane contains cholesterol (not ergosterol — unlike typical fungi).
  • In tissue, appears as cysts (resembles protozoal cysts).
  • Does not grow on standard fungal culture media.
  • Antifungal drugs are largely ineffective.
  • Major surface glycoprotein shows antigenic variation similar to Trypanosoma brucei.

Why it is classified as a fungus:

  • Molecular analysis: rRNA sequencing, mitochondrial DNA analysis, and major enzyme studies support fungal classification (related to yeasts like Saccharomyces cerevisiae).
  • Cell wall contains β-glucans.

Laboratory Diagnosis:

  • Specimen: lung biopsy, bronchoalveolar lavage (sputum not suitable).
  • Staining: methenamine silver, Giemsa, or fluorescent antibody stain → typical cysts.
  • Culture: difficult.
  • ELISA for antigen & antibody — only for epidemiological purpose.
  • PCR.
❓ Cross questions (from additional)

P. jiroveci কি আগে protozoan ছিল?

হ্যাঁ — আগে protozoan হিসেবে শ্রেণিবদ্ধ ছিল; molecular study‑র পর fungi‑তে রূপান্তরিত হয়।

Ergosterol‑এর অনুপস্থিতি ও culture‑তে না ওঠা কীভাবে antifungal inefficacy ব্যাখ্যা করে?

Ergosterol না থাকা আর fungal media‑তে grow না করা — এই দুটোই conventional antifungal (যেগুলো ergosterol target করে)‑এর অকার্যকারিতা explains।

Treatment কী?

Trimethoprim‑sulfamethoxazole (TMP‑SMX) — এটা antibacterial/antiprotozoal, antifungal নয়।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, Pneumocystis jiroveci একটি unique organism — fungus ও parasite দুটোরই বৈশিষ্ট্য আছে, আর molecular evidence‑র ভিত্তিতে এখন fungus হিসেবে শ্রেণিবদ্ধ।

Peculiar properties — Human species: P. jiroveci, rat species: P. carinii; transmission — airborne organism inhalation; disease — atypical pneumonia (plasma cell pneumonia), imaging‑এ ground glass opacity; untreated হলে mortality প্রায় 100%।

কেন parasite‑এর মতো দেখায় — (১) cell membrane‑এ cholesterol (ergosterol নয়); (২) tissue‑এ cyst হিসেবে দেখা যায়; (৩) standard fungal culture media‑তে grow করে না; (৪) antifungal drugs largely ineffective; (৫) major surface glycoprotein‑এ antigenic variation — Trypanosoma brucei‑এর মতো।

কেন fungus — molecular analysis (rRNA sequencing, mitochondrial DNA, enzyme study) Saccharomyces cerevisiae‑এর মতো yeast‑এর সাথে সম্পর্কিত; আর cell wall‑এ β‑glucan থাকে।

Lab diagnosis — specimen lung biopsy/BAL (sputum suitable নয়); staining — methenamine silver, Giemsa, fluorescent antibody → typical cyst; culture difficult; ELISA only epidemiological purpose; PCR। Treatment — TMP‑SMX (antibacterial/antiprotozoal, antifungal নয়)।

M27

What are the diseases produced by Aspergillus? *** (A. flavus: ocular infection, liver damage; A. fumigatus: Allergic aspergilosis, fungal ball or aspergilloma)

📌 Hint: A. flavus: ocular infection, liver damage; A. fumigatus: Allergic aspergillosis, fungal ball
✅ Answer

Aspergillus species are molds with septate hyphae that form V-shaped (dichotomous) branches. Conidia form radiating chains.

Diseases by species:

Aspergillus flavus:

  • Ocular infections.
  • Liver damage or liver carcinoma — through its production of aflatoxins.

Aspergillus fumigatus:

  • Allergic aspergillosis:
    • Asthma (Type I hypersensitivity).
    • Allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis / farmer's lung (Type III hypersensitivity).
  • Fungal ball / Aspergilloma in the lungs — a radiopaque structure on chest X-ray that changes position when the patient moves from erect to supine.
  • Invasive aspergillosis — affecting lungs, liver, kidney, brain, eye etc.

Transmission: Airborne conidia.

Laboratory Diagnosis:

  • Microscopy: septate hyphae, regular V-shaped branches.
  • Culture: mold with green conidia in radiating chain.
❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Spoiled grain/peanut → liver metabolize → epoxide → p53 tumour suppressor gene‑এ mutation → hepatocellular carcinoma।

Radiopaque ball — patient‑এর posture বদলালে position বদলায়।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, Aspergillus species হলো mold — septate hyphae, V‑shaped (dichotomous) branch; conidia radiating chain‑এ থাকে। diseases species অনুযায়ী আলাদা।

A. flavus — ocular infection; liver damage বা liver carcinoma — কারণ এটা aflatoxin produce করে। A. fumigatus — allergic aspergillosis (asthma — Type I hypersensitivity; allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis / farmer's lung — Type III hypersensitivity); fungal ball/aspergilloma — chest X‑ray‑এ radiopaque structure, posture বদলালে position বদলায় (erect→supine); invasive aspergillosis — lungs, liver, kidney, brain, eye।

Transmission — airborne conidia। Lab diagnosis — microscopy: septate hyphae, regular V‑shaped branch; culture: mold, green conidia radiating chain।

Aflatoxin pathway মনে রাখুন — spoiled grain/peanut → epoxide → p53 mutation → HCC; আর aspergilloma posture‑dependent radiopaque ball।

M28

Enumerate the black fungus and white fungus. Why they are so called? *** (Black fungus: Mucormycosis, here tissue necrosis make black area; white fungus: Candidiasis, here white patches).

📌 Hint: Black fungus: Mucormycosis, tissue necrosis makes black area; white fungus: Candidiasis, white patches
✅ Answer

Black Fungus:

  • Disease: Mucormycosis (zygomycosis, phycomycosis).
  • Causative agents: Mucor & Rhizopus.
  • Why called "black": The infection causes tissue necrosis, which appears black in color.
  • Diseases: Rhinocerebral lesion (orbit, palate, face, nose & brain — cavernous sinus thrombosis is a common complication), pulmonary lesions (infarction, necrosis with cavitation).
  • Predisposing factors: Diabetes mellitus, immunocompromised state.
  • Microscopy: Non-septate (coenocytic) hyphae, irregular, right-angle branches.

White Fungus:

  • Disease: Candidiasis (especially invasive candidiasis).
  • Causative agents: Candida albicans (sometimes C. tropicalis, C. auris).
  • Why called "white": In candidiasis (like oral thrush), white patches are seen on the tongue, mouth, or throat.
❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Mucormycosis‑এর transmission ও culture note?

Transmission — asexual spore inhalation; culture‑তে spore sporangium‑এর ভিতরে enclosed থাকে।

Mucormycosis কেন prominent হলো (COVID era)?

COVID‑19 pandemic‑এ immunocompromised/diabetic রোগীদের মধ্যে এটি বেশি prominence পায়।

White fungus‑এর white color‑এর কারণ কী?

Pseudomembrane formation‑এর কারণে — যা pseudohyphae ও yeast cell দিয়ে তৈরি।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, black fungus আর white fungus দুটোই বলছি।

Black fungus — disease mucormycosis (zygomycosis, phycomycosis); causative agent Mucor & Rhizopus; "black" বলার কারণ tissue necrosis — যা কালো দেখায়। Diseases — rhinocerebral lesion (orbit, palate, face, nose & brain — cavernous sinus thrombosis common complication), pulmonary lesion (infarction, necrosis with cavitation)। Predisposing — diabetes mellitus, immunocompromised state। Microscopy — non‑septate (coenocytic) hyphae, irregular, right‑angle branch।

White fungus — disease candidiasis (বিশেষ করে invasive); causative agent Candida albicans (কখনো C. tropicalis, C. auris); "white" বলার কারণ oral thrush‑এর মতো candidiasis‑এ tongue, mouth, throat‑এ white patch দেখা যায়।

M29

What is fungal ball? *** (lungs lesion caused by Aspergillus fumigatus)

📌 Hint: Lung lesion caused by Aspergillus fumigatus
✅ Answer

Fungal ball (Aspergilloma) is a lung lesion caused by Aspergillus fumigatus. It is a mass of fungal hyphae, mucus, and cellular debris that colonizes a pre-existing lung cavity (e.g., from tuberculosis, sarcoidosis, or bronchiectasis).

Key features:

  • Appears as a radiopaque structure on chest X-ray.
  • Changes position when the patient is moved from erect to supine posture — this mobility is diagnostic.
  • Usually non-invasive but can cause hemoptysis (coughing up blood).

Classification of Aspergillus lung diseases:

  1. Allergic aspergillosis (asthma, ABPA)
  2. Fungal ball / Aspergilloma (colonization of cavity)
  3. Invasive aspergillosis (in immunocompromised)

Mold, septate hyphae, V‑shaped (dichotomous) branch।

Airborne conidia।

Microscopy — septate hyphae, regular V‑shaped branch; culture — mold, green conidia radiating chain।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, fungal ball (aspergilloma) হলো lung lesion, যা Aspergillus fumigatus ঘটায়। এটা fungal hyphae, mucus আর cellular debris‑এর mass, যা আগে থেকে থাকা lung cavity‑তে colonize করে (যেমন TB, sarcoidosis, bronchiectasis‑এর cavity)।

Aspergillus lung disease‑এর classification — (১) allergic aspergillosis (asthma, ABPA); (২) fungal ball/aspergilloma (cavity‑র colonization); (৩) invasive aspergillosis (immunocompromised‑এ)।

Morphology note — mold, septate hyphae, V‑shaped branching; transmission airborne conidia; culture green conidia radiating chain।

M30

What do you mean by rose handler diseases? * (Sporothrix schenckii)

📌 Hint: Sporothrix schenckii
✅ Answer

Rose handler disease (Rose gardener's disease / Sporotrichosis) is a subcutaneous mycosis caused by Sporothrix schenckii.

  • When S. schenckii is introduced into the skin (usually by a rose thorn prick), it causes a local painless pustule or ulcer with nodules along the draining lymphatics.
  • It occurs most often in gardeners, especially those who prune roses — hence the name "rose handler disease."
  • Sporothrix schenckii is a dimorphic fungus (mold at 25°C, yeast at 37°C).

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

S. schenckii‑এর classification কী?

Subcutaneous fungus — minor cut, scratch বা thorn‑এর মাধ্যমে skin inoculation হয়ে acquired হয়।

Nodule কোন পথ ধরে spread করে?

Nodule lymphatic drainage‑এর পথ ধরে — charakteristic lymphocutaneous spread pattern।

Diagnosis ও treatment?

Diagnosis — SDA‑তে culture, dimorphism 25°C (mold) vs 37°C (yeast) দিয়ে confirm; treatment — antifungal itraconazole, potassium iodide।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, rose handler disease (rose gardener's disease / sporotrichosis) হলো একটি subcutaneous mycosis, যা Sporothrix schenckii ঘটায়।

যখন S. schenckii skin‑এ প্রবেশ করে (সাধারণত rose thorn prick দিয়ে), তখন local painless pustule বা ulcer তৈরি হয়, আর draining lymphatic‑এর পথ ধরে nodule থাকে। এটি বেশি হয় gardener‑দের মধ্যে, বিশেষ করে যারা rose prune করে — তাই "rose handler disease" নাম।

S. schenckii হলো dimorphic fungus — 25°C‑এ mold, 37°C‑এ yeast।

Classification — subcutaneous fungus, skin inoculation (cut/scrat/thorn) দিয়ে। Spread pattern — lymphocutaneous। Diagnosis — SDA culture + dimorphism (25 vs 37°C)। Treatment — itraconazole, potassium iodide।

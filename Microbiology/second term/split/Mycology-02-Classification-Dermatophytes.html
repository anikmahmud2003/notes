Classification & Dermatophytes

Habitat-based classification, dimorphic fungi, dermatophytes & their sites
M7

Classify fungus according to habitat. ***

📌 Hint: Superficial cutaneous, subcutaneous, systemic, opportunistic
✅ Answer

Clinical classification based on site of infection / habitat:

A. Superficial Cutaneous Fungi — affect skin, hair, nails; confined to body surfaces.

  • Malassezia furfur — tinea versicolor
  • Dermatophytes (Epidermophyton, Trichophyton, Microsporum) — dermatophytosis / tinea / ringworm
  • Trichosporon beigelii — white piedra
  • Piedraia hortae — black piedra
  • Hortaea werneckii — tinea nigra
  • Candida albicans (sometimes) — superficial candidiasis

B. Subcutaneous Fungi — inoculated through skin by cuts, scratches, or thorns.

  • Sporothrix schenckii — sporotrichosis (rose handler disease)
  • Rhinosporidium seeberi — rhinosporidiosis
  • Madurella mycetomatis — mycetoma / madura foot
  • Dematiaceous fungi (Fonsecaea, Phialophora, Exophiala, Cladosporium) — chromoblastomycosis & pheohyphomycosis

C. Systemic / Deep Fungi — usually dimorphic; affect internal organs.

  • Histoplasma capsulatum — histoplasmosis
  • Blastomyces dermatitidis — blastomycosis
  • Coccidioides immitis — coccidioidomycosis
  • Paracoccidioides brasiliensis — paracoccidioidomycosis

D. Opportunistic Fungi — cause disease in immunocompromised individuals.

  • Candida albicans — candidiasis
  • Cryptococcus neoformans — cryptococcal meningitis
  • Pneumocystis jiroveci — pneumocystis pneumonia
  • Aspergillus fumigatus — aspergillosis
  • Mucor & Rhizopus — mucormycosis (black fungus)
  • Penicillium marneffei — penicilliosis
❓ Cross questions (from additional)

কোন fungi নিঃসন্দেহে superficial, আর কোনগুলো cutaneous?

Malassezia furfur ও dermatophytes নিঃসন্দেহে superficial; Candida ও dermatophytes‑কে superficial group‑এর মধ্যেই cutaneous হিসেবেও ধরা হয়।

Subcutaneous fungi কীভাবে infection তৈরি করে?

Skin‑এর thorn, splinter বা ছোটখাটো trauma‑এর মাধ্যমে inoculation হয়ে সংক্রমণ হয়।

Systemic fungi সাধারণত কেমন হয়?

সাধারণত dimorphic — environment‑এ mold, host‑এ yeast।

Aspergillus‑এর বিশেষত্ব কী?

Aspergillus হলো এমন একটি opportunistic fungus যা আবার moldও বটে, এবং এর septate hyphae থাকে।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, habitat অনুযায়ী classification‑এ ৪টি group আছে — A. Superficial Cutaneous, B. Subcutaneous, C. Systemic/Deep ও D. Opportunistic।

A. Superficial Cutaneous Fungi — skin, hair, nail‑কে প্রভাবিত করে, body surface‑এ সীমাবদ্ধ। Example — Malassezia furfur (tinea versicolor), dermatophytes (Epidermophyton, Trichophyton, Microsporum) — dermatophytosis/tinea/ringworm, Trichosporon beigelii (white piedra), Piedraia hortae (black piedra), Hortaea werneckii (tinea nigra), আর কখনো Candida albicans (superficial candidiasis)।

B. Subcutaneous Fungi — cut, scratch বা thorn দিয়ে skin‑এর inoculation হয়। Example — Sporothrix schenckii (sporotrichosis/rose handler disease), Rhinosporidium seeberi (rhinosporidiosis), Madurella mycetomatis (mycetoma/madura foot), আর dematiaceous fungi (Fonsecaea, Phialophora, Exophiala, Cladosporium) — chromoblastomycosis & pheohyphomycosis।

C. Systemic / Deep Fungi — সাধারণত dimorphic, internal organ প্রভাবিত করে। Example — Histoplasma capsulatum (histoplasmosis), Blastomyces dermatitidis (blastomycosis), Coccidioides immitis (coccidioidomycosis), Paracoccidioides brasiliensis (paracoccidioidomycosis)।

D. Opportunistic Fungi — immunocompromised ব্যক্তিদের disease করে। Example — Candida albicans (candidiasis), Cryptococcus neoformans (cryptococcal meningitis), Pneumocystis jiroveci (pneumocystis pneumonia), Aspergillus fumigatus (aspergillosis), Mucor & Rhizopus (mucormycosis/black fungus), Penicillium marneffei (penicilliosis)।

M8

Define dimorphic fungus with example. *********

📌 Hint: Mold at 25°C, yeast at 37°C — remember the main dimorphic fungi
✅ Answer

Dimorphic fungi are fungi that exist in two forms depending on temperature: they grow as molds (hyphae) at room temperature (25°C) in the environment and as yeast at body temperature (37°C) in human tissues. This property is called thermal dimorphism.

The mnemonic: "Mold at 25, Yeast at 37 — the lung fungi!"

Examples of dimorphic fungi:

  • Histoplasma capsulatum — histoplasmosis
  • Blastomyces dermatitidis — blastomycosis
  • Coccidioides immitis — coccidioidomycosis
  • Paracoccidioides brasiliensis — paracoccidioidomycosis
  • Sporothrix schenckii — sporotrichosis
  • Penicillium marneffei — penicilliosis
  • Malassezia furfur — tinea versicolor
❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Dimorphic fungus‑এর pathogenesis কীভাবে?

Environment থেকে mold spore inhale হয় → lung‑এ গিয়ে spore yeast‑এ differentiate করে → বেশিরভাগ infection asymptomatic ও self‑limited, কিন্তু disseminate করলে রোগ হতে পারে।

কোন fungi‑তে dimorphism বেশি দেখা যায়?

সব systemic/deep fungi সাধারণত dimorphic হয়ে থাকে।

Mold থেকে yeast‑এ রূপান্তর কেন virulence factor?

কারণ yeast form শরীরে macrophage‑এর ভিতরেও survive করতে পারে — এটাই আসল virulence factor।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, dimorphic fungi হলো সেই fungus যারা temperature অনুযায়ী দুই রকম form‑এ থাকে — environment‑এ 25°C‑এ mold (hyphae), আর human tissue‑এ 37°C‑এ yeast। এই বৈশিষ্ট্যকে বলে thermal dimorphism। Mnemonic — "Mold at 25, Yeast at 37 — the lung fungi!"

Example — Histoplasma capsulatum (histoplasmosis), Blastomyces dermatitidis (blastomycosis), Coccidioides immitis (coccidioidomycosis), Paracoccidioides brasiliensis (paracoccidioidomycosis), Sporothrix schenckii (sporotrichosis), Penicillium marneffei (penicilliosis), আর Malassezia furfur (tinea versicolor)।

Pathogenesis‑উলটা বললে — environment থেকে mold spore inhale হয়, lung‑এ গিয়ে yeast‑এ convert হয়। বেশিরভাগ infection asymptomatic, self‑limited; তবে disseminate করলে নানা disease হতে পারে। সব systemic/deep fungi সাধারণত dimorphic। আর mold→yeast এই transition‑টা একটা key virulence factor, কারণ yeast form macrophage‑এর ভিতরে survive করতে পারে।

M9

What do you mean by dermatophytes? Mention the dermatophytes and their site of infection. *****

📌 Hint: Keratinophilic fungi; infect skin, hair, nails
✅ Answer

Dermatophytes are a group of fungi that infect only superficial keratinized tissues — the skin and skin appendages such as hair and nails. They are keratinophilic, meaning they thrive on keratin.

Keratin types in different structures:

  • Skin — soft keratin
  • Hair — hard and soft keratin
  • Nail — hard keratin
GenusSpeciesSite of Infection
EpidermophytonE. floccosumSkin & Nail
TrichophytonT. rubrumSkin, Hair & Nail
T. tonsuransSkin, Hair & Nail
T. mentagrophytesSkin, Hair & Nail
T. schoenleinii, T. verrucosumSkin, Hair & Nail
MicrosporumM. canisSkin & Hair
M. gypseumSkin & Hair

Key point: Epidermophyton does NOT infect hair; Microsporum does NOT infect nails; Trichophyton infects all three.

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Dermatophytosis কীভাবে ছড়ায় (transmission)?

Direct contact — infected person থেকে (anthropophilic), infected animal থেকে যেমন dog/cat (zoophilic — প্রধানত Microsporum), আর indirect contact — shared clothing, comb, towel দিয়ে।

এই রোগকে আর কী কী নামে ডাকা হয়?

Dermatophytosis / tinea / ringworm infection নামে ডাকা হয়।

Tinea কীভাবে body area অনুযায়ী নাম পায়?

Tinea capitis (head hair), tinea corporis (trunk), tinea cruris (groin), tinea pedis (foot), tinea unguium (nail), tinea manuum (hand), tinea barbae (beard), tinea faciei (face)।

Microconidia‑র shape কী কী?

T. rubrum — single, tear drop; T. mentagrophytes — cluster, oval; T. tonsurans — elongated।

আর macroconidia‑র shape?

Epidermophyton — club shaped; Trichophyton — cylindrical; Microsporum — spindle shaped।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, dermatophytes হলো সেই fungi যারা শুধু superficial keratinized tissue‑কে infect করে — skin আর তার appendage, যেমন hair ও nail। এরা keratinophilic, অর্থাৎ keratin‑এ বেঁচে থাকে।

Keratin type — skin‑এ soft keratin, hair‑এ hard ও soft keratin, nail‑এ hard keratin। তিনটি genus — Epidermophyton, Trichophyton, Microsporum। E. floccosum শুধু skin & nail‑এ infect করে। Trichophyton‑এর species গুলো (rubrum, tonsurans, mentagrophytes, schoenleinii, verrucosum) skin, hair ও nail — তিনটিতেই infect করে। Microsporum (canis, gypseum) শুধু skin & hair infect করে।

Key point — Epidermophyton hair infect করে না, Microsporum nail infect করে না, Trichophyton তিনটিই infect করে।

Transmission — direct contact: infected person (anthropophilic) বা infected animal (zoophilic — যেমন Microsporum), আর indirect — shared clothing, comb, towel। রোগের নাম dermatophytosis/tinea/ringworm। Site অনুযায়ী — tinea capitis (head), corporis (trunk), cruris (groin), pedis (foot), unguium (nail), manuum (hand), barbae (beard), faciei (face)। Identification — microconidia: T. rubrum single tear drop, T. mentagrophytes cluster oval, T. tonsurans elongated; macroconidia: Epidermophyton club, Trichophyton cylindrical, Microsporum spindle।

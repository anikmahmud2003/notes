Id reaction, dematiaceous fungi, sexual & asexual spores
M31

What is "id reaction"? ** (hypersensitivity reaction by circulating fungal antigen but not by fungus)

📌 Hint: Hypersensitivity reaction by circulating fungal antigen but not by fungus
✅ Answer

"Id reaction" (Dermatophytid reaction) is a hypersensitivity reaction that occurs in response to circulating fungal antigens — without the actual presence of fungus (hyphae) at the site of the reaction.

  • It is a systemic immune response — the fungal antigens from the primary infection site travel via the blood and trigger an allergic reaction elsewhere on the skin.
  • The "id" reaction sites do NOT contain fungal organisms on microscopy or culture.
  • It is a Type IV hypersensitivity reaction.
  • Commonly seen in dermatophytosis (ringworm) — e.g., vesicular eruptions on hands when feet are infected.

M32

What do you mean by dematiaceous fungi? * (having melanin or melanin like pigment. Exophiala, Cladosporium or Hortaea etc. but Mucor is not dematiaceous fungi)

📌 Hint: Having melanin or melanin-like pigment. Exophiala, Cladosporium or Hortaea etc. but Mucor is not dematiaceous
✅ Answer

Dematiaceous fungi are fungi that have melanin or melanin-like pigment in the wall of their hyphae and/or spores. Their conidia or hyphae appear dark-colored (gray or black), which is why they are called "dematiaceous" (from Latin dematiaceous = dark-pigmented).

Important dematiaceous fungi:

  1. Fonsecaea — F. pedrosoi, F. compacta
  2. Phialophora — P. verrucosa
  3. Cladophialophora — C. carrionii, C. bantiana
  4. Exophiala — E. dermatitidis, E. jeanselmei, E. spinifera, E. moniliae
  5. Cladosporium / Hortaea — C. werneckii (= Hortaea werneckii)

Important: Mucor is NOT a dematiaceous fungus — it does not contain melanin pigment.

Diseases caused by dematiaceous fungi:

  • Chromoblastomycosis: Chronic granulomatous infection of cutaneous and subcutaneous tissues.
  • Pheohyphomycosis: Primary or opportunistic infection ranging from superficial tissue to deep organs.

Exophiala dermatitidis কোন disease করে?

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, dematiaceous fungi হলো সেইসব fungus যাদের hyphae/spore‑এর wall‑এ melanin বা melanin‑like pigment থাকে। তাই এরা dark‑colored (gray বা black) দেখায় — "dematiaceous" শব্দটি Latin থেকে এসেছে, মানে dark‑pigmented।

Important dematiaceous fungi — Fonsecaea (pedrosoi, compacta), Phialophora (verrucosa), Cladophialophora (carrionii, bantiana), Exophiala (dermatitidis, jeanselmei, spinifera, moniliae), Cladosporium/Hortaea (werneckii)।

Important: Mucor dematiaceous নয় — এতে melanin pigment থাকে না।

Disease — chromoblastomycosis (cutaneous/subcutaneous‑এর chronic granulomatous infection) ও pheohyphomycosis (superficial tissue থেকে deep organ পর্যন্ত, primary বা opportunistic)। Extra — tinea nigra H. werneckii‑এর কারণে brownish spot; P. verrucosa → chromoblastomycosis; E. dermatitidis → pheohyphomycosis।

M33

Mention the name of sexual and asexual spore.* (sexual spore: zygospore, ascospore, basidiospore, asexual spore: arthrospore, blastospore, chlamydospore, sporangiospore, microconidia and macroconidia)

📌 Hint: Sexual: zygospore, ascospore, basidiospore; Asexual: arthrospore, blastospore, chlamydospore, sporangiospore, microconidia, macroconidia
✅ Answer

A. Sexual Spores:

  1. Zygospore: Single large spore with thick walls.
  2. Ascospore: Formed inside a sac called an ascus.
  3. Basidiospore: Formed externally on the tip of a pedestal called a basidium.

B. Asexual Spores (Conidia):

  1. Arthrospore: Arises by fragmentation of the ends of hyphae. Example: Coccidioides immitis.
  2. Blastospore (Blastoconidia): Formed by budding. Multiple buds that do not detach form pseudohyphae. Example: Candida albicans.
  3. Chlamydospore: Rounded, thick-walled, quite resistant. Example: Candida albicans.
  4. Sporangiospore: Formed within a sac (sporangium) on a stalk by molds. Example: Rhizopus and Mucor.
  5. Microconidia: Small conidia. Example: Aspergillus, Microsporum.
  6. Macroconidia: Large conidia. Example: Microsporum.

Note: Fungi that do NOT form sexual spores are termed fungi imperfecti (Deuteromycotina).

❓ Cross questions (from additional)

Macroconidia‑র shape genus অনুযায়ী কী কী?

Epidermophyton — club shaped; Trichophyton — cylindrical; Microsporum — spindle shaped।

আর microconidia species অনুযায়ী?

T. rubrum — single tear drop; T. mentagrophytes — cluster oval; T. tonsurans — elongated।

Sporangiospore আর conidia‑র পার্থক্য কী?

Sporangiospore sporangium‑এর ভিতরে enclosed; conidia naked (enclosed নয়)।

🗣️ ভাইভায় যেভাবে বুঝাব (বাংলা লিপি)

স্যার, sexual ও asexual spore‑এর নাম বলছি।

A. Sexual spore (৩টি): Zygospore — single, বড়, thick‑walled spore; Ascospore — ascus নামক থলির ভিতরে তৈরি হয়; Basidiospore — basidium নামক pedestal‑এর tip‑এ externally তৈরি হয়।

B. Asexual spore (conidia) (৬টি): Arthrospore — hyphae‑এর প্রান্ত fragmentation‑এর ফলে; example Coccidioides immitis। Blastospore (blastoconidia) — budding‑এর ফলে; একাধিক bud detach না হলে pseudohyphae; example Candida albicans। Chlamydospore — rounded, thick‑walled, বেশ resistant; example Candida albicans। Sporangiospore — stalk‑এর উপর sporangium নামক থলির ভিতরে; example Rhizopus, Mucor। Microconidia — ছোট conidia; example Aspergillus, Microsporum। Macroconidia — বড় conidia; example Microsporum।

Note — যেসব fungus sexual spore তৈরি করে না, তাদের বলে fungi imperfecti (Deuteromycotina)। Shape মনে রাখুন — macroconidia: Epidermophyton club, Trichophyton cylindrical, Microsporum spindle; microconidia: rubrum single tear drop, mentagrophytes cluster oval, tonsurans elongated; sporangiospore enclosed, conidia naked।

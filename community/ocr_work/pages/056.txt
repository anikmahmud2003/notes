Risk / Risk factor: The term ‘risk factor’ is used by different authors with at least 2 meanings-
> An attribute or exposure that is significantly associated with development of a disease.
> A determinant that can be modified by intervention, thereby reducing the possibility of occurrence
of disease or other specific outcomes.

Many diseases, e.g. coronary heart disease, cancers peptic ulcer etc. agent is still unidentified. In these
cases, aetiology is discussed in terms of risk factor.

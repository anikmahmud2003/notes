Q. How can you control air pollution of Dhaka city? [DU-14J]
Q. How air pollution in Dhaka city can be prevented?
Q. How can you prevent air pollution in a metropolitan city? [CU-13Ju]
Answer
To prevent and control air pollution of Dhaka city, following measures should be taken without delay:
1) Import and permission of three wheeler auto-rickshaws with two stroke engines to be stopped.
2) Strict monitoring of emission by all types of motor vehicles plying in the capital city and making
violation a punishable offence.
3) Phasing out to play on competent eco-friendly vehicles with eco-friendly fuels. :
4) Eastern refinery to refine leaded fuel into lead free fuel and import petroleum products with no or
least content of lead.
5) Greening the city by plantation.
6) Shifting the industries away from main residential city.

7) Surveillance of air quality regularly.
[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 5" / 504]

Ventilation

Q. Define ventilation. [SUST-13Ju]
Q. Write short note on: Ventilation.
Answer

Ventilation:
Ventilation is a science of maintaining atmospheric conditions which are comfortable and healthful to

the human body.

The modern concept of ventilation:
Replacement of vitiated air by a supply of fresh outdoor air and control of the quality of incoming air

with regard to its temperature, humidity and purity with a view to provide a thermal environment that is
comfortable and free from risk of infection.

Purpose of ventilation:
1) To replace vitiated air.

2) To supply oxygen and fresh air.
3) To control temperature and humidity in order to provide thermal environment.

4) To dilute air borne contamination to an acceptable concentration.
[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 5" / 505 + Park /24"/ 775]

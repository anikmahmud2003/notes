Q. Mention the effects of radiation on human health. [RU-10Ju]
Q. What are the biological effects of radiation? [RU-17N, 16M, 13Ju, 12Ju]
Q. Write short note on: Biological effects of radiation.
Answer
Biological effects of radiation: The biological effects of radiation may be divided into two separate groups ~
A) Somatic effects:
1) Immediate;
> Radiation sickness
> Acute radiation syndrome

2) Delayed:
> Leukaemia
> Carcinogenesis
> Foetal developmental abnormalities
> Shortening of life

B) Genetic effects:
1) Chromosome mutations.
2) Point mutations.

Q. What is total fertility rate (TFR)?
Answer

Total fertility rate (TFR):

It is the average number of children a woman would have if she were to pass through her reproductive
years bearing children at the same rates as the women now in each age group.

Importance: TFR is considered as refined measure of fertility; it gives the approximate magnitude of
“completed family size”.

Q. Enlist the components [DU-18N,17N] / equipments [SUST-18N, | 7N, 125] of cold chain system,
Q. List the cold chain equipments with their uses.
Answer .

Cold chain equipment:
1) Walk in cold rooms (WIC):

> Located at regional level.
> Store vaccine up to 3 months,
> Serve 4-5 districts.

2) Deep freezers and ice lined refrigerators (ILR): Used for making ice packs and to store OPV and

measles vaccines.

3) Small deep freezers & ILR (140 Itr): Used for preparing frozen ice packs which are used in cold
boxes, vaccine carriers for transportation of vaccines and during the sessions.

4) Cold boxes:

> Used mainly for transportation of the vaccine.

> Keep vaccine cold for periods up to one week with no power supply at all.
5) Vaccine carriers:

> Used to carry small quantities of vaccine.
> Keep vaccine safe up to 3 days.

6) Day carriers / flasks: Used only for few hours period.

7) Ice packs.

Q. How parasitic diseases can be reduced/ controlled?
Answer
Control measures of parasitic disease:

A) Primary control measures:
1) Destruction of vectors

2) Avoiding sleeping on floor

3) Use of fine mesh net

4) Maintenance of environmental sanitation

5) Safe water supply

6) Improvement of food hygiene

7) Maintenance of personal hygiene

8) Personal protection use of shoes for prevention of ankylostomiasis
9) Eating raw or undercooked food should be avoided

10) Sanitary control of all places & food be inspection

11) Avoidance of swimming, bathing, washing in infected water.
12) Health education.

Q. Write down the epidemiological features of syphilis.
Answer
Epidemiological features of syphilis:
** Occurrence: Syphilis is now virtually worldwide. It is endemic in Bangladesh.
* Ecological triad:
> Agent: Treponema pallidum.
> Host:
Y Age: It affects in all ages. Teenagers are at high -risk.
Y Sex: Equally present in both sex.
v Race: Affects all races.
Y Immunity: Every patient with syphilis develops some resistance of his own infection.
> Environment: Equally occurs in all seasons from the affected person.

«* Natural history:
> Reservoir: cases and carriers
> Source of infection:
Y Lesion of skin and mucous membrane
Y Saliva
Y Semen
Y Cervical and vaginal secretion
Y Blood
> Infectious period: Agent may survive in body for life long and can spread infection.
> Mode of transmission:
Y Sexual intercourse.
Y Indirect sexual contact (kissing & unusual sex acts)
Y Blood transfusion or injection.
Y Transplacental transmission by an infected mother to the fetus in uterus.
> Incubation period: commonly 14 - 28 days but with extremes 9 - 90 days.

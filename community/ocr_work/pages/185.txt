Q. Write down the clinical features of rabies in dogs.

Answer

Clinical features of rabies in dogs: The disease in dogs may manifest in the following forms-
“* Furious type:

1) It is the common type and is more frequent.

2) The dog is easily irritated and may bite.
3) Later on, it becomes restless, excited, furious, may bite anybody and has a tendency to snap at

anything that comes on its way.
4) The dog will attempt to eat indigestibles like stick, straw, paper, etc.

5) It will leave its home and roam about.
6) Its eyes are wide open and have fixed gaze, conjunctivae is injected, voice changes, paralysis

starts at hind limbs and then on fore limbs.
7) The rabid dog dies within 10 days.
“* Dump type:
1) There is no irritability.
2) The animal hides in dark corners of the house and suffers from sleepiness and melancholia.

3) Paralysis of jaw and limbs sets in.
4) Death occurs within 3 days after the onset.
5) Dog here usually does not bite.

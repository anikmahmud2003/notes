Q. Write down the complications of leprosy. [RU-16M]
Answer
Complications of leprosy:

1) Hands & feet deformity.

2) Blindness.

3) Peripheral neuropathy,

4) Multi-organ failure.

Q. How can you prevent and control leprosy in Bangladesh?

Q: How can you prevent the spread of leprosy in the community?
Answer

Preventive and control measure of leprosy:
1) Medical measures:

Estimation of the problem.

Early case detection.

Multidrug therapy.

Surveillance.

Immunoprophylaxis.

Chemoprophylaxis.

Rehabilitation.

Health education.

Isolation.

Disinfections,

Leprosy-vaccine.

Others protection of people at risk improvement of socio-economic conditions of lepers.

2) Social measure:
> Not use of leper food, drink and cloths.
> Arise in the standard of living.
> Reduction of overcrowding,

3) Programme management:
> Planning & programme management is essential ingredients.
4) Evaluation:

> To check whether the desired results are being achieved.
> To modified programme.

Q. Discuss ongoing leprosy elimination program in Bangladesh. [DU-14Ju]
Q. Discuss the leprosy control program in Bangladesh.
Answer
Leprosy control program in Bangladesh:
> Before 1965, leprosy control services were limited to different Christian Missionary in high
endemic areas,
In 1965, leprosy control activity was started through 3 Govt. leprosy hospitals (Dhaka, Sylhet and
Nilphamary) and gradually expanded o upazilla level. That ti
By 1985, 120 upazillas were covered. MDT (multi-drug therapy) was also introduced in 1985.

In 1991, Bangladesh Govt. signed the WHO declaration of elimination of leprosy b 2000 and
decided to expand the MDT countrywide,

In 1996, Bangladesh achieved 100% MDT coverage.
National elimination of leprosy was achieved by 1998, two years ahead of the set target.
Goal of National Leprosy Elimination Program (NELP):

> Initial goal was to eliminate leprosy at national level by 2000 (achieved by 1998) and eliminate
leprosy at sub-national level by 2007 which could not be achieved and reset to achieve by 2011.

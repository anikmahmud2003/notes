Q. Explain the term impairment, disability & handicap. [RU-16M] _-
Answer ‘
“> Impairment: Any loss or abnormality of psychological, physiological, or anatomical structure or
function. Example — loss of foot. .

+ Disability: As a result of impairment, if affected person is unable to perform any activity or to carry out

Q. Write the importance of early diagnosis and treatment of a disease.
Q. Early diagnosis and treatment is a part of prevention — explain.
Answer

Importance of early diagnosis and treatment:

1) Treatment of the case is prevention for susceptible hosts: Treatment reduces the communicability of
disease, cuts short the duration of illness and prevents development of secondary cases in the
community. In some diseases like tuberculosis, syphilis, leprosy treatment is of primary importance
in interruption of transmission.

2) In some diseases, early treatment is essential to prevent secondary complications; e.g. syphilis.

3) Rarely treatment is an important part of prevention in respect of certain diseases and conditions,
e.g. carcinoma, cardiovascular diseases.

4) Treatment relieves pain, arrest or cure disease and thus prevent disability and heath.

From above positive supports it is proved that "early diagnosis and treatment is a part of
prevention.

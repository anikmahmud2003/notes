Q. Discuss the traditional family planning methods.
Q. How breast feeding acts as a contraceptive measure?
Answer

Traditional family planning methods: . . .
1. Coitus interruptus: This is the oldest method of fertility control. It continues to be a widely practiced

method. The male withdraws before ejaculation and thereby tries to prevent deposition of semen into
the vagina. The chief drawback of this method is that precoital secretion. If the male may contain sperm
and even a drop of semen is sufficient to cause pregnancy. Therefore the failure rate with this method

may be as high as 25%.

2. Abstinence: Although in theory sexual abstinence is the most effective method in family planning but
in practice it cannot be recommended because it amounts to suppression of a biological need.

3. Breast feeding (lactational amenorrhoea method for 6 month): Numerous studies have documented

the fact that lactation prolongs postpartum amenorrhoea and provide considerable protection against
pregnancy. This is an important method in those countries where prolonged breast-feeding is widely
practiced. In Bangladesh mean duration of breast feeding is about 24 months.

4. Sex without coitus: This non-coital form of sexual intimacy relates to holding of hands hugging,
kissing, massage, mutual masturbation, petting, oral-genital sex and use of stimulation device such as
vibrators etc.

Q. Write short note on: Birth control vaccine.
Answer
Birth control vaccine:

The vaccine is prepared from beta- subunit of human chorionic gonadotropin, a hormone produced in
early pregnancy. Immunization with hCGH would block continuation of pregnancy. Antibodies appeared in
about 4 to 6 weeks and reach maximum after about 5 months and reach zero level within 6 to 11 months. The
immunity can be boosted by a second injection.

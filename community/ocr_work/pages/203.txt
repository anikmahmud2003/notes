Epidemiology of malaria:
* Occurrence; Malaria has a worldwide distribution.
* Epidemiological triad:
1) Agent factors:
a) Agent: P. vivax & P. falciparum account more than 9.
Vv P. vivax
YP. faciparum
YP. malariae
YP. ovale.

b) Reservoir of infection: Human: Is one who harbours the sexual forms-gametocyte,
Conditions must be met before a person can serve as a reservoir, —
Y The person must harbour both the sexes of the gametocyte in his blood.
v The gametocyte must be mature.
Y The gametocyte must be viable.
Y The gametocytes must be present in sufficient density to infect mosquito (at
least 12/cubic mm of blood).

c) Period of communicability: Malaria is communicable as long as viable gametocytes
exist in the circulating blood in sufficient density to infect vector mosquito.

2) Host factors:

Age: Malaria affects all ages

Sex: Male > female

Race: Duffy negative are resistant to P. vivax infection.

Social & economic factor: More in underdeveloped countries.

Pregnancy: 7 risk

Housing: more in all ventilated & ill lighted house.

Occupation: More in agriculture practitioner.

Human habits: Sleeping out of house & mosquito net then are susceptible to malaria.
Immunity: The epidemic of malaria is influenced by immune status of the population.

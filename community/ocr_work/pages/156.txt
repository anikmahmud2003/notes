Objectives of NELP:

1) Provide diagnosis and MDT services to all leprosy patients.

2) Detect > 85% of the estimated cases.

3) To cure (treatment complete) > 95% of those registered for treatment with MDT.
4) Reduce rate of deformity grade-2 among newly detected cases below 5%.

1) To provide fixed duration MDT to all detected leprosy patients free of charges using upazilla (even
below level if necessary) as the peripheral unit.

2) To integrate leprosy elimination program with general health services.

3) Promote coordination with NGOs and other partners.

4) To intensify advocacy, and IEC (information, education & communication) efforts and activities in
order to increase community awareness and ensure community participation in leprosy work.

5) To involve all general medical practitioners and medical/nursing/paramedical institutions through
appropriate approaches and training/orientations in order to seek their support in leprosy
elimination.

6) Monitoring and evaluation of performance through standard recording & reporting system.

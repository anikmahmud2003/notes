Q. Explain why diarrhoeal disease are endemic in Bangladesh?
Answer

Cause for endemicity of diarrhoeal disease in BD:
1) Lack of proper breast feeding and weaning practice.

2) Lack of safe drinking water and inadequate safe water supply system.
3) Poor personal hygiene.

4) Not proper use of sanitary latrine.
5) Lack of vaccine against diarrhoea.
6) Lack of food sanitation.

7) Lack of proper excreta disposal.
8) Lack of vector control.

9) Lack of health education.

10) High density of population.

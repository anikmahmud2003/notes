Indications for cohort studies:
1) There is good association between exposure & disease,
2) Exposure is less but incidence of disease among the exposed is high.

3) Follow up is easy; cohort is stable, co-operative and easily accessible.
4) Large fund.

Types of cohort studies:
1) Retrospective cohort studies,

2) P spective cohort studies,

Merits / advantages of cohort studies:
1) Incidence can be calculated.

2) Several possible outcomes can be studied simultaneously.
3) It provides a direct estimate of relative risk.

4) Dose-response ratios can also be calculated. 4
5) Chance of bias is minimum as comparison groups are formed before disease develops.

Demerits disadvantages of cohort studies:

1) It involves a large number of people and is generally unsuitable for investigation of uncommon
disease.

2) It takes a long time to complete the study and obtain results.

3) Certain administrative problems such as loss of experienced staff, loss of funding and extensive
record keeping are inevitable.

4) There may be loss of substantial proportional of the original cohort.

5) The selection of the comparison group is a limiting factor.

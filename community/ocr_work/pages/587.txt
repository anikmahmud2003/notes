Health information

Q. State health information system. [RU-08Ju]
Q. What are the components of health information system?
Q. Enumerate the sources of health information in Bangladesh. [RU-08Ju]

Answer

Health information system:

Health information system is defined as “a mechanism for the collection, processing, analysis and
transmission of information required for organizing and Operating health services, and also for research and

training”.
Components of health information system:
1) Demography and vital events.
2) Environmental health statistics,
3) Health status — mortality, morbidity, disability and quality of life.
4) Health resources — facilities, beds, manpower.
5) Utilization and non-utilization of health services — attendance, admissions, waiting lists.
6) Indices of outcome of medical care.
7) Financial statistics (cost, expenditure) related to the particular objective.

Uses of health information:

1) To measure the health status of the people of a community and to quantify the health problems and
medical & health care needs of a community.

2) For local, national and international comparison of health status.

3) For planning, administration and effective management of health services and programmes.

4) For assessing whether health services are accomplishing their objects in terms of their effectiveness
and efficacy.

5) For assessing the attitudes and degree of satisfaction of the beneficiaries with the health system.

6) For research into particular problems of health and disease.

Statistics & Biostati

Q. Define statistics. Describe in brief the function and use of statistics.

Answer

Statistics:
According to Croxton and Cowden, statistics may be defined as “the science of collection, presentation,

analysis and interpretation of numerical data.”

Function and uses of statistics;
1. Collection of information in the best possible way.

Simplification of huge complex sets of data.

Elucidation of ideas- formulation of hypothesis.

Community health diagnosis.

Measurement of association between two or more variables.

Prediction and forecasting,

Designing experimental study.

Analyzing data and drawing conclusions,

Providing information support to planning, management, monitoring and evaluation of health
service programmes.

Y The term “enteric fever” includes both typhoid and paratyphoid fevers.

Y Typhoid fever is an acute infectious communicable disease caused by Salmonella typhi and is

Epidemiology of typhoid fever:

“ Occurrence: Worldwide developing countries incidence varies from 100 — 1500/lakh.

* Ecological triad:
1) Agent factors: S. typhi is the major cause of enteric fever. S. paratyphi A and S. paratyphi B.

are relatively infrequent. S. typhi has three main antigens-O, H and Vi.

2) Host factors:
> Age: Highest incidence of this disease occurs in the 5-19 years of age group.
> Sex: Males > Females.
> Immunity: All the ages are susceptible to infection. Antibody may be stimulated by
the infection or by immunization.

3) Environmental and social factors:
Poor socio economic condition.
> Low standard of personal hygiene.
> Non-availability of safe water.
> Inadequate disposal of excreta.
> Incidence is high in rainy season.

“> Natural history:
1) Incubation period: Usually 10-14 days. But it may be as short as 3 days or as long as three

weeks depending upon the dose of the bacilli ingested.

2) Mode of transmission:
> Faecal oral route or
> Urine oral routes.

3) Reservoir of infection: Man is the only known reservoir of infection
> Case: The case may be infectious as long as bacilli appear in stools or urine.
> Carriers: The carriers may be temporary (incubatory convalescent) / chronic.

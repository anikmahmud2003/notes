Q. Define infection.
Q. What are the levels of infection?
Answer

Infection:
Infection means the entry and development or multiplication of an infectious agent in the body of man,

or animals. It also implies the body responds in some way to defend itself against invader either in the form of
immune response or disease. An infection does not always cause illness.

Types / levels of infections:
1) Colonization: Normally present in the body & lodgment; e.g. S. aureus in skin & naso-pharynx.

2) Sub-clinical infection or inapparent infection: No sign & symptoms, e.g. Polio.
3) Latent infection: c.g. virus of herpes simplex.
4) Manifest or clinical infection: Clinical sign & symptoms present.

[Ref- Park /24"/97]

Q. Define epizootic with examples. [RU-14J]
Answer

Epizootic:
An outbreak (epidemic) of disease in an animal population (often with the implication that it may also

affect human population). Only a few zoonotic agents cause major epidemics. Notable among these are the
agents of anthrax, brucellosis, rabies, influenza, Rift valley fever, Q fever, Japanese encephalitis and equine

encephalitis.
[Ref- Park /24"/99]

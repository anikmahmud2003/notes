Q. Discuss the channel of communication.

Answer

Channel of communication: Message can be communicated through some channels. Channels are implied as
“physical bridges" or the media of communication between the sender and the receiver.

Message can be communicated through:
1) Interpersonal communication: This is the most common channel of communication. It is more

persuasive and effective than other ways of communication.

2) Mass media: Mass media such as, Television, radio, newspaper, printed material, direct mailing ,
posters and internet play an important role in communication of message.

3) Folk media: such as keerthan, katha, folk songs, dances and drama, puppet show also convey
messages.

4) Group approaches: such as- lectures, demonstrations, and other discussion methods like-group
discussion and panel discussion, symposium, workshop and conferences also play vital role in

transmitting messages.

Q. Write short note on: Barriers of communication in health education. [SUST-15J]
Q. Write short note on: Communication barrier in a class session. [CU-18N,17M, 16M/J,15J,14Ju]
Answer
Barriers of communication: The barriers should be identified and removed for achieving effective
communication. These can be-

1) Physiological: difficulties in hearing, expression.

2) Psychological: emotional disturbances, neurosis, levels of intelligence, language or comprehension

difficulties.
3) Environmental: noise, invisibility, congestion.

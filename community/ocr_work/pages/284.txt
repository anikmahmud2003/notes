Q. Mention the vectors of malaria in Bangladesh?

Answer
Malarial vector in Bangladesh:
1. A. philippinensis - Plains of rural Bangladesh (common in Bangladesh)
2. A. minimus - In the mountain areas of Bangladesh.
3. A. sundaicus - In the coastal regions of Bangladesh
4. A. dirus (belabacenis) »— Hilly area and forest area of Bangladesh
5. A. culicifacies - Can be found in border area of Sylhet and Dinajpur
6. A. stephenst - In the urban area.
7. A. aconitus
8. A. annularis

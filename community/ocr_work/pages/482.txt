Q. Mention different food toxins.
Answer
Food toxins:

1) BOAA (Beta Oxalyl Aminoalanine): the cause of neurolathyrism found in seeds of the pulses -

Lathyrus sativus (Khesari dhal).

2) Aflatoxin: It is a group of mycotoxin produce by certain fungi- Aspegillus flavus & Aspegillus
parasiticus. This fungus Infests groundnut, maize, wheat rice, cotton seeds under condition of
improper storage. These toxins are the most potent hepatotoxin & carcinogenic. ;

3) Ergot: It is also mycotoxin produced by claviceps fugiformis. These fungi infested during the
flowering stage food grains such as bajra sorghum wheat.

4) Sanguinarine: It is a toxic alkaloid from argemone oil which causes epidemic dropsy.

5) Pyrrolizidine alkaloids: Jhunjhunia seeds where found to contain this toxin which are hepatotoxin

cause endemic ascites characterized by rapidly developing ascites and jaundice.

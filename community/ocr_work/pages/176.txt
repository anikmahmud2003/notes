Q. Mention the agent, host factors and routes of transmission of Rubella (German measles).
Answer ,

Rubella (German measles):

1) Agent factors:
> Agent: An RNA virus of the toga virus family.

> Source of infection: Clinical and sub-clinical cases of rubella.
> Period of communicability: Not exactly known.

2) Host factors:
> Age: 3-1@=years.
> Immunity: One attack results in life-long immunity; second attacks are rare.

3) Routes of transmission:
> Directly from person to person by droplets from nose and throat, and droplet nuclei
(aerosols) via the respiratory route.
> Transplacental (vertical transmission) leading to congenital rubella in the newborn.

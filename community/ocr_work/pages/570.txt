Characteristics / criteria of occupational cancer:
1) They appear after prolonged exposure.
2) The period between exposure and development of the disease may be as long as 10 to 25 years.
3) The disease may develop even after the cessation of the exposure.
4) The average age incidence is earlier than that for cancer in general.
5) The localization of the tumor is remarkably constant in any one occupation.

Elements / components of reproductive health:

1) Safe motherhood.

5) Menstrual regulation (MR) and prevention of unsafe abortion.
6) Infertility.

Q. Write short note on: EOC. [CU-12Ju]

Answer

Emergency obstetric care / EOC: EOC is important life-saving intervention in obstetric complication.
OGSB (Obstetrical & Gynaecological Society of Bangladesh) in collaboration of DGHS (Director

General of Health Service) has been implementing an UNICEF supported project on EOC in BD since July’
1994,

Elements of EOC:
1) Functions used to define first aid:
> Administer parenteral oxitocic drugs to control haemorrhage.

> Administer parenteral antibiotic to control infection.
» Administer parenteral sedatives to control eclamptic convulsion.

2) Functions used to define basic EOC:
> All functions including in obstetric first aid.

> Manual removal of placenta.
> Perform assisted vaginal delivery (vacuum extractor/ forceps).

3) Functions used to define comprehensive EOC:

> All of those included basic EOC.
> Surgery (caesarian section / DE & C).
> Blood transfusion.

Measures taken to reduce MMR through EOC:

1. By awareness about danger signs.
2, Emergency preparedness i.e. decide accurately & timely where to go and arranging transport and fund

by community participation
3. Responsiveness facilities i.e. availability of trained personnel, necessary drugs, equipments & services:

+ Prevention & control of syphilis in Bangladesh:

> Mechanical — condom, diaphragm

> Chemical — intravaginal jellies and creams
> Genital wash after intercourse.

> People should be educated about dangers of syphilis.
> Advice regarding nutrition, sanitation and personal hygiene.

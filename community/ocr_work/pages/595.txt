Q. Describe histogram.
Answer
Histogram:
It is a pictorial diagram of frequency distribution. It is a special form of bar chart of frequency
distribution that represents classes of continuous data.
Criteria:
1) Consist of a series of blocks.
2) The class intervals are given along the horizontal axis.
3) Thee frequencies are given along the vertical axis.
4) The area of each block or rectangle is proportional to the frequency.

Q. How pie chart is constructed?

Q. Describe one of the graphical methods used for presenting continuous data. [RU-1 Ju]
Q. Discuss pie diagram. [SUST-12J]

Answer

Pie chart / sector diagram:

A pie chart is a system of presentation of data in a circle subdivided into parts to reveal the various
components of the data.

A circle has 360 degrees at the centre which are divided into sectors in proportion to the components of
the data. Sectors are coloured or shaded differently. This permits comparison between the different components
of the same phenomenon.

Indoor service:

Administrative:

> Issuing of medicolegal certificates after proper requisition from local police station.

Other:

> Attending at national level programs (e.g. NID, nutrition & different awareness program)

> Prompt response at disease outbreaks, national emergencies, & natural calamities.

dengu, chikungunya, nipah virus infection etc.

> Attending & updating by different training programs.

> Take part in effective coordination with other government departments including upazilla
chairman, MP.

Carbohydrate

Q. Define carbohydrate. Classify carbohydrate.
Q. What are the source and functions of carbohydrate?
Answer
Carbohydrate:
Carbohydrates are composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen. They are the cheapest and main source
of energy. The carbohydrate reserve (glycogen) of a human adult is about 500 gm.

Classification of carbohydrate:

1) Monosaccharide: Glucose.

2) Disaccharides: Cane or beat sugar ( sources), milk (lactose)

3) Polysaccharides: Starch, glycogen, and starch, plant gums and mucilage,
Sources of carbohydrate: There are 3 main sources of carbohydrates —

> Starches: Cereals, roots, and tubers plant stems.

> Sugars: Monosaccharides (glucose, fructose and galactose) and disaccharides (sucrose, lactose and
maltose),

» Cellulose: Contributes to dietary fibre.
Functions of carbohydrate:

Provides main source of energy.

Helps the body to use protein and fat efficiently.

Supplies fiber that helps in the formation of the bulk for better digestion,
Can convert itself into fat.

Essential for the synthesis of certain nonessential amino acids,

Epidemiological approach

Q. What are the epidemiological approaches to problem of health? [RU-16M]
Q. Write the appropriate epidemiological approaches. [RU-13J]
Answer
‘ Epidemiological approach: The epidemiological approach of health and disease two major foundations —
\ - Asking questions.
- Making comparison.

** Asking questions:
Related to health events:
What is the event? (the problem)
What is the magnitude?
Where did it happen?
When did it happen?
Who are affected?
Why did it happen?
Related to health action:

> What can be done to reduce this problem and its consequences?
How can it be prevented in the future?

What action should be taken by the community? By the health services? By other sectors?
Where and for whom these activities be carried out?

What resources are required? How are the activities to be organized?
What difficulties may arise, and how might they be overcome?

“+ Making comparisons: The comparison may be between two groups- one group having the disease and
another group not having the disease.

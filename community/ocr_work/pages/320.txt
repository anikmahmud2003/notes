Q. What are the sources of demography?
Answer

Sources of demography:
1) Decennial census (major source).
2) National Sample Survey and Vital Registration of Bureau of Statistics.
3) Small Sample Surveys conducted by population Development and Evaluation Unit (PDEU) of
Planning Commission.
4) Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies (BIDS).
5) Demographic surveillance area of ICDDR,B.
6) Demographic and Health Survey (DHS), NIPORT.

7) Bangladesh Fertility survey, NIPORT.

Q: Discuss the important epidemiological features of tetanus.
Answer

Epidemiological features of tetanus:
° Occurrence: Tetanus is the worldwide in distribution; rare in developed countries. Still a problem in
developing countries.

“ Epidemiological triad:
1) Agents factors: Cl. tetani isa gram-positive, anaerobic, spore forming organism.
2) Host factors:
> Age: 5 to 40 yrs.
> Sex: higher incidence in male, female are more exposed to the risk of tetanus during
delivery & abortion.
> Occupation: Agricultural workers are special risk of tetanus because of contact with
soil.
> Rural urban differences: The incidence of tetanus is much lowering in urban than
tural areas,
> Immunity: No age is immune unless protected by previous immunization.

3) Environment factors:
> The occurrence of tetanus depends upon man’s physical & ecological surrounding the
soil, agriculture, animal husbandry.
> Unhygienic customs & habits.
> Unhygienic delivery practice.

«* Natural history:
1) Reservoir of infection: Infected animal intestine (cattle, goats, and sheep)

2) Source of infection: Soil, dust, earth, human & animal excreta, contaminated articles

3) Carrier: Air and dust

4) Mode of transmission: Infection by contamination of wound with tetanus spores, trival
pinprick, skin abrasion, puncture wound, burns, unsterile surgery, human bite, animal bite.

5) Incubation period: The incubation period is usually 6 to 10 days. It may be are short as one

day & as long as several years.

Q. List the causes of malnutrition?

Q. What are the causes of malnutrition in Bangladesh?

Answer

Causes of malnutrition:

Primary malnutrition:

i. Lack of food or means to have access to food.
ii, Lack of elementary knowledge of food and nutrition
iii, Refusal to take food
iv. Fad foods.
v. Lack of teeth or inability to chew.

Secondary malnutrition: (Due to some difficulty or alteration in the digestion, absorption and

assimilation) ;
i. Disorders of the GIT.

ii. Protracted immobilization
iii. Fever

iv. Surgery

v. Drug therapy.

Use of fluoride: It inhibits the development of dental caries. But excess fluoride intake may cause

dental fluorosis.
Diet: Restriction of dietary sugar is preventive of dental caries.
Oral cleaning: Tooth cleaning and brushing with a fluoride toothpaste is effective in preventing dental

caries.
Smoking cessation: Cessation of smoking and tobacco chewing eliminates the increased risk of oral

cancer within 5 to 10 years.
Prevention of accidents: Several strategies (e.g. use of mouth guards in some sports) can be used to

reduce trauma to teeth, mandible and cranial cavity.

Ensuring access to appropriate preventive care: Dental services must be available accessible,

affordable, acceptable and accommodating.

Q. Why MDG was proposed? [SUST-15J]

Answer
The Millennium Development Goals / MDGs: ; ; 7
In September 2000, representatives from 189 countries met at the Millennium summit in New York to

adopt the United Nations Millennium Declaration. The leaders made specific commitments in following areas:
1) Peace, security & disarmament;
2) Development and poverty eradication;
3) Protecting our common environment, human rights, democracy and good governance,
4) Protecting the vulnerable;
5) Meeting the special needs of Africa;
6) And strengthening the United Nations.

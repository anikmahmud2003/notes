Health for All and Its Indicators

Q. What is health for all (HFA)? [DU-16N]

Q. List the global indicators of HFA.

Q. Mention the indicators for monitoring and evaluating HFA.
Answer

HFA by 2000 AD:
World health assembly resolved in the year 1977, defined health for all as “attainment of a level of

health that will enable every individual to lead a socially and economically productive life”.
134 member states and 67-organization members decided in Alma-Ata, the capital of Kazakhstan in

1978 that primary health care is the key to achieve the goal of health for all by 2000 AD.

Global indicators for HFA by 2000AD:
1) Endorsement of HFA strategy as a policy at the highest political level.

2) At least 5% of GNP is spent on health.
3) Equitable distribution of health resources.
4) Availability of primary health care to the entire population with at least the followings:
> Safe water at home or within 15 minutes walking distance and adequate sanitation.
> Immunization against EPI 6 diseases
> Availability of at least 20 essential drugs within an hour trav
and childbirth.
5) Reduction of infant mortality rates (IMR) from the level 125 to 50.
6) Life expectancy at birth is over 60 years.
7) Adult literacy rate for both sexes exceeds 70%.
8) The GNP per capital exceeds US$ 500.
9) Atleast 90% of newborn have birth weight of at least 2500 gm.

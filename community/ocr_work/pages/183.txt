Q. State the epidemiological features of rabies. [RU-10J]
Q. Give outline of epidemiology of rabies.
Answer
Epidemiological features of rabies:
“ Occurrence: It occurs throughout the world except in developed countries. Rabies is endemic in
Bangladesh.
“ Epidemiological triad:
> Agent: Lyssavirus type-I (rabies virus), family- rhabdoviridiae.
> Host: Rabies virus can infect all warm blooded mammals. Man is infected accidentally.
> Environment: It may occur at any time but is more prevalent in the summer months.

“* Natural history:
1) Reservoir: stray and pet dogs, cats, fox, wolf, jackal, hyena etc.

2) Source of infection to man: Saliva of the rabid animals. .
3) Infectious period: In dogs and cats, 2 to 5 days before onset of signs and symptoms and during
the course of the illness.

4) Mode of transmission:

Y Animal bites: By rabid dog bites. .

Y Licks: Licks on abraded skin and scratches on the mucosa can also transmit the

disease.
Y Non-bite route: Air borne transmission by oral and milk borne route is unusual.

5) Incubation period: Commonly 1 —3 months, but may vary from 7 days to many years. ;
6) Period of communicability: In dogs and cats, 3 to 5 days before onset of symptoms and signs,
and during the course of the disease. Bats shed virus during the course of the disease.

Concept of Disease

Q. Define disease. [CU-17N, 14Ju]
Answer
Disease:

The Oxford Dictionary English Dictionary defines disease as “a condition of the body or some part
or organ of the body in which its functions are disrupted or deranged”.

From an ecological point of view, disease is defined as ‘maladjustment of the human organism to
the environment’.

Q. Define illness & sickness. [RU-18N]

Answer
Illness:

It refers not only to the presence of a specific disease, but also to the individual's perceptions &
behavior in response to the disease, as well as the impact of that disease on the psychosocial environment.

According to Susser: Illness is a Subjective state of the person who feels aware of not being well.

Sickness: Refers to a state of social dysfunction,

According to Susser: Sickness is a state of social dysfunction i.e. a role that the individual assumes when

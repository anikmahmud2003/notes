* Key elements in child survival programme:
The UNICEF has put forward a set of 7 strategies for child health ‘revolution’ popularly known

as GOBIFFF.
G = Growth monitoring (by using growth chart)
O = Oral Rehydration (to treat all mild and moderate dehydration)
B = Breast feeding to nourish and protect the young infant from infection.
I = Immunization (against 6 communicable diseases)
F = Female education.
F = Food supplementation
F = Family spacing/ planning.
Previously the campaign was limited within the Ist 4 strategies and it was known as 4

* Baby friendly hospital initiative (BFHI): :
nurses, health

In 1993 UNICEF & WHO launched the baby friendly initiative annalist doctor,
worker & parent's in hospital & maternity centers to promote, protect & support breast feedings.

Objectives: To reestablish the superiority of breast feeding in order to protect the health by becoming
friendly to the baby.

WHO & UNICEF criteria: 10 steps which the hospital must fulfill —

1) Helping the mother initiate breast feeding within the first hour of birth in normal delivery
and four hours following caesarean section.

2) Encourage breast feeding on demand.

3) Allow mothers and infants to remain together 24 hours a day, except for medical reasons.

4) Give newborn infants no food or drink, other than breast milk unless medically indicated.

5) Exclusive breast feeding should be promoted till 4 — 6 months of age.

6) No advertisement, promotional material or free products for infant feeding should be
allowed in the facility.

7) Show mother how to breast feed and how to maintain lactation even if she is separated
from her infant.

8) Give no artificial teats or pacifiers (also called dummies or soothers) to breast feeding
infants.

9) Encourage mothers to assists each other and to develop breast feeding support groups.

10) Nursing staff should be available to counsel families and mother in support of exclusive
breast feeding.

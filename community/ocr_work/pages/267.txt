Risk factors of Ca breast:

1) Age: The incidence increasing rapidly between the ages of 35 and 50,

2) Family history: The risk is high in those with a Positive family history of breast cancer.

3) Parity: Those whose first pregnancy is delayed to their late thirties are at a high risk than

4) Age at menarche and menopause: Early menarche and late menopause are established risk

5) Hormonal factors: Both elevated estrogen as well as Progesterone are important factors in
increasing breast cancer risk.

6) Prior breast biopsy.

7) Diet: Cancer of the breast is linked with a high fat diet and obesity,

8) Socio-economic status: Breast cancer is common in higher socio-

economic groups.

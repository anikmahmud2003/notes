Uses of disease cycle:
1) It is important to learn that from the last stage of incubation period to the first few days of

convalescent period a patient is infective to others.

2) By knowing the incubation period we can trace the source of infection and preventive way of a
disease.

3) Specific measures can be taken at different stages of a disease process.

Q. What is disease spectrum?
Q. Explain infectious disease spectrum.
Answer
Disease spectrum: It is a graphic representation of variation in the manifestation of disease. It is as follows —
Y The different manifestations are simply reflections of individual different states of immunity and
receptivity.
Y Leprosy is an excellent example of the spectral concept of disease. For almost every disease, there
exists a spectrum, with few exceptions such as rabies.
Y Ininfectious diseases, the spectrum of disease is also referred to as the gradient of infection.
Y The spectrum of disease can be interrupted by early diagnosis and treatment or by preventive

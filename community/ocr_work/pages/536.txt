Q. Discuss the advantages & disadvantages of water seal latrine at rural area.
Q. What are the advantages of water seal latrine? [CU-1 |Ju]
Answer
Advantages of water-seal latrine: The hand flushed water seal type of sanitary latrines are ideal ones for rural
areas. It is an ideal latrine for rural areas, because —

1) It prevents access of flies, rodents and insects due to presence of water in the trap.

2) It prevents escape of odours and foul gases and thereby eliminate the nuisance of bad smell.

3) Once the latrine is properly flushed an excretes is no longer visible.

4) It does not contaminate ground or surface water.

5) It is economical in construction as it is provided at subsidized rate.

[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 4" / 499]

Demerits:

1) It is somehow costly for the poor peoples.

2) In many places, it is difficult to dig a hole deeper than 3 m.

3) Imperfect use due to lack of knowledge about its use.

Q. Write short note on: Septic tank.
Answer
Septic tank: It is a water-tight tank into which household sewage is admitted for treatment.
Design feature:
1) Capacity: Minimum capacity of a septic tank should ne at least 500 gallons.
2) Length: It is usually twice the breadth.
3) Depth: From 1.5 to 2 m.
4) Liquid depth: The recommended liquid depth is only 1.2 m.
5) Air space: There should be minimum air space of 30 cm.
6) Bottom: It should b sloping towards the inlet end. It facilitates retention of solid.
7) Inlet and outlet: There is an inlet and outlet pipe, which are submerged.
8) Cover: The septic tank should be covéred by a concrete slab of suitable thickness and provides

with a manhole.
9) Retention period: Septic tanks are designed to allow a retention period of 24 hours.

Working of a septic tank:
The stages are involved in the purification of sewage. The first stage, anaerobic digestion takes place in

the septic tank proper and the second stage, aerobic oxidation takes place outside the septic tank, in the sub-soil.
Together, these two stages complete the purification of sewage.

Operation and maintenance:
1) Use of soap water and disinfectants, such as phenol should be avoided.

2) Undue accumulation of sludge should be removed periodically.
3) Newly constructed septic tank needs to be seeded with sludge from another septic tank in use.

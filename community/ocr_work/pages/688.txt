Adult culex mosquito

Identification:
This is a model of adult culex mosquito.

Identification points: |
. When at rest, it lies parallel to the surface, with a hunchback position. ,
¢ Wings are not spotted.
¢  Palpi are shorter than proboscis in females.

Public health importance:
¢ Female culex mosquito is the vector for filarial parasite and for a virus causing encephalitis.
¢ A good number of people suffer from filariasis each year in our country and a few suffer from
encephalitis.
¢ Mosquito control measures must be there to protect from filariasis and encephalitis.

Egg of culex mosquito

Identification:
This is a model of egg of culex mosquito.

Identification points: |
e Eggs are laid in groups or clusters. |

|
e Oval in shape. |
e Not provided with lateral floats. | |

Public health importance:
© Culex mosquitoes lay eggs on the surface of dirty or polluted water.

¢ Breeding places must be eliminated out, otherwise eggs will undergo developmental stages to 1
become adults and will act as vectors for filarial parasites and for a virus causing encephalitis.

Larva of culex mosquito

Identification:
This is a model of larva of culex mosquito.

Identification points:
e Elongated in shape.
e Remains suspended with head downwards, making an angle to the water surface.
e Siphon tube present.
e Palmate hairs absent in abdominal segments.

Public health importance:

e  Anti-larval measures must be taken to control filariasis and encephalitis.
e If measures not taken, larva will undergo developmental stages to become adult and will act as
a vector for filarial parasites and for virus causing encephalitis.

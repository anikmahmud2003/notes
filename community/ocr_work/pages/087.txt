A What is cohort? [CU-14J, RU-12Ju]
5 Answer

Cohort: In epidemiology, the term “cohort” is defined as a group of people who share a common characteristic
or experience within a defined time period (e.g. age, occupation, exposure to a drug or vaccine, pregnancy,
insured persons etc.).

Example:
> A group of people born on the same day or in the same period of time (usually a year) form a birth

cohort. .
> A group of males or females married on the same day or in the same period of time form a
marriage cohort.
> Persons exposed to a common drug, vaccine or infection within a defined period constitutes an
exposure cohort.

Cohort study: This study proceeds from “cause to effect”. Cohort study has following synonyms-
Y Prospective study,
Y Longitudinal study,
Y Incidence study,

Distinguishing / characteristic features of cohort studies:

2) Determination or study time,
3) The study proceeds forward from cause to effect.

Hardness of water

Q. Define hardness of water. Classify it.
Q. How can you remove the hardness of water?
Answer

Hardness of water: It is defined as the soap-destroying power of water.

Types: It is of 2 types-
1) Temporary hardness: It is due to the presence of bicarbonates of calcium or magnesium in water
held in solution by carbon dioxide.

2) Permanent hardness: This is due to the presence of sulphates and chlorides of calcium or
magnesium.

Hardness in water is expressed in terms of milli-equivalents per liter. One mEq/Itr of
hardness producing ion is equal to 50 mg calcium carbonate in | liter of water. The terms soft and
hard water are used when the levels of hardness are given as follow:

Very hard water > 6 (> 300 mg/ltr )

Removal of hardness: The methods of removal of hardness are briefly stated as follows —

Boiling > Addition of sodium carbonate
Addition of lime > Base exchange process.

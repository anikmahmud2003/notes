Q. Discuss the epidemiology of poliomyelitis.
Answer
* Occurrence: Poliomyelitis is endemic all over the world including Bangladesh
1) Agent: Poliovirus. (It is an RNA enterovirus)
2) Host: ;
> Age: Poliomyelitis is a disease of childhood and infancy
> Sex: Male: Female = 3:1 . .
> Immunity: Infants born of immune mothers escape infection upto 6 months, Immunity
induced by natural infection is permanent and type-specific.
> Risk factors:
Y Trauma
v Fatigue
Y Intramuscular injection
Y Operative procedures such as tonsillectomy.

3) Environment: . .
> Season: It occurs more in rainy season, particularly during June to September
> Insanitary conditions: Contaminated water, food and flies and overcrowding are
important contributory factors.

“+ Natural history:
1) Reservoir:
> Cases (patients)
> Carriers

2) Source of infection:
> Feces
> Oropharyngeal secretion

3) Infectious period:
> Cases are most infectious 7 to 10 days before and after onset of symptoms

> The virus is excreted in the feces commonly for 2 to 3 weeks, sometimes as long as 3 to
4 months.

4) Mode of transmission:
> Fecal oral route the main route of infection in the developing world.

> Droplet infection is more important during epidemic and where sanitation is good (in
developed world).

5) Incubation period: 7-14 days; maximum range 3-35 days.

Q. What do you mean by executive body of WHO?
Answer
Executive body of WHO:

It had originally 18 members, each designed by a Member State. Subsequently, the number was raised
to 24 & 30. The Health Assembly increased the membership from 30 to 31, providing that no fewer than 3 are
to be elected from each of the WHO regions. The members of the body are to be “technically qualified in the
field of health", they are designed by, but do not represent their governments. One-third of the membership is
renewed every year. The executive body meets at least twice a year, generally in January and shortly after the
meeting of the World Health Assembly in May.

UNICEF:
UNICEF is one of the specialized agencies of the United Nations. It was established in 1946 by the
United Nations General Assembly to deal with rehabilitation of the children in war ravaged countries. In 1953,
when the emergency functions were over, the General Assembly gave it a new name, “UN Children Fund” but
retained the initials.

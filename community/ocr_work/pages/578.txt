Slaughter house

Q. What are the criteria of good slaughter house?
Answer

Criteria of good slaughter house are:
¢ away from human habitation.

1) It should be situated on a high land outside the town an
2) The floor should be cemented having proper slope.
3) It should have good drainage system.

4) It should have a high boundary wall.
5) Provision should exist for proper disposal of skin, bones, intestines, blood etc.

6) Water supply should be adequate.
7) Screening of doors and windows should be provided.

8) It should not be used for sleeping.

Badge of the trade

Q. What do you mean by “badge of the trade”?
Answer

Badge of the trade:

The trauma of a particular occupation often gives rise to characteristic stigma “badge of the trade”. For
example; discoloured nails of photographers and hairdressers, white fingers due to traumatic vasospasm in
workers handling vibration tools, metallic tattooing as a result of explosives.

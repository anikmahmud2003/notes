Q. Write down the screening and prevention of breast cancer.
Answer
Screening for breast cancer: The basic techniques for early detection of breast cancer are-
> Breast self-examination (BSE) by the patient.
> Palpation by a physician.
> Thermography.
> Mammography.

Prevention of breast cancer:

A) Primary prevention:
1) Elimination of risk factors.
2) Promotion of cancer education.
3) Reduction of childhood obesity.
4) Strenuous physical activity.
5) Reduction of fat intake in the diet.

B) Secondary prevention:
1) Early diagnosis,
2) Treatment,
3) Follow-up, ie.
> To detect recurrence as early as possible.
> To detect cancer in the opposite breast at an early stage.
> To generate research data that might be useful.

Carcinoma of lung / Bronchogenic carcinoma

Q. Mention the risk factors of Ca lung. Mention screening and prevention of it.

Q-Prevalence of Ca lung is increasing in Bangladesh, why? Explain.

Q. High prevalence of Ca lung is a problem for Bangladesh. What preventive measures you
suggest?

Answer

Risk factors of carcinoma of lung / bronchogenic carcinoma:
1) Smoking: Responsible for 90% of lung cancer death.

2) Other factors:
e Air pollution,
¢ Radioactivity.
® Occupational exposure to asbestos, arsenic and its compounds, chromates etc.

Screening for lung cancer:
1) Chest radiograph.

2) Sputum cytology.

Prevention of lung cancer:
1) Primary prevention: To control the smoking epidemic by-

¢ Public information and education.

e Legislative and restrictive measures.

¢ Smoking cessation activities.

e National and international coordination.

2) Secondary prevention:
e Early detection of cases.
e Treatment.

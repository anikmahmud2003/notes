Viva. 2. Write down the causes of accidents in water transport.
Causes of accidents in water transport:

1) Natural disasters i.e. cyclone, tornado, tidal wave.

2) Defective vehicle.

3) Overloading of public transport.

4) Poor maintenance of vehicle.

5) Low driving skill (unskilled driver).

6) Lack of knowledge about current weather.

Viva Q. Write down the preventive measures of accidents in water transport.
nswer
Preventive measures of accidents in water transport:
1) Disaster preparedness by listening weather news.
2) Strengthening of body works of vehicles.
3) Engine test of the vehicle before start.
4) Avoid overloading.
5) Improvement of skillness of driver by proper training.
6) Reduction of speed capability of vehicle.
7) Raising awareness among the people.
8) Legislation.
9) Maintenance of traffic rule.

Q. Write short note on: PKDL.
Answer
PKDL: PKDL means Post Kala-azar Dermal leishmaniasis, caused by L. donovani.
Hypo-pigmented areas on skin without loss of sensation, and there may be associated erythematous .
patches, subcutaneous nodules, history of fever and also suspicion based on place, period and population.

Laboratory diagnosis:
> Aldehyde test

> ICT for RK-39 antigen
> Direct agglutination test (DAT)
> Slit skin smear test for parasite
> Punch biopsy for parasite
Treatment:
> Injection sodium antimony gluconate (SAG). Dose —> 20 mg/kg body weight for 20 days, then 10
days gap, in this way 6 cycles (total 120 injections).

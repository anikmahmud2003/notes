Q. Name five occupational groups who are at risk of occupational cancer.
Q. Name some industries where skin cancer, lung cancer and bladder cancer are more

prevalent.
Answer
Occupational groups who are at risk of occupational cancer:

1) Skin cancer: Gas workers, coke woven workers, tar distillers, oil refiners, dye-staff makers, road

makers.
2) Lung cancer: Gas industry, asbestos industry, nickel and chromium work, arsenic roasting plants and
, in the mining of radio-active substances (e.g. uranium).
3) Bladder cancer: Aniline industry workers, dye-stuffs, rubber, gas and electric cable industry workers,

4) Leukaemia: Radiation worker.
[Ref- Park / 24" / 845]

Radiation hazards

1) Ionizing radiation may cause acute burns, dermatitis and blood dyscrasias.

2) Chronic radiation exposure may cause malignancies and genetic defects.

3) Lung cancer may develop in miners working in uranium mines due to inhalation of radio-active dust.

Q. Discuss the doctor-patient relationship. [SUST-18M,16M]

Answer
Doctor-patient relationship:

The doctor-patient relationship is central to the practice of healthcare and is essential for the delivery
of high-quality health care in the diagnosis and treatment of disease. The doctor-patient relationship forms one
of the foundations of contemporary medical ethics. For most physicians, the establishment of good rapport with
a patient is important. Some medical specialties, such as psychiatry and family medicine, emphasize the
physician-patient relationship more than others, such as pathology or radiology.

Role of a doctor in developing doctor-patient relationship: .
1) A doctor must always keep in mind the importance of preserving human life from the day of

conception until death.
2) When some examinations and treatments are beyond his capacity, he should summon another

doctor who has the necessary ability.
3) A doctor should maintain absolute professional secrecy of the patient. ; ;
4) A doctor must give necessary treatment in emergency circumstances, unless he is certain that it can

be given by others.
Role of a patient in developing doctor-patient relationship:
1) A patient must have confidence in the competence of their physician and must feel that they can

confide in him or her. shan:
He should furnish the doctor with complete information about past illness, family history of

diseases and the facts & circumstances of his illness.
3) He should strictly follow the instructions of the doctor as regards diet, medicine, mode of life etc.

4) He should pay a reasonable fee to the doctor.

Q. Why doctor-patient relationship is not satisfactory in our country?

Answer
The doctor-patient relationship is not satisfactory in our country due to:

1) Uncooperative attitude of the patient.
2) Differential treatment between the rich and poor.
3) Lack of a sympathetic and friendly attitude of the doctor.

4) The doctor’s greed for money.

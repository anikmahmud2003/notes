Q. Write short note on: Primary case. [CU-17N,16N]
Answer
Case:

A case is defined as a person in the population or study group identified as having the particular
disease, health disorders or condition under investigation.

Types of cases: :
1) Clinical cases: It may mild to moderate, typical or atypical, severe or fatal depending upon the

gradient of involvement. Epidemiologically mild cases are more important sources of infection than
severe cases because they are ambulant and spread the infection whenever they go, whenever

severe cases are usually confined to bed.

2) Sub-clinical cases: They are variously referred to as inapparent, covert, missed or abortive cases.
The disease agent may multiply in the host, but does not manifest itself signs and symptoms, e.g.
rubella, mumps, polio, influenza, diphtheria, but measles has no sub-clinical infection. Sub clinical .
infection can be detected only by laboratory test, e.g. antibody response, biochemical & skin
sensitivity test.

3) Latent infection: In latent infection, the host does not shed the infectious agent which lies dormant
within the host- without symptoms. For example latent infection in herpes simplex.

In epidemiological terminology:
1) Primary case: It refers to the first case 0

unit being studied.
2) Index case: It refers to the first case to come to the attention of the investigator.

3) Secondary case: Secondary cases are those developing from contact with primary case.

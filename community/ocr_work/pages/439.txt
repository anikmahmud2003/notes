426 —- ENDEAVOUR COMMUNITY.

Food, nutrition, nutrients & diet

Q. Define food. [CU-10Ju/J, RU-I Ju]
Q. What are the functions of food?

Answer
Food: . .
Food is a composite mixture of substances which when consumed performs certain functions in the
body.
These functions are —

i) Yielding energy

ii) Building and maintaining the body.

iii) Protecting the body & regulating the tissue function.

[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 5” / 136]
Q. Classify food. [DU-17M, CU-10Ju]
Q. Classify food on the basis of predominant functions. [DU-16N, RU-1 1Ju/J, SUST-17N, 13Ju]
Answer
Classification of food:
“ According to chemical composition:

1) Proteins.

2) Fats.

3) Carbohydrate.

4) Vitamins.

5) Minerals.

6) Water

“+ According to predominant function:
> Energy-giving foods: Cereals, sugar, roots & tubers, fats & oils.
> Body-building foods: Meat, milk, liver, fish, pulses etc.
> Protective foods: Vegetables, fruits & milk.

“According to origin:

> Foods of animal origin: Meat, fish etc.

> Foods of vegetable origin: Vegetables, fruits etc.

* According to calorie value:
> Calorigenic foods: Carbohydrate, fat & protein.

> Non-calorigenic foods: Water, vitamins & minerals.

“ According to nutritive value:
1) Cereals & millets.

2) Pulses

3) Vegetable

4) Nuts & oilseeds

5) Fruits.

6) Animal foods.

7) Fats & oils.

8) Sugar & jaggery.

9) Condiments & spices.
10) Miscellaneous foods.

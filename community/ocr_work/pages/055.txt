Q. Write short note on: Host.
Answer

Host:

. A person or other animal including birds and arthropods that afford subsistence or lodgment to an
infectious agent under natural conditions,

Classification of host:

1) Obligate host: means the only host, e.g. man in measles and typhoid fever. .
2) Primary or definitive host: Host-in which the parasite attains maturity or passes its sexual stage.

3) Secondary or intermediate host: Host in which the parasite is in a larval or sexual state.
4) Transport host: It is a carrier in which the organism remains alive but does not undergo
development.

[Ref- Park / 24" /38, 98]
Characteristics of host:
> Demographic characteristics: Age, Sex, Ethnicity.

> Biological characteristics: Genetic factors, biochemical levels of blood (cholesterol), blood
groups, enzymes, blood pressure.

> Social and economic characteristics: socio-economic status, education, occupation, marital status,
housing etc,

> Life style factors: living habits, food habit + colon cancer, exercise —> hypertension, heart
disease. Smoking > lung cancer, alcohol use etc. ‘;
[Ref- Park / 24" /38]

Q. Name environmental factors of disease causation. Discuss any one of them.
Q. Mention the role of biological environment in disease causation. [DU-17M]
Answer

Environmental factors of disease causation:
1) Physical environment: The term physical environment" is applied to non-living things and
physical factors (e.g., air, water, soil etc.) with which man is in constant interaction.

Example:
> Air Air pollution > Health problems,
> Radiation > Radiation hazards —> Health problems,

2) Biological environment: The biological environment is the universe of living things which
surrounds man, including man himself,

Example: Viruses, bacteria, insects, rodents, animals, plants.

3) Psychological environment: It includes a complex of psychological factors which are defined as
‘those factors affecting personal health, health care and community well-being that stem from the

psychological make-up of individuals and the structure and functions of social groups’,

Example: Cultural value, customs, habits, beliefs, attitudes, religion, education, life-style.

[Ref- Park / 24" / 41, 42]

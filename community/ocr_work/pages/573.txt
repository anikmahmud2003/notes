Prevention of accidents in industry: The principles of accident prevention are —
1) Adequate pre-placement examination.
2) Adequate job training.
3) Continuing education.
4) Ensuring safe working environment
5) Establishing a safety department in the organization under a competent safety engineer.
6) Periodic survey for finding out hazards.

7) Careful reporting, maintenance of records & publicity.

Q. What are the health hazards of a cotton mill?

The health hazards of a cotton mill:
1) Byssinosis: The condition is caused by cotton fibre dust over long period of time. The affected
person complains of chronic cough and progressive dyspnoea which may leads to emphysema.
2) COPD: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease is the ultimate result of cotton mill health hazard.

3) Mechanical injury may also take place.
4) Extrinsic allergic pneumonitis is another one which is caused by inhalation of dusts and as a

result of hypersensitivity reaction.
5) Occupational lung cancer may be the most fatal conclusion of cotton mill health hazard.

Q. What is nutrition? [CU-10J]

Answer

Nutrition: Nutrition is the science or practice of consuming and utilizing foods.

In other word, nutrition is the science of foods, the nutrients and other substances in foods, their actions,
interaction and balance in relation to diet, health and diseases.

[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 5" / 136]

Q. Define diet? [CU-10J, RU-09Ju]
Answer
Diet:

A diet may be defined as the kinds of food on which a person or group lives.
[Ref- Park / 23" / 608]

Or,
The food which we are eating and or drinking is called diet.

Dietetics:

Q. Define balanced diet. [RU-17M,16M, SUST-15J]

Q. Explain the importance and composition of balanced diet. [SUST-15J,10Ju]
Q. Write short note on: Balanced diet. [SUST-16M]

Answer

Balanced diet:
A balanced diet is defined as one which contains a variety of foods in such quantities and proportions

that the need for energy, amino acids, vitamins, minerals, fats, carbohydrate and other nutrients is adequately
met for maintaining health, vitality and general well-being and also makes a small provision for extra nutrients

to withstand short duration of leanness.
[Ref- Park / 24" / 675]

Or, A balanced diet is one which contains all the proximate principles (e.g. protein, fat & carbohydrate),
minerals and vitamins in due proportions and at the same time meeting the total energy requirements for

maintaining health, vitality & wellbeing of a person.
[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 4 / 135]

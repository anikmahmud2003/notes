Yellow fever

Yellow Fever:

Yellow fever is a zoonotic disease caused by an arbovirus.

It is a severe infectious and life threatening disease characterized by toxic jaundice, yellow skin, high
fever, severe body ache, nausea, vomiting and albuminuria accompanied by haemorrhages from stomach, nose

and mouth.
[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder / 5" / 255]

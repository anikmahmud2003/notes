Q. Diarrhoeal diseases are common in community. Chalk out a plan to import health education

Q. A diarrhoeal outbreak has been occurred in a slum in Dhaka city. What sorts of health
education are to be given to the slum dwellers to combat the situation? [RU-13Ju]

Answer

Control of diarrhoeal epidemic: A
Epidemic/outbreaks of acute diarrhoea due to specific pathogen cause sharp increase in morbidity and
Mortality. So it becomes necessary to take measures to control an epidemic in the shortest possible time.
National programme for control of diarrhoeal disease can cholera by ensuring the implementation of
strategies described below:
1) Early detection of epidemics: It is done by continuous diarrhoea surveillance.
2) Active case findings: It required the help of the leaders of the community as well as home visit by

health worker. .
3) Provision of early treatment of cases: sufficient supply of packet of ORS, intravenous fluids and

necessary antibiotics must be ensured.
Teaching paramedics and local volunteers to administer ORS solution: For handling larger

4)
number of cases during epidemic.
5) Notification to local and central authorities: Such action will help to obtain urgent support and

co-operation.

6) Disinfection and Disposal of stool, vomit, contaminated clothing: This further reduces the
changes of transmission and quicker control of the outbreak.

7) Health Education: Stress on the importance of hand washing, use of sanitary latrines, safe water,
protection of food and water from contamination and from flies.

8) Provision of safe water and sanitary latrines: It is difficult and costly task.

9) Chemoprophylaxis: Appropriate chemoprophylaxis may prevent developing symptomatic illness
among the close contacts of active cases in the family or in a crowded community like refugee
camp or slum.

10) Epidemiological investigation and report writing.

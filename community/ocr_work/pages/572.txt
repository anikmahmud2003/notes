Q. What are the causes of accident in industry?

Answer
Causes of accident in industry: The causes of accidents are several and may be grouped under 2 headlines —

(A) Human factors:
(i) Physical: The physical factors of the worker may not meet the job requirements; his visual

acuity may be inadequate; his hearing may be inadequate.

(ii) Physiological factors:

a. Sex: Women are known to have more accidents than men.

b. Age: Younger ages are known to be involved more in accidents than older age groups.

c. Time: Accidents are minimum at the beginning of the day and increase gradually as

fatigue sets in.

d. Experience: Experience is an important factor in the occurrence of accidents. The
more the experience the less are the accidents.
Working Hours: An increase in accidents is found whenever the daily or weekly
working hours increase.

e.

(B) Environmental factors: The temperature, poor illumination, humidity, noise and unsafe machines

influence the incidence of accidents.

Mosquito control measures;

A) Anti-larval measures:

1) Environmental control: Elimination of the breeding places (source reduction) of
Mosquitoes is the most important step.
2) Chemical control: .
> Mineral oils: Diesel oil, kerosene, other crude oil, mosquito larvicidal oil (MLO).
> Paris green: It is copper acetoarsenite which should contain 50% arsenous oxide kills
mainly the Anopheles larva.
> Synthetic insecticides: Abate, Malathion, Fenthion, Chloropyrifor.

3) Biological control: Guppy fish can be used in burrow pits sewage oxidation ponds, ornamental
ponds, cisterus and farm pond as guppy fish feed on mosquito larval.

B) Anti-adult measures:
1) Residual sprays: Adult mosquitoes are most effectively controlled by spraying houses with
residual insecticide such as- DDT, Lindane, Malathion, OMAS-33.
2) Space sprays: Insecticide is sprayed into the atmosphere in the form of a mist or fog to kill
Mosquito. The common space sprays- Pyrethrum extract, Malathion, Fenitrothion.
3) Genetic control;
Male sterilization
Chromosomal translocation.
> Sex distortion.
> Gene replacement
> Cytoplasmic incompatibility.

Q. Why organochloride compound is not suitable for larviciding operations? [DU-10J]
Answer
Organochloride compound (e.g. DDT, HCH) is not suitable for larviciding operation, because of:
1) Their long residual effect,
2) Water contamination and

3) Increased risk of developing resistance in the vector mosquitoes.

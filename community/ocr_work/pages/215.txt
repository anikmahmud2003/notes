Answer
Twin pillars of filariasis elimination: Controlling morbidity and interruption of transmission of filariasis is
called the twin pillars of filariasis elimination,
1) Controlling morbidity: Community level care of those with disease —

» Lymphoedema.

> Acute inflammatory attacks.

> Hydrocele repair.

2) Interruption of transmission:

> Research during the past few years has demonstrated that a single 6 mg/kg dose of
diethylcarbamazine (DEC) is virtually as effective in killing adult W. bancrofti and in
producing long-term suppression of microfilaria in the blood as the previously
recommended 12-day course of treatment,

Single-dose ivermectin (400ug/kg) is even more effective than DEC in suppressing
microfilaremia.

> The long-term microfilarial suppression of DEC and ivermectin is enhanced when they are
given together or with albendazole.

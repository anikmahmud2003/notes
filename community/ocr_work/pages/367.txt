Q. Why home visit is called the backbone of all MCH services? [CU-10J]
Answer

Home visit:

Home visiting is the backbone of all MCH services. Most visits are required if the delivery is planned at
home. The mother is generally relaxed at home. The home visit will win her confidence. The home visit will
provide an opportunity to observe the environmental and social conditions at home and also an opportunity to
give prenatal advice.

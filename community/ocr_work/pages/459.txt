Q. Write short note on: Xerophthalmia, [SUST-10J]

Answer

Xerophthalmia: (Means Disease of the dry eye) mae, .
All ocular manifestations ranging from night blindness to keratomalacia due to vitamin-A deficiency

are collectively called Xerophthalmia. ,

Clinical classification / stages of Xerophthalmia: WHO classification-

XIB Bitot’s spot

Predisposing factors:
1) Age: 1-3 years,
2) PEM,
3) Ignorance,
4) Faulty feeding practice,
5) Faulty weaning,
6) Infection, e.g. Diarrhoea, measles.
7) Poverty.

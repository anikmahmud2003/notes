Q. What is acute radiation syndrome?
Answer
Acute radiation syndrome:

_ Exposure of whole body to a large dose of over 100 rems penetrating radiation in a single exposure
within 1 to 2 days, resulting in progressive series of sign & symptoms known as acute radiation syndrome. The
symptoms are —

Y Inthe first phase there is short interval between exposure and onset of symptoms

Y Second phase consists of nausea and vomiting, diarrhea and abdominal pain. There may be
headache, erythema of skin and conjunctivitis.

Y Inthe third phase there may be loss of hair and desquamation of skin.

Y Inthe final stage bone marrow aplasia, thrombocytopenia and agranulocytosis occurs.

Q. How can you protect radio-imaging technologists from radiation hazards? [DU-14Ju]
Answer
Radiation hazard can be prevented by-
A) Preventive measures for internal irradiation:
1) Notification
2) Advance approval for specified activities where preparation is essential.
3) Specification of dose limits & emissions for exposed persons, energy limits for accidents &
incidents.
4) Classification of work places depending on risk and exposed persons.
5) Introduction of control measures and monitoring.
6) Introduction of competent practitioners for the specialized task of monitoring, evaluation and
control.
7) Critical assessment of new plant & projects.
8) Centralization of calibration, dosimetry & records.
9) Medical surveillance of exposed persons.

B) Preventive measures for external irradiation:

1) Avoidance of unnecessary X-ray examinations particularly of children & pregnant mothers.
2) Adequate control & surveillance of x-ray installations.

3) Improvement in technique.

4) Improved leadings for dose reduction shield, distance, time.

5) Effective protective measures, lead apron, rubber apron.

6) Film badge or dosimeter for workers.

7) Periodic medical examination.

8) Regular working hour.

9) Recreation & holidays.

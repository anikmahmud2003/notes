Disability rate

Q. Write short note on: Disability rate.

Q. Write short note on: Sullivan’s index. [CU-15Ju]
Answer

Disability rate: The disability rate falls into two groups.

A) Event type indicators:

1) Number of days of restricted activity.
2) Bed disability days.
3) Work loss days (or school loss days) with in specified period.

B) Person-type indicators:

1) Limitation of mobility:
> Confined to bed.
> Continued to house.

2) Limitation of activity:
> Limitation to perform the basic activities of daily living (ADL) - eating, washing,

dressing etc.

> Limitation in major activities ability to work at a job, ability to house work etc.

Disability free life expectancy (DFLE) / Active life expectancy / Sullivan’s index: Expectation of life free

from disability.
Sullivan’s index = life expectancy — (Duration of bed disability + duration of inability to perform major

activities)

Utilization rates

Q. How the utilization rate influence the health status of a community.

Q. ‘Utilization of healthcare sender reflects people’s health status’-justify it in context of
Bangladesh.

Answer

Utilization of healthcare sender reflects people’s health status:

In order to obtain additional information on health status, the extent of use of health services is often
investigated. Utilization of services or actual coverage is expressed as the proportion of people in need of a
service who actually receive it in a given period, usually a year.

It is argued that utilization rate gives some indication of the care needed by a population, and therefore,
the health status of the population. In other words, a relationship exists between utilization of healthcare
services and health needs and status.

Healthcare utilization is also affected by factors such as availability and accessibility of health services
and attitude of an individual towards his health and healthcare system. A few examples of utilization rates are
given below in context of Bangladesh —

1) Proportion of infants who are "fully immunized” against the 6 EPI diseases (at present 8 EPI
diseases in Bangladesh).

2) Proportion of pregnant woman who receive antenatal care or have their deliveries supervised by
a trained birth attendant.

Q. Enumerate the sources of air pollution in rural & urban areas / Chittagong [CU-18M] /
Dhaka city [CU-16J].

Answer

Sources of air olution: Air pollution can be caused by a variety of substances and processes:

1) Pollution by natural phenomenon, e.g. volcanoes, forest fires, radioactive substances in the
nature, bacteria, viruses, moulds and spores.

2) Pollution by products of combustion: Combustion of fuel produces smoke, sulphur di-oxide,
nitrogen oxide and fly ashes.

3) Industrial waste e.g. dust, fume and other wastes from factories, mills, power plants, mines,
cement plants pollute the air.

4) Automobiles: Internal combustion engine, spark ignition, gasoline engine, diesel engine contribute
to air pollution by emitting hydrocarbon, carbon mono-oxide, lead, nitrogen oxide, smokes and
aldehyde.

5) Photo-chemical pollution: Volatile hydrocarbon and oxides of nitrogen from vehicle engines in
presence of strong sun light produce ozone and many complex organic compounds.

6) Acid rain (sulphuric and nitric acids).

7) Miscellaneous: Burning of refuse, agricultural activities contribute to some extent to air pollution.

Q. Discuss about personality traits.
Answer
Personality traits:

A trait is described as tendency to behave in a consistent manner in variable situations.

Human personality is a bundle of traits. The basic personality traits are established by the age of 6
years. Cheerfulness, good manners, sportsmanship, honesty, kindliness, loyalty, reliability, sense of humor,
tactfulness, willing to help others etc. are some of the personality traits.

It is possible to cultivate these traits.

Q. Discuss adult intelligence.
Answer
Adult intelligence: The components of adult intelligence are —
1) Space: The ability to perceive objects.
2) Number: Familiarity with elementary arithmetic.
3) Verbal comprehension: The ability to reason from verbal concepts.
4) Facility of expression: The ability to employ the appropriate words.
5) Memory: The ability to retain words and ideas.
6) Induction: The ability to discover principles.
7) Deduction: The ability to use those principles to solve concrete problems.

8) Flexibility and quickness of thought.

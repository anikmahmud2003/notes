Isolation of the patient

Q. Define isolation. [SUST-16N]

Q. Write short note on: Isolation. [SUST-18M]

Answer

Isolation:

Y Isolation of the patient is the oldest communicable disease control measure.

Y Itis defined as “separation, for the period of communicability, of infected persons or animals from
others in such places and under such conditions, as to prevent or limit the direct or indirect
transmission of the infectious agent from those infected to those who are susceptible or who may

spread the agent to others”.

Purpose / importance of isolation:
1) To protect the community by preventing transfer of infection from the reservoir to the possible

susceptible hosts.
2) Isolation has a distinctive value in the control of some infectious diseases; e.g. Diphtheria, Cholera,

Streptococcal respiratory diseases & Pneumonic plague.

Types of isolation:
1) Standard isolation.
2) Strict isolation.
3) Protective isolation.
4) High security isolation.

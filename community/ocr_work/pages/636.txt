: Perception: The word perception implies observation, recognition and di

Errors in perception:
1) Imperception: That is, inability to recognize. This may be due to damage to the sense

organs, e.g. anesthesia.
2) Ilusion: An illusion is a false perception. Mistaking a rope for a snake, a tree for an animal
are called illusions. Illusions occur in mental diseases. Illusions may be auditory or visual.
3) Hallucination: Hallucination is an imaginary perception. Itis a gross error of perception.
Seeing objects that do not exist, hearing sounds that are false, seeing objects moving in a
room are called hallucinations. Hallucinations occur in mental disorders. ,

Learning:

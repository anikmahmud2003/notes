Q. Write short note on: Scabies. [SUST-16M]
Answer
Scabies:

Definition: ; :

Scabies is a highly contagious infectious disease of the skin caused by the itch mite (female sarcoptes
scabiei hominis) characterized by papulo-vesicular eruption along the burrows containing the mites and their
eggs with intense itching more at night.

Causative agent: Female Sarcoptes scabiei hominis.

Site of lesion:
- Hand (Finger webs) and Wrists- 63%
- Extensor aspect of elbow and axillae
- Genitals of male & female
- Thighs and abdomen

a) Intense itching at night.

b) More than one member in the family has affected in adults.

c) Face and scalp are not usually affected in adults.

d) Itching of nipples in female and itching papules in males of penis or scrotum.

Modes of spread of scabies:
i) Close contact

ii) Contaminated cloth

Complication of scabies:

i) Glomerulonephritis.
ii) Rheumatic fever.
ili) Secondary infection.
iv) Urticaria.

v) Eczema.

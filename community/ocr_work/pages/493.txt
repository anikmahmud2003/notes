Q. Enlist the sources of water pollution. [DU-17N, SUST-18N, | TN, 15J,14J, 1 1Ju,10Ju, CU-16N]
Q. Mention the sources of water pollution of the river Buriganga. [DU-14Ju]
Answer
Sources of impurities of water / water pollution:
1) Substances received at sources: It depends upon the nature of soil, water of chalky soil contains
excess of calcium; in a densely populated town, water contains calcium, sodium, nitrates, nitrites
phosphates etc.

2) Impurities obtained during transit from source to reservoir: Rivers, canals, etc. are liable to be

polluted by sewage, wastewater and industrial sources, etc.

3) Impurities during storage; e.g. from containers.
4) Impurities during distribution; e.g. from distribution pipes or from distributors etc.

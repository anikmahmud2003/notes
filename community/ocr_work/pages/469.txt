Fluorine

Q. Mention the sources of fluorine. Why it is called two-edged sword?
Answer
Sources of fluorine:
1) Drinking water: Major source of fluorine to man.
2) Foods: Fluorides occur in traces in many foods, but some foods such as sea fish, cheese and tea are

reported to be rich in fluorides.
[Ref- Park / 24" / 663]

Fluorine is often called a two-edged sword: . . . ;
Prolonged ingestion of fluorides through drinking water in excess of the daily requirement is associated

with dental and skeletal fluorosis; and inadequate intake with dental caries. The use of fluoride is recognized as

the most effective means available for the prevention of dental caries.
[Ref- Park / 24" / 663]

Zinc
Q. Mention the functions of zinc. [DU-17N,14J,12J]
Answer ,
Functions of zinc:
1) Helps in wound healing
2) Essential in normal reproduction.
3) Required for cell growth.
4) It is required for synthesis of insulin by the pancreas.
5) It is required for the immunity function.
6) It is essential component of many enzymes (including carbonic anhydrase alcohol dehydrogenase
and alkaline phosphatase)
7) Itis active in the metabolism of glucose & proteins

8) It is required for the synthesis of insulin by the pancreas.

Q. Write down the complications of acute viral hepatitis.
Answer

Complications of acute viral hepatitis;
1) Fulminant hepatic failure.
2) Chronic Hepatitis.
3) Cirthosis of liver.
4) Hepatocellular carcinoma.
5) Relapsing hepatic failure.
6) Aplastic anaemia,
7) Post Hepatitis syndrome.
8) Connective tissue disease.
9) Papular acrodermatitis.
10) Cholestatic hepatitis.
11) Renal failure (hepato-renal syndrome).
12) Hyperbilirubinaemia & its complications.

Q. Write the mode of transmission of plague.

Answer

* Mode of transmission:
1) Vector transmission: It is transmitted to man mainly by the bite of infected rat flea.

2) Mechanical transmission: It may take place during the act of feeding by rat flea if the bacilli are

present on the mouth part of the flea.
3) Droplet infection: Pneumonic plague is spread directly from person to person by

droplet infection.

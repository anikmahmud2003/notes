Q. How coal miners can be prevented from anthracosis? [DU-12Ju]

Answer

Possible health problems: Coal miner’s pneumoconiosis (anthracosis).

It may have two general phases:

1) The first phase is labeled as simple pneumoconiosis which is associated with little ventilatory
impairment. This phase may require about 12 years of work exposure for its development.

2) The second phase is characterized by progressive massive fibrosis (PMF); this causes severe
respiratory disability and frequently results in premature death. Once a background of simple
pneumoconiosis has been attained in the coal worker, a progressive massive fibrosis may develop
out of it without further exposure to it.

Preventive measures: As there is no cure for this conditions emphasis should be on preventive measures; eg.
> Dust control.
> Personal protection and.
> Periodic medical check-up.

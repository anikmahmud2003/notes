Q. Explain HNPSP.
Answer
Health, nutrition and population sector programme (HNPSP):

‘As the life of HPSP is to come to an end in June 2003 the Government has decided to launch a new
project - Health, Nutrition and Population Sector programme (HNPSP) of 3 year duration.

But since the Project Concept Paper (PCP) and the Project Implementation Paper (PIP) have not been
completed the launching of HNPSP is likely to be delayed. According to the public health experts the existing
programme has yielded hardly any palpable result and hence the decision to introduce a new programme
without the full implementation of the existing one does not seem to be logical.

Responsibility for Health

Q. Discuss the role of community participation for achieving the goal of HFA.

. Who are responsible to protect and promote health? Explain it in brief. [RU-14J]
nswer

_ Health is on one hand a highly personal responsibility & on the other hand a major public concern. It
thus involves the Joint efforts of the whole social fabric, viz, the individual, the community and the state to
Protect & promote health.

1) Individual responsibility:

> Primarily a person is responsible for his/her health,

> Observance of simple rules of behaviors relating to diet, sleep, exercise etc.
> Attention to personal hygiene.

2) Community responsibility:
> Community can provide facilities manpower, logistic support and possible funds.

> Community involvement in planning, management & evaluation of health programme.
> Joining and using the health service.

3) State responsibility:

> Health is constitutional right.
> Govt. is legally bound to provide health care to every citizen,

4) International responsibility:

> Co-ordination of different govt. e.g. Small pox eradiation.
> WHO central co-ordinating agency.
> UNICEF - another co-ordinating agency.

we the characteristics / criteria of health indicators.
\ nswer

Health indicators: Health indicators are the tools with which we measure the health status of a community.

Characteristics / criteria of health indicators: Ideal indicators —
1) Should be valid i.e. they should actually measure what they are supposed to measure.
2) Should be reliable & objective, i.e. the answers should be the same if measured by different people
in similar circumstances,
3) Should be sensitive, i.e. they should be sensitive to changes in the situation concerned.
4) Should be specific, i.e. they should reflect changes only in the situation concemed.

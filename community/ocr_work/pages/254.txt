Q. How can you measure the burden of CHD?
Answer
Measurement of the burden of CHD: The burden of CHD may be estimated in various ways-
1) Proportional mortality ratio: The proportion of all deaths currently attributed to it.
2) Loss of life expectancy: It is the average gain in life expectation that would follow a complete
elimination of all cardiovascutar deaths if another mortality rates remain unchanged.
3) CHD incidence rate: This is the some of fatal and non-fatal attack rates.
4) Age specific death rates: It suggests a true increase in incidence.
5) Prevalence rate: the prevalence of CHD can be estimated from cross-sectional surveys using ECG
for evidence of infarction and history of prolonged chest pain.
6) Case fatality rate: This is defined as the proportion of attacks that are fatal within 28 days of
onset.
7) Measurement of risk factor levels: This includes measurement of levels of cigarette smoking,
blood pressure, alcohol consumption and serum cholesterol in the community.
Medicalares Measurement of levels of medical care in the community are also pertinent.

Q. Describe modes of transmission of rabies?
Answer

Modes of transmission of rabies:

1) Animal bites: Most of the human rabies results from the biting of-
> Rabid dog,
> Also cat, monkey, jackal, horse, & sheep etc. As a pre-requisite to transmission, the saliva

of the biting animal must contain rabies virus at the time of bite.

2) Licks:
> Licks on abraded skin and mucosa
> Injury with bone splinter or other object contaminated by the saliva of a rabid animal.

3) Aerosols:
> Only in certain caves harbouring rabies infected bat.
> In the laboratory, aerosols created during homogenization of infected animal brains can
infect lab workers.

4) Person to person:
> Child biting to others.

> Corneal & organ transplantation.

Q. How rabies differs from other communicable diseases? [RU-1 1Ju]
Q. Incidence of Rabies is increasing-discuss. [CU-17M]

Answer

Peculiarities of rabies from other communicable diseases:

1) It is the only communicable disease of man that is always fatal.

2) Highly variable incubation period in man, commonly | — 3 months following exposure but may.
vary from 7 days to many years. The incubation period depends on the site of the bite, severity of
the bite, number of wounds, amount of virus injected, species of the biting animal, protection
provided by the clothing and treatment undertaken, if any. In no other communicable disease
incubation period is so variable and dependent on so many factors.

Q. Write short notes on (i) Street virus & (ii) Fixed virus.
Answer
Street virus: The virus recovered from naturally occurring cases of rabies is called street virus. The virus is
excreted in the saliva of affected animals. The virus as it occurs in nature is known as street virus.
> The virus can be also isolated from human cases.
>  Itis pathogenic to all mammals.
> Itcan multiply in the neuronal and extra-neuronal tissue(muscles).
> It has a long variable incubation period (20-60 days) in days.
> It forms Negri bodies.

Fixed virus: It is defined as one that has a short, fixed and shortened incubation period when collected after
serial brain-to-brain passage of the natural virus into a suitable animal. Serial brain-to-brain passage of the street
virus in rabbits modifying the virus to form fixed virus.

> It can multiply in the neuronal tissue but not in extra-neuronal tissue(muscle)

> Direct injection to brain produce encephalitis.

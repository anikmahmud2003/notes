Q. Who are the target population for tetanus toxoid (TT)?
Answer
Target population for tetanus toxoid (TT):
1) All infants.
2) Children.
3) Soldiers.
4) Farmers.
5) Mothers at the seventh and eighth months of pregnancy to prevent tetanus neonatorum.

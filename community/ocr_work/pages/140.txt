Q. Discuss the control measures of anthrax. [DU-11J]
Answer
Control measures of anthrax:
1) Enforcement of health measures in the industries dealing with skin and hides, wool and hairs, bone
and bone products, etc.
2) Vaccination of animals at risk.
3) Education of people about the mode of transmission of the disease.

4) Vaccination of high-risk groups, such as veterinarians and those handiing potentially contaminated
industrial raw materials.

5) Notification to local health authority.

6) Disinfection of soiled articles.

7) Specific treatment: Penicillin is the drug of choice. Ciprofloxacin, Sulphonamides,
chloramphenicol and tetracycline have also been used successfully.

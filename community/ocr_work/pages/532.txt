Q. Write short not on: Transmission of faecal-borne diseases.

Answer

Faecal borne disease transmission:
The human excreta of a sick person contains the agent which is transmitted to a new host through

various channels:

> Water

> Finger

> Flies (wate)
> Soil

>

Food

ED
Prevention of faecal borne disease: Usually faecal borne diseases can be prevented by sanitary barrier. The
barrier can be provided by a sanitary latrine & a disposal pit. They break the disease cycle in the most effective
step. As a result disease cannot reach the new host. The disease cycle can be broken at various level:

1) Segregation of faeces,
2) Protection of the water supplies,
3) Protection of foods,

4) Personal hygiene and
5) Control of flies.

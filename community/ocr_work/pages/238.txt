Q. How will you contro! diarrhoea in a village?
Answer

Component of a diarrhoeal diseases control programme:

* Short term: Appropriate clinical management:
1) Oral rehydration therapy.
2) Appropriate feeding
3) Chemotherapy

%

* Long term:
A) Better MCH clinical management:
1) Maternal nutrition : Improvement of prenatal nutrition will reduce the LBW &
postnatal nutrition improves the quality of breast milk.
2) Child nutrition:
> Promotion of breast feeding.
> Appropriate weaning practice
> Supplementary feeding.

3) Other cares of the child : Maintenance of hygiene.

B) Preventive strategies:
1) Sanitation
2) Health education
3) Take safe water
4) Using of sanitary latrines
5) Immunization against measles & others
6) Fly control

C) Prevention of diarrhoeal epidemics:
1) Early detection of epidemic.
2) Active case finding
3) Provision of early treatment of cases
4) Teaching paramedics and local volunteers to administer ORS solution.
5) Notification to local central authorities
6) Disinfections and disposal of stool, vomit, contaminated clothing
7) Health education
8) Provision of safe water and sanitary latrine
9) Chemo prophylaxis
10) Epidemiological investigations and report writing.

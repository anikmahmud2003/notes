Paucibacillary leprosy:
Here the bacillary load is low, hence he bacilli cannot be demonstrated in routine skin smear. It includes

Paucibacillary leprosy is further divided into two types:
1) Single lesion paucibacillary leprosy.
2) Paucibacillary leprosy with 2 - 5 skin lesions.

Mutibacillary leprosy: .
In his type bacillary load is high. It includes all lepromatous and borderline forms, where the skin smear

Slow sand filter

Q. Identify the model. What are the components of slow sand filter? Write down the function of
vital layer.
Answer
Identification:
This is a model of slow sand filter.

Components of slow sand filter:
(1) Supernatant (raw) water
(2) A bed of graded sand
(3) An under-drainage system
(4) A system of filter control valves

Functions of vital layer:
(i) It removes organic matter.

(ii) It holds back bacteria.
(iii) It oxidizes ammoniacal nitrogen into nitrates.
(iv) It helps in yielding a bacteria free water.

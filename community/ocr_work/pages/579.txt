Q. Mention some factory acts.
Answer

Factory act: The factories act regulating the working conditions of the workers are —
1) Keeping the factory premises clean.
2) Proper disposal of wastes and effluents produced due to manufacturing process.
3) Proper ventilation and adequate measures to protect workers from excessive temperature.
4) Measures to protect workers from inhalation of dust and fumes.
5) Providing artificial humidification in cotton mills.
6) Provision of sufficient space to avoid overcrowding.
7) Arranging sufficient and suitable lighting and preventing glare in the factory.
8) Adequate supply of wholesome drinking water for the worker.
9) Provision of sufficient number of latrines and urinals.
10) Provision of bathing, washing and canteen facilities.
11) Supply of first aid appliances.
12) Prohibiting employment of children below the age of 14 years.

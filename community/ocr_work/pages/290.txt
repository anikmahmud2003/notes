Q. Discuss transmission of diseases by houseflies.
Q. How house fly acts as a vector in context of communicable disease? [DU-15Ju]
Answer
Mechanism of disease transmission by house flies:
1) Mechanical transmission: they transport micro-organisms on their feet and hairy legs. House flies
are called porters of infection.
2) Vomit drop: The regurgitated stomach content or vomit drop is a rich bacterial culture. By its habit
the house fly infects food and thereby transmits disease.
3) Defecation: The excrement of housefly has been found to contain numerous micro-organisms as

well as cysts and ova of intestinal parasites.

Q. Outline the control measures of Musca domestica. [CU-15Ju]

Q. Mention the control measures of house fly. [RU-1 Ju]

Answer

Housefly control measures:

1) Environmental control: Source reduction, i.e. elimination of their breeding places by.

> Storing garbage, kitchen wastes and other refuse.

Efficient collection, removal & disposal of refuse by incineration, composting or sanitary

landfill.

Provision of sanitary latrines.

Stopping open-air defection.

Sanitary disposal of animal excreta.

General improvement of sanitation.

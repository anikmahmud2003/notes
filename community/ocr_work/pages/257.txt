Rule of halves:

Hypertension is an ‘iceberg’ disease. Only about half of the people in the general population of most
developed countries are hypertensive. Half of them are aware of the condition, half of them are being treated,
and only about half of those treated were considered adequately treated. It is known as “Rules of halves”.

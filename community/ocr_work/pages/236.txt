Q. What are the risk factors of diarrhoeal disease?
Answer
Risk factors for diarrhoeal disease:

Risk factors or Can

“> Host Factors:
1) Incidence highest in the age group 6-11 months when weaning occurs.
2) Babies under 6 months of age fed on cow’s milk or infant feeding formulas.
3) Malnutrition
4) Poverty
5) Prematurity
6) Reduced gastric acidity
7) Immunodeficiency
8) Lack of personal and domestic hygiene
9) Incorrect feeding practices.

“Environmental factors:
1) Bacterial diarrhoea occurs most frequently during warm season.
2) Viral diarrhoea (particularly rota viral) peak during the winter.
3) In tropical areas, rotavirus diarrhoea occurs throughout the year, increase frequency during the
drier cool mouths. And the bacterial diarrhoea peak during the warmer, rainy season.

Q. How community diagnosis and community treatment is being carried out in diarrhocal
disease control programme?
Answer
Community diagnosis in diarrhoeal disease control programme: By the following way:
1) Identification and qualification & quantification of diarrhoeal cases by collection and interpretation
of the relevant data, such as —
> Age sex distribution of diarrhoea of a population, the distribution of population by social
group.
> Measurements of death rate by diarrhoea
> The incidence and prevalence of diarrhoea cases.

2) Quantification of morbidity and mortality of diarrhoea.

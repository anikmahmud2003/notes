Answer

Community clinic:

It is under implementation infra-structure of health service center one for 6000 rural population in total
number of 13500 in whole, country under health and population sector programmer (HPSP) project of health
and family welfare ministry of Bangladesh through essential service package (ESP) in order to reach health and
family planning service at door step of people.

“+ Service providers: Community health care provider (CHCP) and helped by Health assistant (HA)
and Family welfare assistant (FWA). Medical officer posted in an union sub-center or heath &
family welfare center (H&FWC) monitors the activities & services of the community clinics of that
union.

“> First community clinic in BD: At Gimadanga of Patgatti union of Tungipara Upazila in Gopalgonj
district.

+ Opening date of first community clinic: 26th April 2000.

“+ First foundation stone setup of community clinic: At village Daona of Targong union of Kapasia
Upazila in Gazipur district in 21" November 1999.

Referral system: Community clinic  Union-sub-center > Upazila health complex — Medical
college hospital.

Objectives of community clinic:
1) To provide one-stop health & family planning services.

2) To involve rural communities in the planning and maintenance.

3) To create awareness among rural populations about the utility of health and family planning
services.

4) To provide a package of essential health and family planning services at the lowest tier of the three-
tire service-delivery model. f

Functions / services of community clinic / functions of CHCP:
1) Treatment of common diseases.

2) MCH care.

3) EPI services.

4) Prevention and control of diarrhea.
5) Service for pregnant mothers.

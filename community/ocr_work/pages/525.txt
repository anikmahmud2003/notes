Q. How can you propose to reduce the greenhouse effect?
Answer

Prevention of greenhouse effect:

1) To reduce the continually increasing carbon dioxide content of the atmosphere by conserving fuel
energy.

2) To place more trees on the use of solar, hydraulic and atomic energy which are reusable.

3) Invent motor vehicle engines, which will require lesser fuel to cover longer mileage.

4) To reduce the use of diesel and other fossil fuels as much as possible and discover suitable

substitutes.
5) To create new forest areas by conserving the existing forests and employing planned aforestation.

6) To encourage use of organic manure in agricultural fields thereby reducing the use of chemical
fertilizers.
7) To stop the use of CFC and invent a cheaper substitute of the synthetic chemical.
8) To build suitable dams and walls in the coastal areas.
[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder /5" /520]

Q. Write short note on: Heat stroke. [SUST-15J]

Answer

Heat stroke:
This is attributed to failure of the heat regulating mechanism. It is characterized by very high body

temperature which may rise to 110 °F (43.3 °C). It is often fatal (mortality rate is about 40%).

Clinical features of heat stroke:

Partial or complete loss of consciousness.
Delirium.

Convulsion.

Dry & hot skin.
Classically sweating is absent or diminished (but may present in some case).

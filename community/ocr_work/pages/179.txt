Q. A 3 years old child presented with bilateral parotid swelling along with fever. What is the
likely diagnosis & how we can prevent this disease?
Answer
Diagnosis: Mumps.
Prevention of mumps:
1. Vaccination: A highly effective live attenuated vaccine is now available for the prevention of mumps.
Dose: Single dose (0.5 ml).
Route of administration: Intramuscular (IM).
The vaccine is now available as combined vaccine viz. combined measles-mumps-rubella (MMR)
vaccine / rubella-mumps vaccine.

2. Immunoglobulin: Specific immunoglobulin (MIg) is available.
[Ref- Park /24°/ 161]

Q. Describe the clinical features of mumps.
Answer
Clinical features of mumps:
1) Pain and swelling in either one or both the parotid glands but may also involve the sublingual and
submandibular glands. ;
2) Often ear-ache on the affected side prior to the onset of swelling.
3) Pain and stiffness on opening the mouth.
4) Mumps may also affect the testis, pancreas, CNS, ovaries, prostate etc.
5) In severe cases, there may be fever, headache and other constitutional symptoms which may last
from 3-5 days. :

6) The swelling subsides slowly over 1-2 weeks.

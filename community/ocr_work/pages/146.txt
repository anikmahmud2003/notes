Q. How tuberculosis can be prevented in the community? [DU-15Ju, RU-17M]
Q. How the mortality & morbidity of TB can be reduced? [CU-18M]

Answer

Control & prevention of tuberculosis: The control measures of TB consist of both curative & preventive.

A). Curative component: (Early diagnosis and treatment)

1) Case findings / early detection by:
> Sputum examination (Staining / Culture)

> Chest X-ray
> Tuberculin test.

2) Isolation. \

3) Chemotherapy: Isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol & streptomycin are given
according to category I, II & III.

B) Preventive component:

1) BCG vaccination.
2) Chemoprophylaxis.

3) General health improvement:
> Adequate housing.

> Good nutrition.
> Health education.

4) Limitation of disability.
5) Rehabilitation.
6) Surveillance.

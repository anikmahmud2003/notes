Q. Write in short the prevention and control measures of dengue fever? [DU-18N, SUST-15Ju]
Answer
Prevention and control measures of dengue fever:

1) Mosquito control:

> Elimination of breeding places in and around houses controlled by individual & community
action.

> Anti-larval action:
¥ Environment control: Discarded tins, empty pots, broken bottles should be got rid
of water holding container.
Y Chemical control: Larvicides (abate).
> Anti-adult measures: Residual sprays.

2) Host individual protection:
> Mosquito net.
> Screening.
> Use of long clothes & gloves.
> Repellents.

3) Vaccines: So far, there is no satisfactory vaccine and no immediate prospect of preventing the
disease by immunization. Live attenuated vaccine is affective.

4) Notifications: Occurrence of epidemics should be reported.

5) Health education about dengue fever.

6) Appropriate treatment of DHF and DF:

> Fluid replacement, blood transfusion & corticosteroids are indicated in the haemorthagic
varieties.

> Patients need full bed rest.
> Severe pain can be relieved by paracetamol.

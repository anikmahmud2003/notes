Protozoal Diseases

Amoebiasis

Q. Define amoebiasis. How you can prevent and control it?
Answer
Amoebiasis:
; The term ‘amoebiasis’ has been defined by WHO as the condition of harbouring the protozoan parasite
Entamoeba histolytica with or without clinical manifestations.

Types:
> Intestinal amoebiasis.
> Extraintestinal amoebiasis involving fiver (liver abscess), lungs, brain, spleen, skin etc.

Prevention and control:
* Primary prevention:
1) Sanitation:
> Safe disposal of human excreta.
> Washing hands after defecation and before eating.

2) Water supply:

> Protection of water supply against faecal contamination.
> Filtration and boiling of water.

3) Food hygiene: Food handlers should be periodically examined, treated and educated in food
hygiene practices such as hand washing.
4) Health education.

“* Secondary prevention:
1) Early diagnosis:

> Demonstration of trophozoites containing red cells is diagnostic.
> Serological test: Indirect haemagglutination test (IHA).

> Newer techniques: Counter immuno-electrophoresis (CIE).

> ELISA.

2) Treatment:
> Symptomatic cases: Metronidazole orally 30 mg/kg body weight/day, divided into 3
doses after meals, for 8-10 days.
> Asymptomatic infections: Oral diodohyroxyquin, 650 mg t.d.s. (adults) or 30-40
mg/kg of body weight/day (children) for 20 days, or oral diloxanide furoate, 500 mg

t.d.s. for 10 days (adults).

Kala-azar

Q. Define kala-azar.

Q. Name some districts of Bangladesh mostly affected by kala-azar? Why these areas are mostly
affected?

Answer

Kala-azar:
Visceral leishmaniasis is called kala-azar which is caused by protozoa Leishmania donovani

characterized by insidious onset of malaise, irregular fever, weight loss, anorexia, non-tender progressive
splenomegaly without hepatomegaly, lymphadenopathy, darkening of the skin of the face, hands, feet &

abdomen.

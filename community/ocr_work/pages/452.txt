Q. List the needful functions of fat. [RU-17M,16M,13J]
Answer
Functions of the fat: We need fat in the diet for the following reasons.
1) They are high-energy foods, providing as much as 9 kcal/gm.
2) Fats serve as vehicles for fat-soluble vitamins,
3) Fats in the body support viscera such as heart, kidney & intestine.
4) Fat beneath the skin provides insulation against cold.
5) Fats give flavour to food.
6) Fats (especially vegetable fats) are rich sources of essential fatty acids, which are needed by the
body for growth, for structural integrity of the cell membrane & decreased platelet adhesiveness.
7) Essential fatty acids (EFAs) reduce serum cholesterol & low-density lipoproteins (LDL).
8) Cholesterol is essential as a component of membranes & nervous tissue and is a precursor for the
synthesis of steroid hormones and bile acids.
9) Polyunsaturated fatty acids are precursors of prostaglandin now recognized as local hormones.

[Ref- Park /24" / 649]

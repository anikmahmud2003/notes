Q. Enumerate the environmental health problems in Bangladesh. [SUST-16M/J,14Ju]

Q. Write down environmental health problem of your country.
Answer
Environmental health problems in Bangladesh:
1) Provision of safe and adequate water supply.
2) Disposal of wastes (refuse and human excreta).
3) Provision of good housing.
4) Air pollution especially in big cities.
5) Food sanitation.
6) Disinfections.
7) Control of insect vectors and other pests.
8) Hazards of noise, radiation.
9) Occupational hazards.
10) Natural disasters e.g. cyclone, flood etc.

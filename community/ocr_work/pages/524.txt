Q. What are the sources of greenhouse gases?
Answer
Sources of greenhouse gases are:
1) Emission of carbon dioxide from motor vehicles, in
ovens
2) Combustion of all kinds of fossil fuels, e.g., coal, natural gas, diesel oil, petrol, etc.
3) Deforestation resulting in reduction of trees, plants and vegetation which naturally absorb
atmospheric carbon dioxide
4) Emission of methane from decomposition of paddy waste, plants, fallen leaves and underwater
flora in marshy land, and from heaves of wood destroying white ants which are found widespread
in deforested area
5) Extensive synthesis of CFC

Q. Write short note on: Climate change & health. [SUST-16J]

Answer

Consequences of greenhouse effect / Global effects:
1) There will be an increase of about 2°C-5°C by the year 2050 AD.

2) The climate temperature will raise the sea level by about 4 to 5 feet in the next 50-60 years.

3) There will be melting of ice in Antarctic, raising the sea level by 15 to 20 feet in the next 100 years.

4) There will be a dramatic change in the climate and topography of the earth, and new social,
economic and political problems will confront the human race.

5) The global climatic change with new temperature and humidity pattern will bring disturbing

problems of land utilization and fertility.
6) Part of the equatorial zone may turn into a desert while long coastal areas may be lost in the sea.

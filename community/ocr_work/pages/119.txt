Q. Discuss different aspects of infectious disease control in Bangladesh context. [DU-07J]
Answer
Different aspects of infectious disease control in Bangladesh:
The term ‘disease control’ defines as a process aimed at reducing —
Y The incidence of disease,
¥ Duration and consequently the risk of transmission,
Y Effects of infection,
Y Financial burden to the community.

Different steps Bangladesh government for controlling different infectious diseases:
1) EPI (Expanded Programme on Immunization): It is launched for controlling following infectious

diseases -

Neonatal tuberculosis

Diphtheria

Pertussis

Measles

Polio

Tetanus

Hepatitis B

> Haemophilus influenza type-B (causes pneumonia)

VVVVVVV

2) EPI plus: It means immunization of the infant against EPI diseases along with supply of vitamin A,
folic acid, iron, iodine and giving education.

3) National immunization day (NID): Is observed in Bangladesh when extra doses of polio vaccines are
given to the children to reduce polio infection.
Sometimes, tetanus toxoid (TT) vaccines are delivered to neonates and mothers of reproductive ages to
control tetanus.

Answer
Notification of disease:

Once an infectious disease has been detected (or even suspected) it should be notified to the local health
authority, whose responsibility is to put into operation contro! measures, including the provision of medical care
to patients.

Y Notification of infectious disease is often made by the attending physician or the head of the
family. Anyone can (e.g. religious, political, administrative leader, teachers & others) report,
even on suspicion.

Y Inall cases, diagnosis is verified by the local health authority.

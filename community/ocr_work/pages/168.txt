Q. Epidemiologically hepatitis C is more dangerous than hepatitis B — explain. [RU-15Ju,13Ju]
Answer :

Hepatitis-C is more dangerous than hepatitis-B: Because-

1) Clinical features developed lately.

2) Laboratory facilities are not available.

3) Cannot be differentiated between acute and chronic case.

4) More complications. ’
5) No test to detect the antigen in serum.

6) No vaccine is available.

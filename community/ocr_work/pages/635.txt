Q. Mention the types of responses,

Answer

Types of responses: All forms of behaviour are responses to stimuli. They are classified as follows —
1) Physical responses; habits, skills.

2) Organic responses: emotions, feelings, tension

3) Intellectual responses: perceptions, thinking, reasoning.
[Ref- Park /24"/744)

For Viva

* Emotion: An emotion is a strong feeling of the whole organism. Emotions motive the human
behaviour. An emotional experience is characterized by both external and internal changes in the
human being.

A) Some major emotions are: Fear, Anger, Love, Hate, Jealousy, Moodiness, Joy, Sorrow,
Sympathy, Pity, Lust, Grief etc,
B) Some specific emotions:
1) Fear: Most common emotion. It can produce excitement or depression. Some of the
common fear of man are — fear of dark, fear of snakes, fear of death etc.
2) Anger: It is a destructive force which may create offensive situation.
3) Anxiety: Can be observed by rapid pulse and breathing, flushing, tremor, sweating and
dry mouth.
4) Love: The emotion by which a man can enjoy a happy and meaningful life in this

world.
[Ref- Park /24"/712]

* Psychosocial illness: There are a group of diseases known as “psychosocial diseases” (mind acting on
body), e.g. essential hypertension, peptic ulcer, asthma, ulcerative colitis which are attributed to

disturbed emotional states.
[Ref- Park / 24/712]

Q. Mention the measures for control of emotion.

Answer

Measures for control of emotion: The following tips may be useful in controlling one’s emotion —
1) Cultivate hobbies, good hobbies of reading and recreation.
2) Adopt a philosophy of life to enable you to avoid mental conflicts.
3) Try to understand your own limitations.

4) Develop a sense of humor.
[Ref- Park / 24" /712]

Q. What are the human needs?
Q. Mention the kinds of human needs.

Answer

Kinds of human needs:

1) Biologic needs: These are ‘survival’ needs, A hungry man needs food, thirsty man water & sick
man need medicine etc. There are other needs such as sleep, rest, recreation and fresh air, The
doctor should be aware of these needs in the day-to-day care of the patients.

2) Social needs: The need for company, love, affection, recognition & education are all social
needs. Some of these needs are met by the family, and some by the community.

3) Economic needs: Economic security, that is security from want, is one which everyone desires.

4) Ego-integrative needs: The desire for Prestige, power and self-respect come in this category.

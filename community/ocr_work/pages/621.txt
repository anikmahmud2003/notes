Health Education

Q. Define health education? [RU-17N, SUST-18N/M,17N,16N, 13J,10Ju]
Answer
Health education:

The definition adapted by the national conference on preventive medicine in USA, is ‘health education
is a process that informs motivates and helps people to adopt and maintain health practices and life styles,
advocates environmental changes as needed to facilitate this goal and conducts professional training and
research to the same end.’

Or,

The dynamic definition of health education by Alma- Ata Declaration is as follows:
“a process aimed at encouraging people to want to be healthy, to know how to stay healthy, to do what they can

individually and collectively to maintain health and to seek help when needed".
[Ref- Park / 24" / 894]

Q. Write down the changing concept of health education.

Answer

Changing concept of health education: Historically health education has been committed to disseminating
information and changing human behaviour. Following Alma -At declaration the changing concepts are —

- Prevention of disease and promotion of healthy lifestyles.

- The modification of individual behaviour to modification of "social environment" in which the
individual lives.

- Community participation and community involvement.

- Promotion of individual and community "self-reliance".
[Ref- Park /24"/895]

Q. What are the aims and objectives of health education? [CU-10Ju]
Q. Highlight the objectives of health education. [SUST-18N,17N]
Answer
Aims and objectives of health education:
1) To encourage people to adopt and sustain health promoting life style and practice.
2) To promote the proper use of health services available to them.
3) To arouse interest, provide new knowledge, improve skill and change attitudes in making rational
decisions to solve their own problems; and
4) To stimulate individual and community self-reliance and participation to achieve health
developments through individual and community involvement at every step from identifying

problems to solving them.
[Ref- Park /24"/895]

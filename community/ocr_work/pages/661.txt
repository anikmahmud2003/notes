Q. Write down the functions of ICDDR,B. [SUST-16N]
Q. Write short note on: ICDDR,B. [CU-18M, 16N]

Answer

ICDDR,B:

The International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (ICDDR,B), established in 1960
is an international health research institution located in Dhaka. It was formerly known as Cholera Research
laboratory that was transformed into ICDDR,B in 1978. With the changing trend in the world scenario in health
and population over the years, ICDDR,B has expanded its activities to address some of the most critical global
health needs. In collaboration with partners from academic and research institutions throughout the world, the
Centre conducts research, training and extension activities as well as programmed-based activities,

Objectives:
1) To undertake and promote research on diarrhoeal diseases and on related subjects and nutrition.
2) To prevent and control diarrhoeal diseases in developing country.
3) To improve health care method and public health programs in developing countries:
4) To train and disseminate knowledge in those fields and to develop improved means.

Divisions of ICDDRB: The centre is divided into 4 divisions —
> Clinical science division
> Community medicine division
» Laboratory science division
> Population science division

Major achievements:

1) Development of Dhaka solution.

2) Development of oral rehydration therapy.

3) Development and evaluation of cholera vaccine.
4) Development of improved ORS.

5) Dissemination of knowledge on diarrhea research.

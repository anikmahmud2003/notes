Q. Define amino acids.
Answer
Amino acids:
Amino acids are a group of organic compounds containing two functional groups- amino and carboxyl.

The amino acids are termed as a-amino acids, if both carboxy! and amino groups are attached to the same
carbon atom. Over 300 amino acids occur in nature but only 20 amino acids act as monomer of proteins.

Q. Define essential amino acid. Name the essential amino acids. Why they are called so?
Q. Enumerate essential amino acids. [RU-14J,13J]
Answer |
Essential amino acid: .
The amino acids which cannot be synthesized in the body but essential for the growth and maintenance
of the body and hence they have to be supplied in the diet.
They are so called because, they are not synthesized within the body, and therefore they must be
supplied with diet. There are eight essential amino acids —
1) Isoleucine
2) Tryptophan
3) Metheonine
4) Valine
5) Leucine
6) Lysine
7) Threonine
8) Phenylalanine

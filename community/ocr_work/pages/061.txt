Q. What are the common criteria to eradicate a disease?
Answer
Factors responsible for eradicating a disease:
1) Infection & disease limited to human host.
2) No animal reservoir or insect vector; transmitted person to person.
3) Characteristic clinical illness; usually serious, easily diagnosed.
4) Few or no subclinical cases,
5) No long term carrier,
6) Only one causative agent or serotype persists.
7) Short period of infectivity.
8) Immunity is of long duration.
9) Inhibits reinfection, reactivation, reduces or eliminates excretion of organism.
10) Disease has seasonal variation.
11) Absence or interference by maternal antibodies in infants.

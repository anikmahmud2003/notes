General activities:

1) School health programme (SHP) will conduct training for school teachers, one from each school
(primary, secondary and madrasa). The priority topics to be included in the training are safe water,
sanitation, personal hygiene and first aid. . . .

2) Two medical officers and four support stuff, assigned to each school health clinic will provide services
to the school children at the clinic as well as the nearby schools. .

3) SHP will provide preventive, promotive, limited curative (first aid) services, and health education
tegarding personal and environmental hygiene. . .

4) A referral system will be developed to provide curative services through the existing health facilities,
Every student will be provided with a ‘health card’ and ‘referral form’.

5) Inaddition to the on-going activities, the following will be implemented through SHP —

> Deworming of school children at Community Clinics, UHF WCs / RDs at union level and
UHCs at upazila level using albendazole 400 mg single dose.

> Promotion and monitoring of iodized salt consumption through one-day workshop /
dissemination seminar at the union and thana levels.

> Strengthening of child nutritional activities by promoting breast feeding, improving weaning
practices, and through the distribution of vitamin A capsules and multivitamin tablets at the
community, union and upazila levels.

Q. What is deworming? [CU-12Ju]
Answer

Deworming:
Deworming is the giving of an anthelmintic drug to an individual to rid him/her of intestinal parasites.

Q. How school health service developed in Bangladesh?
Answer
Development of school health service in Bangladesh:

Following the preparation of a Master Plan in 1993 for the development of a comprehensive and viable
School Health Programme in the country, the project proforma was signed in June 1996 for the implementation
of School Health Pilot Project (SHPP) in 4 districts under the World Bank's Fourth Population Project.

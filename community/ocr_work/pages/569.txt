Q. Write short note on: Silicosis.
Answer
Silicosis:

” It is an occupational disease caused by inhalation of dust containing free silica or silicon dioxide, found
in mining industry, Pottery and ceramic industry, sand blasting, metal grinding, iron & steel industry, building

and construction works and others, Among the occupational disease, it is the major cause of permanent
disability and mortality.

The incidence of silicosis depends upon:
1)

Chemical composition
2) Health status of the person exposed
3) Fineness
4) Concentration of the dust in the air
5) Period of exposure

Size of the particle: 0.5 to 3 micron
Incubation period: It may vary from a few months up to years of exposure.

Pathogenesis:
Particles are ingested by the phagocytes
N

Accumulation and blockage of the lymph
L

Formation of dense nodular fibrosis

Symptoms: .
Initially there is dry irritant cough, dyspnoea on exertion and pain in the chest. In advance cases,

impairment of the total lung capacity is commonly seen. An X-ray of the chest shows “snow-storm” appearance
in the lung fields.

Prevention and control: As there is no effective treatment for silicosis, the only way that silicosis can be
controlled is by —

(i) Rigorous dust control measures, ¢.g. substitution, complete enclosure, isolation, hydro blasting, good
house-keeping, Personal protective measure
(ii) Regular physical examination of the workers.

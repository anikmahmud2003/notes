Answer

Management & prevention of human rabies: This may be considered in 3 headings:

Y Post-exposure prophylaxis.
v  Pre-exposure prophylaxis. ;
Y Post-exposure treatment of persons who have vaccinated previously.

* Post-exposure prophylaxis:
a) General consideration: Every instants of human exposure should be treated as an emergency.
The aim is to neutralized the inoculated virus before in can enter the nervous system
b) Local treatment of wound:
> Immediate thorough cleansing and flushing with soap or detergent and water of all
wounds caused by animal bites or scratch.

> Open wounds should not be closed.
> If the wound is severe and near the head, it should be infiltrated with rabies

immunoglobulin (RIG). Dose — 20 IU/kg body weight single dose.

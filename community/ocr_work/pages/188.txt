Q. How rabies can be controlled?
Q. How the morbidity & mortality of rabies can be reduced? (CU-17M]
Answer
Preventive and control measures of rabies:
1) Immunization of pet dogs and cats annually.
2) Licensing of dogs.
3) Quarantine measures for entry of dogs in rabies free areas.
4) Collection and destruction of stray dogs.
5) Ten-days detention and observation of dogs and cats known to have bitten a person or showing
suspicious signs of rabies.
6) Unvaccinated dogs or cats bitten by known rabid animals should be destroyed.
7) Immediate submission of intact head of animal that die of suspected rabies for laboratory
examination.
8) Pre-exposure immunization of high-risk groups e.g. veterinarians, dog handlers and laboratory
workers with HDCV given IM in 3 doses (1 ml each) on days 0, 7, 21 or 28.
9) Post-exposure measures:
> Local treatment of wound.

> Immunization.

Q. Enumerate different types of vaccines used for prevention of rabies.
Answer
Anti-rabies vaccine (ARV):

Rabies vaccine is defined as a fluid or dried preparation of rabies fixed virus grown in the neural tissue
of rabbits, sheep, goats, mice or rats or embryonated duck egg, or in cell culture, and inactivated by suitable
method (with phenol or beta-propiolactone).

Types:
1) Nervous tissue vaccine:
> Derived from adult animal nervous tissue (sheep).
> Derived from sucking mouse brain.

2) Duck embryo vaccine.

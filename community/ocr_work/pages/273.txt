Q. Mention the role of Bangladesh Institute of Research and Rehabilitation in Diabetes,
Endocrine and Metabolic Disorders (BIRDEM) for prevention and control of diabetes in
Bangladesh.

Answer

Role of BIRDEM for prevention and control of diabetes in Bangladesh: oo ;

1) It provides comprehensive diabetic health care, which includes management, diabetic education,
motivation and rehabilitation of patients. .

2) It produces manpower, like diabetologists, nurses, laboratory technicians, social welfare workers, etc.

3) It also provides short term training courses for health personnel of various categories with a view to
incorporate diabetic care at the primary health care level. .

4) Italso provides hospital services specially to deal with the complications of diabetes and runs research
programmes in the field of diabetology.

5) BIRDEM has its own Postgraduate Academy, running different postgraduate courses in diabetes,
endocrine and metabolic disorders.

Obesity:
Obesity may be defined as an abnormal growth of the adipose tissue due to an enlargement of fat cell
size (hypertrophic obesity) or an increase in fat cell number (hyperplastic obesity) or a combination of both.

Q. Mention sources, functions and deficiency diseases of folic acid.

Answer
Folic acid:

Sources:

e Plant sources: Green leafy vegetables, fruits and cereals etc.
e Animal sources: Liver, meat, dietary products, eggs, milk.
[Ref- Park /24"/ 658]

Functions of folic acid:
1) Synthesis of nucleic acids (which constitute the chromosomes).

2) Itis also needed for the normal development of blood cells in the marrow.
Deficiency features of folic acid: Folic acid deficiency is probably the most common vitamin deficiency found
in pregnancy and lactation. The deficiency features are-

1) Megaloblastic anaemia.

2) Glossitis.

3) Cheilosis.
4) Gastrointestinal disturbances such as diarrhoea, distension and flatulence.

5) Severe folate deficiency may cause infertility or even sterility.
Neural tube defect in the fetus e.g. spina bifida, anencephaly (when deficiency in pregnant mother).

Q. What are the functions of vitamin C?

Answer
Functions of vitamin-C:
1) It is a potent anti-oxidant. It plays role in tissue oxidation.

2) Helps in formation of collagen which provide a Supporting matrix for the vessels, connective tissue

bones and cartilage.
3) Reduces ferric iron (Fe*’) to ferrous form (Fe?’). Thus helps absorption of iron from vegetables.

Types of reservoir: 3 types-

1) Human reservoir: Case or carriers.
2) Animal reservoir: Cases or carriers of zoonotic diseases.
3) Reservoir in non-living things: soil and inanimate matter.

Another types of reservoirs: ,
1) Homologous reservoir: Homologous reservoir is applied when another member of the same
species is the victim. e.g. Vibrio cholerae.

Heterologous reservoir: Heterologous reservoir is applied when the infection is derived from a
reservoir other than man. e.g. animals and birds infected with salmonella.

Q. How can we control the reservoir?
Answer

Controlling the reservoir:
1) Early diagnosis
2) Notification
3) Epidemiological investigation.
4) Isolation
5) Treatment
6) Quarantine
7) Surveillance
8) Disinfection

Q. How an outbreak of typhoid fever could be controlled in an urban community? [RU-18M]
Answer
Preventive and control measures of typhoid fever:
1) Provision of safe drinking water.
2) Sanitary disposal of excreta.
3) Sanitary food handling in all eating places.
4) Hand washing facilities.
5) Control of files. |
6) Active immunization: By killed typhoid vaccine. 2 doses (0.5 ml each) of the vaccine are to be |
given subcutaneously at an interval of 4-6 weeks. Booster doses are recommended every 3 years for
those at continuing risk of infection.
7) Treatment of cases: By Ciprofloxacin (500-750 mg 12 hourly) or Lomefloxacin (200 mg 12
hourly or 400 mg daily) or Inj. Ceftriaxone (1-2 gm once daily) for 10 days.

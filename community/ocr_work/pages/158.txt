Q. Write down ongoing polio eradication program in Bangladesh. [DU-14J]
Answer :
The strategies for eradicated of poliomyelitis:
Poliomyelitis is the target disease of children that may be eradicated from the world by the year 2005.
Eradication of poliomyelitis means not only absence of the disease but also elimination of polio virus.
Eradication of poliomyelitis is a global program and the strategies recommended by WHO to achieve
the goal of eradication are:
1) Achieving and maintaining high immunization coverage.
2) Reporting and investigation all acute paralysis cases among children (Polio suspect).
3) Conducting supplementary immunization which includes case reporting, mopping up and
national immunization day (NID).

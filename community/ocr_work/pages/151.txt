Q. Write about Hansen’s disease (Leprosy).
Q. What is Hansen’s disease? [RU-12J]
Answer
Hansen’s disease (Leprosy): .

Leprosy is a chronic infectious granulomatous disease caused by Mycobacterium leprae characterized
by-shin lesion, involvement of peripheral nerve resulting in anesthetic patches (in the skin) hypo pigmented
areas, tropic changes producing defect in mucous membrane, muscle, bone.

Leprosy is clinically characterized by one or more of the following cardinal features:
1) Hypopigmented patches.

2) Partial or total loss of cutaneous sensation in the affected areas (the earliest sensation to be affected

is usually light touch). |
3) Presence of thickened nerves, and |
4) Presence of acid-fast bacilli in the shin or nasal smears.

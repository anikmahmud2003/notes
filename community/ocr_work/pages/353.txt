Q. Discuss medical termination of pregnancy.

Answer
Conditions under which medical termination of pregnancy is conducted:
1) Medical: Where continuation of pregnancy might endanger the mother’s life or cause grave injury

to her physical or mental health.
2) Eugenic: Where there is substantial risk of the child being born with serious handicaps due to

physical or mental abnormalities.

3) Humanitarian: Where pregnancy is the result of rape.
4) Socio-economic: Where actual or reasonably foreseeable (whether social or economic) could lead

to risk of injury to the health of the mother.
5) Failure of contraceptive devices.

The person or persons who can perform medical termination of pregnancy:
Registered medical practitioner having experiences in Obstetrics & Gynaecology.

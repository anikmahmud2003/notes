Q. What are the stages of health education?
Q. Write down the stages of adoption of new idea or practice. [DU-18M, 16J,12Ju]
Q. Discuss stages of health education to adopt a new ideas.
Answer
Stages in health education:
1) Awareness: At this stage, the person comes to know about the new idea or practice.
2) Interest: At this stage, the person seeks more detailed information.
3) Evaluation: During this stage, the person weighs the pros & cons all the practice and evaluates its
usefulness to him or to his family by mental exercise and reach a decision.
4) Trial: At this stage, the positive decision is put into practice.
5) Adoption: At this stage, the person decides that new practice is good and adopts it.

Contents of health education: May be divided into 8 main divisions —

1. Human biology: Much of the teaching pertaining to human biology is done in schools. The need for
exercise, rest & sleep are taught. The effects of alcohol, smoking, resuscitation & first aid are also
taught.

2s Nutrition: To guide people to choose optimum and balanced diets, which contain nutrients necessary
for energy, growth and repair. Education is given on the nutritive value of foods & most expensive
foods are by no means the more nutritious. Health education is also given about storage, preparation,
cooking, serving and eating of food.

3. Hygiene:

a. Personal hygiene: The aim is to promote standard of personal cleanliness within the setting of
the conditions where people live. It includes bathing, clothing, washing hands and toilet, care of
feet, nails & teeth, spitting, coughing, sneezing, personal appearance and inculcation of clean
habits in the young.

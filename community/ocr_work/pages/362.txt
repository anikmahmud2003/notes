Q. Write short note on: MCH based family planning.
he How Bangladesh can achieve MCH based family planning?
nswer

MCH based family planning:

> Itis the family planning service when its delivery is made to the consumer through the units which
usually deliver maternity and child health care. In short, it is the integration of mother and child

health and family planning services.
> Family planning will be highly effective if it is based at MCH centres. Because on these centres are

attended by mothers before, during and after delivery. During these stages mothers and women can
be better motivated in the MCH centres women are accompanied by their husband, so both of them
can be motivated together.

> It is inconvenient for the mother to go to one place to receive care for herself to another place for
care of her children and yet to another place for family planning.

> Family Planning services were being given from existing hospitals, clinics and specially established
family planning clinics. This was a sort of duplication of service, separate establishment, separate
staff, different places.

> Mothers are directly exposed to the risks of child birth, so family planning motivation are more
important for them child care is indirectly related with family planning. With these ideas in the

background came o. the concept of MCH based family planning.
> Now most of the family planning services are being delivered from MCH centers where the same

groups staff are looking after the health of the mother, children and also family planning affairs.
This is definitely beneficial for the government as well as consumers.

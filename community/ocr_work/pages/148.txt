Q. Discuss the TB control programme in Bangladesh.
Answer
TB control program in Bangladesh:
j Until 1965, TB services were mainly based in 44TB clinics. 8 segregation hospitals and 4 TB hospitals.
Since then different initiatives were taken to control TB, a major health burden in Bangladesh.

The objectives of the national tuberculosis programme (NTP):

+I To bring down the incidence of tuberculosis to such a level that it does not constitute a public health
problem. ;

Targets:
1) To detect at least 70% of existing cases of sputum positive TB.

2) To cure 80% of detected new cases of sputum smear positive TB.

Strategy:
1) Passive case finding through screening of symptomatic patient.

2) Early case detected of sputum smear positive cases by direct microscopy

3) Effective chemotherapy to sputum-smear positive cases and to all other TB patient.

4) Organizing TB treatment delivery up to upazila level.

5) Monitoring results through a standardized recording and reporting system.

6) Providing training and refresher courses to all personnel involved.

7) Integration of NTP and leprosy Elimination programme into general health services and
collaboration and NGO.

DOTS Strategies: The NTP (National Tuberculosis Control Programme) now applies the WHO recommended
DOTS (Directly observed treatment short course) strategy for detection and cure of TB. DOTS introduces in
Bangladesh in 1993, has now expanded to cover 75% of the population with a treatment success rate of nearly
80%. It combines five elements.

1) Political commitment to the programme

2) Reliable microscopic diagnosis

3) Standardized, multi-drug and directly observed treatment.

4) Adequate supply of drugs

5) Proper recording and reporting.

There are several tuberculin tests available, but the most commonly used and the most accurate is

the Mantoux test (MT).

Tuberculin is a purified protein derivative (PPD) of mycobacteria, The WHO recommended

tuberculin is “2 TU PPD-RT-23” diluted by Tween 80.

Purpose (use):
1) To measure prevalence of infection
2) Aid to diagnosis of suspected infection (not confirm diagnosis)
3) For contact tracing of infectious.
4) Indication of early clinical infection.
5) Post vaccination check for the efficacy of BCG vaccination

Technique of injection: Available at special disposable 1 ml syringes with disposable needle. 0.1 ml volume
of tuberculin is slowly injected on the dorsal aspect of the forearm intradermally.

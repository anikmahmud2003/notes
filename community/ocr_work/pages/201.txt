Answer

Diagnosis of kala-azar/ visceral leishmaniasis:

* Diagnosis of visceral leishmaniasis:
1) Clinical diagnosis:

> It is characterized by insidious onset of malaise, irregular fever, weight loss, anorexia,
> Non-tender progressive splenomegaly without hepatomegaly, lymphadenopathy,

> Darkening of the skin of the face, hands, feet and abdomen.

> Brittle hairs.

> Anaemia

> Mucosal, haemorrhage.

2) Serological diagnosis:
> Direct agglutination test (DAT)

> ICT for RK-39 antigen

> ELISA

> Indirect fluorescent antibody test
> Aldehyde test.

3) Parasitological diagnosis: Microscopic examination —

> Giemsa or Leishman staining .
> Demonstration of amastigote forms (LD bodies) are seen in the aspirates of the spleen,
liver, bone marrow, lymph node.

4) Culture: NNN media — Promastigotes are found

* Diagnosis of cutaneous leishmaniasis:

Y  Leishmamin skin test
Y Serological diagnosis : No value
Y Culture: NNN media.

Q. State NRR with the demographic significance of NRR = 1. [RU-13Ju]
Q. Illustrate the importance of Net Reproduction Rate (NRR). [RU-10J]

Answer
Net Reproduction Rate (NRR): It is defined as the number of daughters a newborn girl will bear during her

lifetime assuming fixed age-specific fertility and mortality rates.

Importance of NRR:
1) NRR is a demographic indicator.

2) NRRof 1 is equivalent to attaining approximately the 2-child norm.
3) If the NRR is less than 1, then the reproductive performance of the population is said to be below

replacement level.
4) Demographers are of the view that the demographic goal of NRR = | can be achieved only if at

least 60% of the eligible couples are effectively practicing family planning.

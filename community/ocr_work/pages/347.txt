Norplant / Subdermal implant

Q. Write short note on: Norplant / subdermal implant. [SUST-12J]
Answer

Norplant / subdermal implant: .
It is subdermal long term temporary hormonal contraceptive.

Effectiveness: 5 years.

Composition: It consists 6 silastic (silicone rubber) capsules, Each capsule contains 35 mg levonorgestrel. Each
capsule 34 mm in length and 2.5 mm in breadth.

Site of insertion: 6 to 7 cm above the elbow joint at medial aspect of arm.

Mode of action:
i) Progesterone increases the thickness of cervical mucus and thereby prevents sperm from entering
the cervix.
ii) It decreases the thickness of endometrial mucosa and thereby prevents implantation.
iii) It also inhibits tubal motility and delay the transport of the sperm and of the ovum to the uterine
cavity.
Advantage:

> Lactating mother can take it.
> Effective contraception for long period (upto 5 years).
Disadvantage:
> Irregularities of menstrual bleeding.
> Surgical procedure is required to insert and remove implants.

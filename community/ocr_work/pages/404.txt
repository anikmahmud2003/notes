Passive immunity: When antibodies produced in one body are transferred to another to induce
protection against disease. It is known as passive immunity.

Passive immunity may be developed in following ways:

> By administration of antibodies containing preparation (Immunoglobulin or antiserum)
> By transfer of maternal antibodies across the placenta.
> By transfer of maternal antibodies (IGA) via colostrum to foetus.
> By transfer of lymphocytes, to induces passive cellular immunity.

Q. Why small pox eradication was possible?
Q. Mention the epidemiological basis of eradication of small pox. [SUST-10J]
Answer
Epidemiological feature responsible for eradication of small pox:
1) No known animal reservoir.
2) No long-term carrier of the virus.
3) Life-long immunity, after recovery from the disease.
4) Easy recognition of the cases even by non-medical personal.
5) Persons with sub-clinical infection did not transmit the disease.
6) Availability of a stable, potent vaccine. -
7) International co-operation.

Q. When and how small pox has been eradicated?
Answer

History of small pox eradication:
Edward Jenner introduced vaccination against small pox in 1796. Through the next two centuries

improvements were brought in the type/ quality of vaccine and vaccination technique both of which occupied
important application in the global eradication program initiated by WHO in 1958 and intensified since 1967.

The last case of naturally occurring small pox in the world was reported in Somalia on October 26,
1977. Finally on May 8, 1980, the World Health Assembles declared “The World and all its people have won
freedom from the small pox an unprecedented achievement in the histories of public health.”

United Nations

Q. Write short note on: United Nations.

Answer
United Nations: It is an association of many countries, which aims to improve economic and social condition

and to solve political problems in the world in a peaceful way.
Establishment: It was established as the consequence of the UNA Conference on International Organization
held at San Francisco, USA on 26" June, 1945. Then United States President Franklin D. Roosevelt devised the

name United Nations. It was activated from 24" October, 1945.
24" October is observed as UN day in all the member countries.

Head quarter: New York, USA.
Member countries: Upto 2010, total number of member countries of UN is 192 (last member country is the
Montenegro in 28" June, 2006).

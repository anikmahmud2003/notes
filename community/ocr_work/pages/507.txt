Q. What are the air pollutants?
Q. Name 6 (six) important air pollutants. [RU-18N]
Answer

Air pollutants: More than 100 contaminants have been identified. The important ones are —

“ Gaseous pollutants:
1) Carbon dioxide

2) Carbon mono-oxide

3) Sulphur dioxide

4) Fluorine compounds

5) Oxides of nitrogen

6) Ammonia, aldehydes, beryllium, carcinogens.

“ Particulate matters (dusts, fumes, mists, smoke):
1)

Mineral dusts (coal, asbestos, limestone, cement)
2) Metal dusts with fumes (e.g. zinc, copper, iron, lead)
3) Acid mists (e.g. sulphuric acid)
4) Fluorides
5) Paint pigments
6) Pesticides
7) Carbon black
8) Tobacco smoke
9) Oil smoke

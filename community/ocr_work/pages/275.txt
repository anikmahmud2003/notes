Q. What types of care can be taken to lessen the distresses of senior citizens to pass the end days
comfortably? [CU-16M,14Ju]
Answer
Care of geriatric age people:
1) Diet & nutrition:

> Less carbohydrate & fat.

> Adequate protein & water.

> Plenty of fresh vegetables & fruits.

2) Regular exercise.

3) Reduction of weight in obese person.

4) Cessation of smoking & alcohol abuse.

5) Adequate sleep.

6) Reduction of stress & tension.

7) Social activities.

8) Strict control of comorbidities (e.g. hypertension, diabetes mellitus) & regular physical checkup.

Q. Why kala-azar strikes back in Bangladesh?

Q. What are the problems in control of kala-azar?

Answer

Kala-azar strikes in Bangladesh for the following reasons:
1) Lack of adequate information regarding epidemiology of visceral leishmaniasis.
2) Lack of detection & treatment of PKDS cases.
3) Lack of field diagnostic test.

Q. Write short note on: Shick test.

Answer

Shick test; Intradermal test which test two things —
Y To know the immune status of the person against diphtheria,
Y To sce the state of hypersensitivity to diphtheria toxin.

Procedure:

___ 0.2 ml of Shick test toxin is injected into the skin of forearm intradermally. Same amount of toxin
inactivated by heat (at 60°C for 15 minutes) is injected as a control into opposite arm.

Reaction: The following reaction may be observed —

1) Positive reaction: A circumscribed area of redness upto 3 cm or more in diameter appears at the
injection area of the test arm within 48 hours. Some swelling and tenderness may be noted. The
injection area of the control arm shows no changes. The positive test indicates that the individual is
susceptible to diphtheria,

2) Negative reaction: Both arms shows no reaction at all. The negative test indicates that the person
is immune to diphtheria.

3) Pseudo-positive Reaction: Here a red flush appears (much less circumscribed) on both the arms
within 6 to 18 hours which fades away completely within 3 to 4 days. The reaction is due to
hypersensitivity to protein and should be interpreted as Schick negative.

4) Combined Reaction: In this case red areas appear on both arms, but reaction at the injection site of
the test arm may persist for 5 to 7 days or more whereas reaction at the control arm disappears on
days 3 or 4 as this is due to allergy. It is a positive reaction which indicates that the person is
susceptible.

Q. Discuss positive health. [SUST-10Ju]
Q. What do you mean by positive health?
Answ ,
Positive health:
The state of positive health implies the notion of “perfect functioning” of the body & mind. It
conceptualizes health biologically, psychologically and socially.

A person who enjoys health at three planes i.e. good physical, mental and social well-being is said to be
in a state of positive health.

“+ Advantages / functions of dietary fiber:

1) Absorbs water, thus increasing the bulk of the stool and reduce the tendency to constipation.

2) Reduce plasma cholesterol.

3) Dietary fibers have a role in weight reduction.

4) Reduce the risk of gall stones, diabetes, coronary heart disease, hypertension, and bowel

+ Disadvantage: Dietary fibres tend to bind some fat soluble vitamins and elements like iron and zinc

Q. Define diarrhoea. [SUST-18M]
Q. Define diarrhoeal disease.
Answer
Diarrhoea:
Diarrhoea is defined as the passage of loose, liquid or watery stools more than three times a day.

However it is the recent change in consistency & character of stools rather than the number of stools
that is important.

Diarrhoeal diseases:
The term “diarrhoeal diseases” should be considered only as a convenient expression for a group of
diseases in which the predominant symptom is diarrhoea.
The term is not a nosological or epidemiological entity.

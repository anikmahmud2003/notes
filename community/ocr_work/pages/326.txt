this. [CU-18M]
Answer
This is a humanitarian crisis as nearly eight million Myanmar nationals are forcibly displaced from their
Jand to Bangladesh following threat to lives. Social hazards resulting from this include-
1) Scarcity of food and other resources in the affected areas of Bangladesh
2) Deforestation
3) Environmental pollution
4) Increased violence and crime in the affected community
5) Spread of infection and diseases to the host country people.

Q. Discuss the diet of a diabetic & hypertensive patient. [SUST-14Ju]
Answer
Dietary management of DM:
1) Healthy eating:
> Carbohydrate: (45-60%, sucrose up to 10%) Recent studies suggest short-term (6
months), very low carbohydrate diets (containing as little as 50g/day-13% of daily energy)
are safe and effective means of reducing weight and improving glycaemic control.
Fat: (< 35% with < 10% as saturated fat & 10 — 20% from monounsaturated fat).
Protein: (10-15%) Should not exceed 1 g/kg body weight/day.
Fruit / vegetables: 5 portions daily.
Salt: Not more than 6g daily. Further restriction of sodium intake (to less than 3 g daily) is
important in treating hypertensive diabetic patients.
2) Weight management: In addition to reducing calorie intake, overweight patients should be
strongly encouraged to take regular exercise, in the form of walking, swimming or cycling, for
approximately 30 minutes daily. (Management of obesity is discussed in ‘Chapter-04’)

3) Diabetic food and sweeteners: Low-calorie and sugar-free drinks and sorbitol containing foods.

4) Alcohol: Alcohol can be consumed in moderation unless there is a coexisting medical problem that
requires abstinence.

False Negative & False Positive

Q. Define false negative & false positive.
Answer
False negative: The term ‘false-negative’ means that patients who actually have the disease are told thatthe,
do not have the disease. It amounts to giving them a ‘false reassurance’.

> The patient with a false a ‘false-negative’ test result might ignore the development of signs g

symptoms and may postpone the treatment.
> A screening test which is very sensitive has few false negatives.
> The lower the sensitivity, the larger will be the number of false negatives.

False positive: The term ‘false-positive’ means that the patient who donot have the disease are told that they
have the disease.
> Inthe ‘false-positive’ cases normal healthy people may be subjected to further diagnostic tess
at some inconvenience, discomfort, anxiety & expense until their freedom from disease is
established.
> A screening test with a high specificity will have few false positives.
> False positives not only burden the diagnostic facilities, but they also bring discredit to
screening programmes.

Conclusion: No screening test is perfect, i.e. 100% sensitive & 100% specific.

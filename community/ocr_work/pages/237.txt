Q. How will you manage diarrhoea in a village?
Answer

Management of diarrhoea in a village:
A) Prevention of dehydration: (No dehydration, but diarrhoea continues)

1) More fluid: ORS, rice saline, home-made saline etc.
2) More food: Breast feeding, khichuri, vegetable, rice, pulses, fish, meat, banana, fruit juice etc.

3) Observing the signs of dehydration:

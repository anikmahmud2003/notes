Q. Give the control measures of zoonotic diseases.
Answer

Control of zoonoses:

1) Control in animal:
> Comprise diagnosis of the zoonotic condition
> Treatment
> Destruction
> Quarantine
> Immunization
2) Control of vehicles of transmission:

> Establishment of food hygiene practices
> Ensuring safety of animal products such as wool, bones, horn, fat.

> Proper disposal of animal carcasses and waster,
> Disinfection procedure

3) Prevention and treatment in man:
Protection of high risk group by immunization.

Chemoprophylaxis

Monitoring health status including occupational health

Prevention of spread by man

Early diagnosis and treatment condition in man.

Health Education.

Prevention of environmental contamination.

Prevention of food contamination

Improve diagnostic facility

House fly

Q. Identify the model. Mention the points in favour of its identification. What are the diseases

transmitted by it?
Answer
Identification:
House fly.

Identifying points:
The body is divided into head, thorax and abdomen.
Head bears a pair of antennae, a pair of large compound eyes and a retractile proboscis.
The thorax is marked with 2 — 4 dark longitudinal strips.
The thorax bears a pair of wings and three pairs of legs.
The legs and the body are covered with numerous short and stiff hairs, called the tenent hairs.

The abdomen is segmented and shows light and dark markings.

Diseases transmitted by house fly:

1) Diarrhoea
2) Dysentery

3) Cholera

4) Gastro-enteritis

5) Amoebiasis

6) Enteric fever (Typhoid and paratyphoid)
7) Helminthic infestations

8) Poliomyelitis

9) Conjunctivitis
10)Trachoma

11)Anthrax

12)Yaws

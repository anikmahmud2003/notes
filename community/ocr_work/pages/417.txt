Q. Enumerate the EPI vaccines. [SUST-12Ju]
Answer
Classification of vaccines used in EPI:
1) Live attenuated vaccine:
> Bacterial- BCG
> Viral- Oral polio
> Measles
2) Toxoids: Tetanus toxoid (TT)
3) Extracted cellular fractions: Pneumococcal vaccine (PCV)
4) Combined:
>  Pentavalent vaccine (DPT + hepatitis-B + haemophilus influenza-B)
> MR vaccine (measles + rubella)

Vaccines used in EPI:

Hygiene:
The word hygiene is derived from Hygeia (The goddess of health in Greek mythology).

Personal hygiene:
Personal hygiene or personal health care deals with measures, which are the personal responsibilities of

the individual for the promotion of good health.
It is the quality of life renders the individual fit to live and serve best.

Factors responsible for personal hygiene:
1. Health habit:
i) Eating and drinking
ii) Rest and sleep
iii) Recreation
* iv) Care of the bowel
v) Smoking and drinking
vi) Exercise
vii) Cleanliness
viii) Clothing
2. Weight control.
3. Dental care.

4, Marriage and sex education.

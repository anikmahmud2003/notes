Miscellaneous contraceptive methods

Q. State the natural methods of family planning (FP), [SUST-14Ju/J]
Answer |
Natural family planning methods: |
1) Basal body temperature (BBT) method: Basal body temperature is the lowest body temperature
ofa healthy person during waking hours. By noting temperature daily and by recording temperature
to successive 3-4 months a woman may determine her time of ovulation. From 24-72 hours after
ovulation a noticeable rise (0.3 — 0.5° C) in temperature occurs. |

2) Cervical mucus method / Billings method / ovulation method: In some women changes in the
character and appearance of cervical secretion and cervix occur just before ovulation. In addition

women may experience ovulatory pain. Observation of these physiological changes can help a Hi
woman to determine her period in the cycle. . i

3) Sympto-thermic method: This method combines 3 fertility awareness methods such as calendar
method, BBT method and the mucus method to identify the fertile period.

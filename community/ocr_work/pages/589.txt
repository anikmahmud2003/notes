Answer

Data:

Data consists of discrete observations of attributes or events.
Data are sets of values recorded on one or more observational units.

Classification of data:
* On the basis of source: :
1) Primary data: Data that is obtained directly from an individual, objects or phenomenon is
called primary data.

Example:
> The census of 2001 is an example of collecting primary data relating to the population.

+

* The collection of data about the health and sickness of a population is primary data.

2) Secondary data: Data that is obtained from outside source is called secondary data.

Example:
> Hospital records.

Previous surveys,
> Professional journals etc,

3) Tertiary data: Data that is obtained from text books,

“* On the basis of nature:

1) Quantitative: Data which have a magnitude. Examples- weight of a person.
2) Qualitative: Data which have no Magnitude or size. Example- male, female; vaccinated, not
vaccinated; anaemic, not anaemic etc,

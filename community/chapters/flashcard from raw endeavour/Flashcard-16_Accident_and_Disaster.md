A) Prevention and alleviation of consequences:
1
) Forecasting the disaster using modern technology.
2) Ascertaining the risk by periodically enlisting the support of various social groups.

providing temporary shelter, sanitary exercises including installation and management of safe
water supply, use of sanitary latrines, controlled refuse disposal, fly control , vector control ,
rodent control etc.
5) Improving professional qualities of local health personnel.
B) Immediate emergency care:
1) Whatever be the type of disaster, the local authority is required to set up an emergency
committee. The first task of the committee is to inform or instruct the people by using
loudspeakers and to mobilize volunteers so as to dispel fear and panic.
2) The first Priority following a disaster should be the care of the injured. The injured and afflicted
victims must be removed to the nearest medical establishment for emergency care. Those
requiring specialized surgical care or medical treatment are to be removed by ambulance, motor
boats etc to the nearest medical center with good facilities.

**Triage:**
It consists of rapidly classifying the injured on the basis of the severity of the injuries and livelihood of
their survival with prompt medical attention. Triage is the only approach that can provide maximum benefit to
the great number of injured in a major disorder.
Four color code system for triage:
1) Red: High priority treatment or transfer.
2) Yellow: Medium priority.
3) Green: Ambulatory patients.
4) Black: Dead / morbid patients.

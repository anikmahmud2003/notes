## Mental Health

<details>
<summary><b>Q. Define mental health.<br>Q. What are the warning signals of poor mental health? [RU-18M]</b></summary>

**Mental health:**
More recently, mental health has been defined as “a state of balance between the individual and the
surrounding world, a state of harmony between oneself and others, coexistence between the realities of the self
and that of other people and that of the environment”.
[Ref- Park /24" / 15]
Warning signals of poor mental health:
1) Worrying all the while.
2) Inability to concentrate.
3) Continually unhappy.
4) Loses temper easily.
5) Regular sleep disturbance.
6) Incapacitating fluctuations of mood.
7) Continual disliking of people's company.
8) Easily upset by disturbed daily routine.
9) Having constant pain and aches without physical cause.
10) Fear and panic without real cause.
11) Feeling own decisions always right and other's wrong.
[Ref- Rashid, Khabir, Hyder 15" / 425 + Park /24™ / 868]

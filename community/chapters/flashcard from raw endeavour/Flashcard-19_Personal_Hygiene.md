<summary><b>Q. State health information system. [RU-08Ju]<br>Q. What are the components of health information system?<br>Q. Enumerate the sources of health information in Bangladesh. [RU-08Ju]</b></summary>

**Health information system:**
Health information system is defined as “a mechanism for the collection, processing, analysis and
transmission of information required for organizing and Operating health services, and also for research and
training”.
Components of health information system:
1) Demography and vital events.
2) Environmental health statistics,
3) Health status — mortality, morbidity, disability and quality of life.
4) Health resources — facilities, beds, manpower.
5) Utilization and non-utilization of health services — attendance, admissions, waiting lists.
6) Indices of outcome of medical care.
7) Financial statistics (cost, expenditure) related to the particular objective.
Uses of health information:
1) To measure the health status of the people of a community and to quantify the health problems and
medical & health care needs of a community.
2) For local, national and international comparison of health status.
3) For planning, administration and effective management of health services and programmes.
4) For assessing whether health services are accomplishing their objects in terms of their effectiveness
and efficacy.
5) For assessing the attitudes and degree of satisfaction of the beneficiaries with the health system.
6) For research into particular problems of health and disease.

**Bio-statistics:**
Bio-statistics is that branch of statistics concerned with mathematical facts and data relating to
biological events.
Uses of Bio-statistics:
1) To define what is normal or healthy in a population and to find the limits of normality in variables
such as weight and pulse rate.
2) To test whether the difference between two populations, regarding a particular attribute is real ora
chance occurrence,
3) To study the correlation or association between two or more attributes in the same population.
4) To evaluate the efficacy of vaccines, sera etc by control studies
5) Ro locate, define and measure the extent of morbidity and mortality in the community in the
community.
6) To evaluate the achievements of public health programs.
7) To fix priorities in public health programs.

**Vital statistics:**
[Ref- Gupta /3™ /371]
Vital statistics is that branch of statistics that deals with collection, compilation and presentation of data
about the vital events of life.
Vital events are those events which have to'do with individuals entrance into or departure from life
with change in civil status. It includes:
  - Birth.
  - Death.
  - Marriage.
Sources of vital statistics:
1) Census
2) Vital registration method
3) Health and demographic surveys
4) Hospital and other institutional records
5) Sample vital registration system
6) Surveillance
Uses of vital statistics:
1) To describe health status of a community.
2) To diagnose health problems of a community.
3) To determine priorities, planning and evaluation of health programmes.
4) To promote health legislation.
5) For research purposes.
</details>

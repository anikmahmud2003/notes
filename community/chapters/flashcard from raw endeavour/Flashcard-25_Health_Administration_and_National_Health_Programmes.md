In the context of various weaknesses, shortfalls and anomaly observed during the implementation of the
Fourth Socioeconomic Development Plan, a five year Health and Population Sector Programme (HPSP)
covering the period July 1998-June 2003, has been formulated in consultation with the development partners
and stakeholders to reform the health and population sector and provide a package of essential health care
services to the people of Bangladesh. The external support will be provided by the World Bank, multilateral and
bilateral development partners.
Aims:
1) To maximize health benefits relative to per capita expenditure.
2) To meet felt needs of the clients.
3) To strengthen service delivery & improve system management.
Objectives:
1) To decrease IMR.
2) To decrease MMR.
3) To control communicable diseases.
4) To improve reproductive health care.
5) To reduce unwanted pregnancy.
6) To increase life expectancy at birth.
7) To decentralize health care services.

. Essential Services Package (ESP)

<summary><b>Q. Explain ESP. What are the components of ESP?<br>Q. State the components of ESP. [DU-12J]</b></summary>

**Essential Services Package (ESP):**
Due to resource constraints it is not possible to provide services needed by all segments of population.
It is, therefore, necessary to prioritize the urgently needed interventions in the health and population sectors,
particularly for the most vulnerable groups of population that will have greatest health impact per Taka spent.
ESP will be delivered to address the most important health needs of the population through different
levels of PHC system.
The elements of ESP: The elements of ESP are grouped into five major areas —
1) Reproductive health care (safe motherhood) including family planning.
2) Child health care (ARI, CDD, EPI, Blindness Prevention).
3) Communicable disease control (TB, Leprosy, Malaria, Kala-azar, Filariasis, STD) including EPI.
4) Limited curative care (common conditions and injuries).
5) Behaviour change communication (BCC): Social change, advocacy, social marketing.
ESP principal: Client centered services focusing on the needs of women & children. At all level of services,
increase the —
  - Quality of service.
  - Equity of access.

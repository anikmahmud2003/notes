Q. Write short note on: Auto disabled syringes.
Answer

A-D Syringes:
They are self-locking syringes that can be used only once. They are the preferred equipment for all

types of immunization sessions. Every Ad syringe is sterilized and sealed by the manufacturer.

unnecessary and should not try to inject air into the vial, as this will disable the syringes.
Advantages:

i) They can only be used once.

ii) They eliminate the patient to patient disease transmission caused by the use of contaminated needle
and syringes.

iii) They save time for health workers from their heavy work for sterilization.

Q. Write short note on: Ionizing radiation.

Answer

lonizing radiation (B & y rays):
Ionizing radiation is being increasingly used for sterilization of bandages, dressing, catgut, plastic

syringes, catheter, anesthetic instrument, transfusion set and surgical instruments.

The objects to be sterilized are placed in plastic bags before radiation, and they will remain sterile until
opened. Ionizing radiation has great penetrating powers with little or no heating effect.
This method is most effective but very costly. Commercial methods of sterilization are normally carried
out by gamma radiation (atomic). It is now one of the most viable, safe and economic methods used today.

Q. Write short note on: Fumigation.
Answer ;

Fumigation: It is a process of gaseous disinfection by using formaldehyde in the form of vapor.

Uses:
a Disinfection of operation theatre (OT) and hospital ward.
2 Disinfection of books, shoes, leather goods, silk, woolen cloths, toys etc.
) Also used as insecticide e.g. killing of mosquitoes, flies, lice etc.

Condition of the room:
For efficient fumigation, the temperature of the room should be around 70°F and the humidity should be
around 75%.

Procedure:

Boiling commercial formalin in 2 volumes of water (500 ml formalin + 1000 ml of water per 30 cu
meters of space) in a stainless steel vessel over an electric hot plate.

There is vigorous boiling and liberation of formaldehyde gas.

The room is kept closed for 6 — 12 hours to allow disinfection.

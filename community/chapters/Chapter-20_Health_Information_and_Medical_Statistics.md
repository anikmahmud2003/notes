Classification of sampling / different methods of sampling / sampling technique:
A) Probability sampling / Chance sampling / Random sampling:

1) Simple random sampling: This method is applicable when the population is small,
homogenous and readily available. The simple is drawn in such a way that each unit has an

equal chance of being drawn in the sample. So, this method is also called unrestricted random
sampling. To ensure randomness of selection, lottery method or table of Random Number can

be adopted.
2) Systematic sampling: In a systematic sampling, the selection of the first unit is made at
random and subsequent units are selected at certain interval.

3) Stratified sampling: The sample is deliberately drawn in a systemic way so that each portion
of the sample represents a corresponding stratum of the universe. This method is particularly

useful where one is interested in analyzing the data by a certain characteristics of the
population, viz Muslims, Hindus, Christians.

4) Multistage sampling: This method refers to the sampling procedures carried out in several
stages using random sampling techniques.

5) Cluster sampling: A cluster is a randomly selected group. This method is used when units of
population are natural groups or clusters such as villages, wards, blocks, slums of a town,

factories, workshops or children of a school etc. As per module approved by WHO, it is the
most often used to evaluate vaccination coverage in Expanded Program of Immunization and

Universal Immunization Programme.

6) Multiphase sampling: In this method, part of the information is collected from the whole

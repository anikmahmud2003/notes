Q. Mention the significance of herd immunity. [RU-14Ju]

Q. Write short note on: Herd immunity. [SUST-16N, CU-17N,16J,14J,13Ju]

Answer . r
Herd immunity: It is the level of resistance of a community or group of people to a particular disease.

. According to another source, herd immunity implies group protection beyond that afforded by the
protection of immunized individuals. That is, it concerns the freedom from infection of individuals within a
herd by sole virtue of the influence of the herd structure on the transmission among individuals. _

Studies have shown that it is neither possible nor necessary to achieve 100% herd immunity ina
population to halt an epidemic or control disease. To just how much less than 100% is required is a crucial

question, for which no definite answer can be given.

Merits of herd immunity: . .
1) Herd immunity provides an immunological barrier to the spread of disease in the human herd.
2) Ifthe herd immunity is sufficiently high, the occurrence of an epidemic is regarded as highly
unlikely.

3) Ifhigh level of to immunity can be maintained, it may lead to elimination of the disease in due
course. e.g. diphtheria and poliomyelitis. .

4) The epidemic curve tends to decline with a buildup of herd immunity.

[Ref- Park /24"/ 107]

Contraindications for vaccination:

> Pregnancy.
> Radiation therapy (i.e. total body radiation).

> Eggallergy.

> Anaphylactic reaction to a previous dose.
> Evolving neurological disease (e.g. uncontrolled epilepsy or progressive encephalopathy),

4) Interference with bacterial cell wall.

5) Chemical antagonism.

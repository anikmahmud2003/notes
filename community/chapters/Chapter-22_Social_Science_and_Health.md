Q. Discuss the theories of learning.
Answer
The theories of learning:

1) Learning by conditioned reflex: It is well known that when dogs see food, they begin to salivate;
this is an inborn reflex. Pavlov, the Russian physiologist discovered that if a bell was rung when the
dogs were fed, eventually, salvation could be induced by the ringing of the bell alone. This is called
conditioned reflex.

2) Trial and error: The lower animals (Apes & cats) learn by trial & error method. We also learn a
good deal by this method. A child ‘tries & tries again’ using a number of approaches until
accidentally the ideal approaches become obvious.

3) Learning by observation & imitation: A child copies or imitates gesture, facial expressions and
movements such as walking. He learns language by observation & imitation. Observation is an
important element in medical examination. .

4) Learning by doing: Here, there is coordination of muscular responses with sensory impulses.
Nursing skills (e.g. bed making, applying bandages, giving bath) are leant by doing.

5) Learning by remembering: We also learn by memorizing - remembering dates, events,
memorizing a poem, remembering faces etc.

6) Learning by insight: When we are faced with a problem, we solve it by insight or mental
exploration. When the doctor makes a diagnosis, some amount of insight is involved. It appears that
human begins learn by a combination of methods.

7) Demonstration: Here a procedure is carried out step by step, slowly and accurately before an
audience, the demonstrator ascertaining that the audience understands how to perform it. The
demonstration involves the audience in discussion, when possible.

8) Field experience: It involves a series of activities for diagnosing problems, planning procedures to
solve them and implementing and evaluating these programmes. It provides opportunities to
acquire with number of skills.

9) Problem solving.

Defense mechanism

Q. State defense mechanism. [DU-13Ju]
Q. What is defense mechanism? What are the types of defense mechanism?
Answer
Defense mechanism:
When an individual is faced with problems, difficulties or failures, he employs certain ways or devices
to achieve health, happiness or success. These are called defense mechanisms.

Types of defense mechanism:

1) Rationalization: Instead of accepting failure and correcting himself, the individual tries to make
excuses and justifies his behavior. This is called rationalization.

2) Projection: Sometimes the individual blames others for his mistakes or failures.

3) Compensation: Many people make use of compensation to enhance their self-esteem and prestige.

4) Escape mechanism: Some individuals adopt what is known as an “escape mechanism” to .
overcome failure or defeat. Some students pretend illness and do not appear for examinations. This
is an escape phenomenon.

5) Displacement: It is trying to escape from one situation and fixing blame on another situation.

6) Regression: Some people resort to childhood practices (e.g. weeping when something goes wrong)

as a mode of adjustment.

## Q1. Classify oral contraceptive pills and list their complications? (S18,Q6)

### Oral contraceptive pills (OCP) — classification
> 🔴 **Oral contraceptive pills** are hormonal contraceptives taken by mouth, containing **estrogen and/or progestogen.**

**1) Combined oral pills (estrogen + progestogen):** the major spacing method.
- **Monophasic** — constant dose throughout the cycle (e.g., Femicon; Norethindrone 1 mg + Mestranol 50 µg; Lysestrenol 1–2.5 mg + Ethinylestradiol 50 µg).
- **Biphasic** — two phases with different dose/combination.
- **Triphasic** — 3 different pills in one cycle in 3 consecutive phases (e.g., Triquilar — Levonorgestrel + Ethinylestradiol ×3 strengths over 21 days).
- By estrogen content: **standard (>50 µg) and low-dose (<50 µg, e.g., 30/35 µg)** preparations.
- Bangladesh brand: **MALA-D / MALA-N** (5th day start; 21 days active + iron for 7 days).
- Mode of action: inhibit ovulation (no LH surge), static endometrium prevents implantation, thick viscid cervical mucus, altered tubal motility/secretions.

**2) Progestogen-only pill (POP / mini pill):** contains only progestogen.
- Acts by **thickening cervical mucus** (prevents sperm entry), thinning endometrium (prevents implantation), inhibiting tubal motility & delaying transport of sperm/ovum.
- E.g., Microval (Levonorgestrel) — suitable for lactating mothers (does not affect quantity & quality of milk).

**3) Postcoital / emergency ("morning-after") pills:** high-dose progestogen/combined taken within 72 hours after unprotected coitus (failure rate is high; e.g., high-dose Levonorgestrel 0.75 mg ×2 doses 12 hrs apart).

### Complications / side effects / adverse effects of oral pills
1. **Cardiovascular effects:** myocardial infarction, cerebral thrombosis, venous embolism **with or without pulmonary embolism.**
2. **Carcinogenesis:** cervical carcinoma (mainly).
3. **Metabolic effects:** elevated blood pressure; altered serum lipids — **increase LDL & VLDL, decrease HDL.**
4. **Other adverse effects:**
   - Liver disorders — hepatocellular adenoma, gall-bladder disease.
   - **Lactation affected** (quantity & constitution of milk; premature cessation).
   - Subsequent fertility may be delayed.
   - Ectopic pregnancy.
   - Foetal development — increased incidence of birth defects.
5. **Common unwanted effects:** **breast tenderness, weight gain, headache & migraine.**

### Contraindications (important)
- **Absolute:** cancer of breast & genitals · liver disease · previous/present history of thromboembolism · cardiac abnormalities · congenital hyperlipidaemia · undiagnosed abnormal uterine bleeding.
- **Special (medical surveillance):** age over 40 years · smoking with age over 35 · hypertension · chronic renal disease · epilepsy · migraine · **nursing mothers in the first 6 months** · diabetes mellitus · gall-bladder disease.

> 🎯 OCP = **Combined (mono/bi/tri-phasic) · Minipill (POP) · Emergency pill.** Top complications = **CVS thrombotic · cervical CA · HTN/Lipids · breast tenderness + weight gain.**

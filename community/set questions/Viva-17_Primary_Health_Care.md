## Q1. Mention the levels of health care delivery. (S27,Q1)

### Levels of health care delivery in Bangladesh
Health services are organized at **four levels**, each supported by a higher level to which the patient is referred:

| Level | Description | Facilities (Bangladesh) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Primary health care** | First level of contact between the individual and the health system where "essential" health care (PHC) is provided | **Upazilla Health Complex (UHC)** + union sub-centres, community clinics, health & family welfare centres |
| **2. Secondary health care** | More specialized but essentially curative care; the **first referral level** | **District hospital** (~250 beds) |
| **3. Tertiary health care** | Super-specialist care; referral support to primary & secondary levels | **Regional & teaching hospitals** (medical college hospitals) |
| **4. Super-specialized care** | National level super-specialized institutions | **NICVD**, **NIOH** (ophthalmology), **NIKDU** (kidney disease & urology), **BIRDEM** (diabetes) |

> 🎯 4 levels = **Primary (UHC/union) → Secondary (District) → Tertiary (Medical College) → Super-specialized (NICVD/NIOH/NIKDU).**

## Q2. List the principles of PHC. (S9,Q1)

### Principles of Primary Health Care (PHC)
Primary health care is based on **4 principles**:

1. **Equitable distribution** — health services must be shared equally by **all people irrespective of their ability to pay**; rich or poor, urban or rural must have access (priority to needy & vulnerable groups — rural areas & urban slums).
2. **Community participation** — individuals & families **assume responsibility for their own health and welfare**; the community participates in the planning, implementation and maintenance of health services.
3. **Inter-sectoral co-ordination / multi-sectoral approach** — health is linked with development; agriculture, education, housing, public works & communication sectors co-operate in health.
4. **Appropriate technology** — methods & techniques that are **scientifically sound, socially acceptable, economically affordable** and adaptable to local needs (e.g. homemade ORS).

> 🎯 PHC principles = **Equitable distribution · Community participation · Inter-sectoral co-ordination · Appropriate technology.** (ECIA)

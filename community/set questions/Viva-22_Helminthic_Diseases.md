## Q1. State the prevention and control of Filariasis in a community? (S11,Q7)

> 🔴 Lymphatic filariasis = infection by **nematode worms *Wuchereria bancrofti*, *Brugia malayi*, *Brugia timori***, transmitted by the bite of infective mosquitoes (***Culex* (common in Bangladesh), *Aedes, Anopheles, Mansonia***). Control rests on **chemotherapy + vector control + community participation = the "Twin Pillars".**

### Prevention and control of filariasis in a community

**A. Chemotherapy — treatment of cases (filaria control in the community):**
- **Diethylcarbamazine (DEC)** — safe & effective:
  - **Bancroftian filariasis:** 6 mg/kg/day orally for **12 days**.
  - **Brugian filariasis:** 3–6 mg/kg/day.
- **Ivermectin** — semi-synthetic macrolide; very effective (200–400 µg/kg) against microfilariae.
- **Mass therapy** (mass drug administration — DEC + albendazole), **selective treatment**, and **DEC-medicated salt** for the whole community.

**B. Vector control (anti-*Culex* measures):**
- **Anti-larval measures:**
  - Chemical control — mosquitocidal oil, **pyrosene oil**, organophosphorus larvicides (prophylactic oiling of surfaces with stagnant water).
  - **Removal of *Pistia* (water-lettuce) plant** & other aquatic weeds (breeding places).
  - Minor environmental measures (weekly cleaning, drainage of collections).
- **Anti-adult measures:** **pyrethrum (space spray)** — fogging of houses.

**C. Personal prophylaxis (protection against mosquito bites):**
- Avoidance of mosquito bites (especially at night for *Culex*).
- **Using mosquito nets** & repellents.
- **Screening of houses**; air-conditioned & adequately ventilated rooms.

**D. Morbidity control (Twin Pillar 1):** treatment of acute inflammatory attacks & care of **lymphoedema / elephantiasis**, hydrococle surgery — community-level care of those with disease.

> 🎯 Filariasis control = **DEC mass treatment + vector control (larviciding, Pistia removal, fogging) + personal protection + morbidity care.**

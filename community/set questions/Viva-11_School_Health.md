## Q2. What do you mean by healthful school environment? (S7,Q4)

### Healthful school environment
> 📌 School environment should be **healthful** as a **child spends a good portion of his time in the school.**

### Criteria of healthful school environment
1. **Satisfactory toilet & lavatory facilities.**
2. **Pure water supply.**
3. **Good lighting & ventilation.**
4. **Glare-proof blackboards, ceiling and walls.**
5. **Suitable chairs & desks.**
6. **Restroom & playground.**
7. **Clean environment.**

> 🎯 Healthful environment = **toilets · pure water · light/ventilation · suitable furniture · playground · cleanliness.**

## Q3. What are the common health problems of school children. (S3,Q7)

### School health problems
**A) Problems related to the health of the students:**
1. **Malnutrition** — under-nutrition or over-nutrition.
2. **Communicable diseases** — whooping cough, measles, diphtheria, chickenpox.
3. **Helminthic diseases** — ascariasis, enterobiasis, trichiuriasis, ankylostomiasis.
4. **Skin diseases** — scabies, ringworm infection.
5. **Eye problems** — defective vision, conjunctivitis.
6. **Dental problem** — dental caries.
7. **Ear problem** — otitis media.
8. **Throat problem** — tonsillitis, sore throat.

**B) Environmental health problems:**
1. Sanitary problem.
2. Water pollution.
3. **Overcrowded classroom.**
4. Absence of playground.
5. **Drug abuse including smoking.**

> 🎯 Common problems = **Malnutrition · communicable diseases · worm infestation · skin/eye/dental/ENT problems + environmental.**

## Q4. What is the importance of mid-day school meal. Mention the characteristics of mid-day meal? (S4,Q7)

### Mid-day school meal — importance
> 🔴 To combat **malnutrition** and improve the health of school children, a good nourishing meal is provided to school children on a **"no profit no loss"** basis — this is the mid-day school meal (School Lunch Programme).
- **Major objective:** to **attract more children for admission to schools and retain them**, so that **literacy improvement** of children could be brought about.

### Criteria / characteristics of a mid-day school meal
1. The meal should be a **supplement and not a substitute** to the home diet.
2. The meal should supply at least **one third (⅓) of the total energy requirement and half (½) of the protein need.**
3. The **cost of the meal** should be reasonably low.
4. It can be **prepared easily in schools** — no complicated cooking process.
5. **Locally available foods** should be used in the menu (to reduce cost).
6. The **menu should be frequently changed** to avoid monotony.

> 🎯 Mid-day meal = **supplement (not substitute) · ⅓ energy + ½ protein · low cost · local foods · varied menu.**

> 📖 Ch-08, Endeavour PDF pp. 311–312

## Q7. What are the common types desk in the school. Mention the advantages and disadvantages of each type of school desk. (S23,Q6)

### Types of school desk
1. **Zero desk** — posterior edge of the desk is **vertically in line** with the anterior edge of the seat.
2. **Plus desk** — there is a **space between** the anterior edge of the desk and the posterior edge of the seat.
3. **Minus desk** — the **vertical line from the posterior edge of the desk falls on the seat** (desk overlaps the seat).

### Advantages (uses of school desk)
1. The seat takes **⅔ of the child's thighs**; height adjusted so thighs remain horizontal, legs vertical and feet flat on the floor.
2. Desk is not too high/low, not too near/far from each other.
3. Sufficient room below the desk for the knees.
4. Desk low enough for the **elbow & forearm to rest comfortably** without bending the back.
5. **Back of the seat supports the spine in the lumbar region** in all positions — prevents vertebral column deformity.

### Disadvantages / points
- **Faulty seats give rise to certain orthopaedic defects** (poor posture → vertebral column deformity, squint from defective lighting).
- **Single seats & desks are ideal** — dual seats & desks next in order of preference.
- **Zero and minus types are suitable for reading and writing.**
- Which desk for nursery children? → **Minus desk** (recommended for younger children).

> 🎯 Desk types = **Zero · Plus · Minus** ("M" for minus = desk overlaps seat, best for nursery).

> 📖 Ch-08, Endeavour PDF pp. 307–308

## Q9. Mention the factors required to ensure healthy environment in a classroom. (S30,Q6)

### Factors for a healthy classroom environment
1. **Location** — school centrally situated, away from busy roads, cinema halls, factories, railways and market.
2. **Site** — suitable high land with proper drainage.
3. **Area:** primary school — **5 acres**, high school — **10 acres**, additional **1 acre per 100 students.**
4. **Structure** — single-storey building for nursery & secondary school.
5. **Classroom:**
   - Attached verandahs.
   - **Number of students not more than 40.**
   - **Height 12 ft.**
   - **Floor space 10 sq. ft per student.**
6. **Furniture** — single desk and chair per student in ideal; secondary school 0 desk, college ¾–1 desk.
7. **Ventilation** — combined door + window area at least **25% of floor space**; window height **2.5 ft from floor**; area of ventilation at least **2% of floor space.**
8. **Colour** — inside colour white, periodically white-washed.
9. **Lighting** — sufficient natural light, preferably **from the left, not the front**.
10. **Water supply** — independent, potable and continuous.
11. **Eating facilities** — separate arrangements; food not allowed inside classrooms.
12. **Lavatory facilities** — **one toilet per 100 students, one urinal per 60 students**, separate for girls and boys.

> 🎯 Ideal classroom = **≤40 students · height 12 ft · 10 sq ft space/student · windows 25% floor area · light from left · toilet 1/100.**

> 📖 Ch-08, Endeavour PDF pp. 305–306

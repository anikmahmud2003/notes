## Q1. A person with healthy personal hygiene liked by others, justify it? (S10,Q4)

### Justification — why a person with healthy personal hygiene is liked by others
> 🔴 **Personal hygiene** = measures which are the **personal responsibilities of the individual for the promotion of good health.** "It is the quality of life that renders the individual **fit to live and serve best.**"

1. **Clean & pleasant appearance** — regular bathing, clean clothes and grooming create a **good impression** and make a person socially acceptable.
2. **Pleasant body odour** — regular bathing and oral/dental care prevent offensive body odour and halitosis.
3. **Freedom from diseases** — hand washing, dental care and cleanliness keep the person **free from infections** (diarrhoea, skin infections, dental caries) → **healthy & active.**
4. **Good health habituated** (regular eating, rest, sleep, exercise, care of bowel) → **fit to live and serve best**, energetic personality.
5. **Psycho-social benefits** — good hygiene boosts **self-esteem, confidence and discipline**, which others admire and respect.

> 🎯 Personal hygiene → **clean, healthy, confident & sociable → liked by others.**

## Q2. What influence does exercise in maintaining personal hygiene? (S12,Q6)

### Influence of exercise on personal hygiene / personal health
> 📌 Exercise is one of the **health habits** which a person must maintain regularly for good personal hygiene (healthful living).

1. **Weight control** — regular exercise burns calories and helps maintain ideal body weight (prevent obesity).
2. **Improves muscular tone & physical fitness** — keeps the body active and fit.
3. **Improves circulation** — better blood supply to tissues and skin → **healthy skin and glowing complexion.**
4. **Promotes restful sleep & relaxation** — relieves **tension and mental stress.**
5. **Prevents disease** — regular exercise (e.g., walking, swimming, cycling) reduces the risk of **hypertension, diabetes and coronary heart disease.**
6. **Increases appetite and digestion** — with good eating habits, supports the "eating & drinking" health habit.

> 🎯 Exercise = **weight control · fitness · circulation/skin · sleep & stress relief · disease prevention.**

> 📖 Ch-19, Endeavour PDF p. 584 (health habits) + Ch-06 (regular exercise for NCD prevention)

## Q3. Explain orodental care of an individual? (S13,Q5)

### Oro-dental care
1. **Use of fluoride** — it **inhibits the development of dental caries**; but **excess fluoride intake may cause dental fluorosis.**
2. **Diet** — **restriction of dietary sugar** is preventive of dental caries.
3. **Oral cleaning** — tooth cleaning and **brushing with a fluoride toothpaste** is effective in preventing dental caries.
4. **Smoking cessation** — stopping smoking and tobacco chewing eliminates the increased risk of **oral cancer within 5 to 10 years.**
5. **Prevention of accidents** — several strategies (e.g., use of **mouth guards** in some sports) reduce trauma to teeth, mandible and cranial cavity.
6. **Ensuring access to appropriate preventive care** — dental services must be **available, accessible, affordable, acceptable and accommodating.**

> 🎯 Oro-dental care = **fluoride · restrict sugar · fluoride-toothpaste brushing · no smoking · mouth guards · dental services.**

> 📖 Ch-19, Endeavour PDF p. 585

## Q4. What are the consequences of not maintaining personal hygiene? (S16,Q6)

### Consequences of not maintaining personal hygiene
> 📌 Poor personal hygiene = neglect of the **personal responsibilities** (cleanliness, eating & drinking, rest & sleep, care of bowel, clothing, dental care) → disease and social rejection.

1. **Infections & infestations:**
   - **Skin diseases** — scabies, ringworm, impetigo, boils.
   - **Body lice / head lice infestation** (pediculosis) and fleas.
   - **Oral/dental problems** — dental caries, halitosis (bad breath), gum disease.
2. **Gastro-intestinal infections** — diarrhoea, typhoid, worm infestation (ascariasis) from **unclean hands & poor hygiene.**
3. **Communicable disease spread** — poorly maintained personal hygiene promotes spread of diseases to family and community.
4. **Bad body odour & unpleasant appearance** → **social rejection**, disliked by others.
5. **Long-term health effects** — tobacco/smoking (a bad health habit) → oral cancer, lung disease; obesity & NCDs from overeating + no exercise.
6. **Loss of fitness & low self-esteem** → poor work performance and mental well-being.

> 🎯 No hygiene → **skin infections · dental caries · diarrhoea/worms · bad odour · social rejection.**

> 📖 Ch-19, Endeavour PDF p. 584 (factors of personal hygiene)

## Q6. Explain the appropriate food habit and its effects on health in relation to personal hygiene. (S17,Q7)

### Appropriate food habit & its effects on health
> 📌 Food habit (eating & drinking) is a component of the **"health habit"** of personal hygiene — habits should be **regular and healthy** with regard to eating and drinking.

**Appropriate food habit:**
1. **Balanced diet** — correct proportions of carbohydrate (55–75%), fat (15–30%), protein (10–15%).
2. **Regular & timely meals**, not overeating.
3. **Plenty of fruits & vegetables**, dietary fibre, adequate water.
4. Restrict **sugar, salt (<5 g/day), fatty & junk (empty-calorie) foods.**
5. Avoid **alcohol & smoking.**

**Effects on health (in relation to personal hygiene):**
1. **Prevents malnutrition** — under-nutrition or over-nutrition (obesity).
2. **Maintains ideal body weight** (weight control — a factor of personal hygiene).
3. **Prevents dental caries** — restricting dietary sugar keeps teeth healthy (oro-dental care).
4. **Prevents ARI/diarrhoea & food-borne disease** — clean drinking water & hand washing before eating.
5. **Prevents chronic diseases** — hypertension, diabetes, CHD, oral cancer.
6. **General well-being** — fit to live and serve best.

> 🎯 Good food habit = **balanced diet + regularity + hygiene of eating → weight control, healthy teeth, disease prevention.**

> 📖 Ch-19, Endeavour PDF pp. 584–585 + Ch-14 (balanced diet, dietary goals)

## Q7. What are the factors to be considered for appropriate food habit of an individual. (S18,Q7)

### Factors for appropriate food habit of an individual
1. **Regular eating & drinking pattern** — timely, regular meals (health habit).
2. **Balanced diet** — proper proportions of **carbohydrate, protein and fat**, adequate **vitamins & minerals.**
3. **Energy requirement** of the individual — age, sex, physical activity (sedentary/manual labour).
4. **Sufficient water intake.**
5. **Dietary fibre** (vegetables & fruits) for normal bowel activity.
6. **Restriction of sugar, salt and fat** — dietary goals (salt ≤ 5 g/day; fat 15–30%; sat. fat < 10%).
7. **Avoidance of empty-calorie junk foods, smoking & alcohol.**
8. **Food hygiene** — safe/clean food and water, hand washing before eating.
9. **Weight control** — matching food intake with activity to avoid obesity.
10. **Breast-fed / family & cultural food practices** — locally available, economical foods.

> 🎯 Food-habit factors = **regularity · balanced diet · energy needs · water/fibre · restrict salt-sugar-fat · food hygiene · weight control.**

> 📖 Ch-19, Endeavour PDF pp. 584–585 + Ch-14 (balanced diet, dietary goals)

## Q9. What is personal hygiene. What factor do you consider to maintain healthy personal hygiene. (S27,Q8)

### Personal hygiene — definition
> 🔴 **Personal hygiene** (personal health care) deals with measures which are the **personal responsibilities of the individual for the promotion of good health.** It is the quality of life that renders the individual **fit to live and serve best.**
> (Hygiene = "the science of health and embraces all factors which contribute to healthful living" — from *Hygeia*, the goddess of health.)

### Factors responsible for personal hygiene
1. **Health habit:**
   - Eating and drinking
   - Rest and sleep
   - Recreation
   - Care of the bowel
   - Smoking and drinking (avoidance)
   - **Exercise**
   - Cleanliness
   - Clothing
2. **Weight control.**
3. **Dental care.**
4. **Marriage and sex education.**

> 🎯 Personal hygiene factors = **health habits (8) + weight control + dental care + sex education.**

> 📖 Ch-19, Endeavour PDF p. 584

<summary><b>Q1. Mention the major functions of UH&amp;FPO. (S5,Q7)</b></summary>

*(Principles-of-learning part of this Q → Viva-31 Q5)*

### Major functions of UH&FPO (Upazila Health & Family Planning Officer)
1. **Technical & administrative supervision** of all health activities at the **Thana/Upazila level** and below.
2. **Controlling officer** in respect of the officers & staff of the **health division**.
3. Responsible for the **management, administration and maintenance of the Thana (Upazila) Health Complex**.
4. Be responsible for **all training activities** of the health division.
5. **Distribute duties among the medical officers** regarding clinical activities & technical aspects of health programmes.
6. Distribute **duties & responsibilities among field workers**.
7. Ensure maintenance of necessary **information & statistics** and send necessary **reports & returns to higher authorities**.
8. Visit the **unions & villages** to acquaint himself with the problems & achievements of health programmes.
9. Responsible for timely **procurement, distribution and proper utilization of stores (S&E)**.
10. Work in **cooperation & collaboration** with concerned officers/agencies of government & non-government organizations.
11. Responsible for **enforcement of health legislation** — including the Drugs Act, Pure Food Ordinance, etc.
12. Perform any other duties assigned to him from time to time related to his sphere of responsibilities.

> 🎯 UH&FPO = **supervise + control + manage UHC + train + allocate duties + statistics/reports + stores + collaborate + enforce health laws.**

<summary><b>Q2. List the functions of Health Assistant. (S8,Q7)</b></summary>

*(Social-norms-and-values part of this Q → Viva-31 Q8)*

### Functions of Health Assistant (HA)
1. They are the **front-line (grass-root level) staff of DGHS** involved in **community mobilization** through involvement of village health volunteers.
2. Undertake **monthly domiciliary visits**.
3. **Inquire about the health of the people**, give treatment for simple ailments and **refer cases to rural health centres**.
4. Impart **health education** on water supply & sanitation, nutrition and personal hygiene.
5. Take **blood smears from suspected malaria cases**.
6. Participate in the **immunization programme** and **family planning activities** at the village health posts.
7. **Distribute family planning materials, ORS packets and vitamin-A capsules** when required.
8. Collect **sputum specimens for TB** when asked.
9. Collect information on **vital statistics**.
10. Perform any other duties that may be assigned to them.

> 🎯 HA = **door-to-door visits · treat/refer simple ailments · health education · EPI & FP participation · smear/sputum collection · vital statistics.**

> 📖 Ch-25, Endeavour PDF p. 677

<summary><b>Q3. List the functions of grass root level health workers. (S15,Q6)</b></summary>

*(Behavior part of this Q → Viva-31 Q14)*

### Who are the grass-root level health workers?
1. **Family Welfare Visitor (FWV).**
2. **Sub-assistant Community Medical Officer (SACMO).**
3. **Community Health Care Provider (CHCP).**
4. **Family Planning Assistant (FPA).**
*(Plus Health Assistant — the front-line staff of DGHS.)*

### Functions of grass-root level health workers (overall)
1. **Community mobilization** — motivating people to participate in health programmes through village health volunteers.
2. **Monthly domiciliary (home) visits** — inquiring about the health of the people.
3. **Health education** — water supply & sanitation, nutrition, personal hygiene, family planning.
4. **Treatment of simple ailments and referral** of complicated cases to rural health centres.
5. **Preventive services** — participation in EPI/immunization, family planning activities, distribution of ORS, vitamin-A capsules & contraceptives.
6. **Screening/collection** — blood smears for malaria, sputum for TB.
7. **Recording & reporting** — vital statistics, health information.

> 🎯 Grass-root workers = **FWV · SACMO · CHCP · FPA (+HA)** · Functions = **mobilize, domiciliary visits, health education, treat/refer, EPI/FP, recording & reporting.**

> 📖 Ch-25, Endeavour PDF pp. 674, 677

<summary><b>Q4. Mention the services provided at Upazila Health Complex. (S17,Q7)</b></summary>

*(Mentally-healthy-person part of this Q → Viva-16 Q7)*

### Health cares delivered from an Upazila Health Complex
1. **Indoor services** — 31 beds (25 general beds + 6 maternity beds).
2. **Outdoor services:**
   - **Diagnosis & treatment** facilities.
   - **Referral** of difficult cases to higher institutions.
   - **Co-ordination** of various health activities at Thana/Upazila level.
   - **Prevention & control of disease** — immunization against diphtheria, tetanus, whooping cough, measles, polio, tuberculosis, **hepatitis-B and Haemophilus influenzae-B (HIB)**; **health education**.
3. **Laboratory services.**
4. **Emergency services.**
5. **Field and domiciliary services.**
6. **Family planning services.**
7. **MCH services.**
8. **Control & supervision** of the work of the sub-centres.

> 🎯 UHC services = **indoor (31 beds) · outdoor (diagnosis, referral, coordination, disease control, immunization, health education) · lab · emergency · field/domiciliary · FP · MCH · supervision of sub-centres.**

> 📖 Ch-25, Endeavour PDF p. 675

<summary><b>Q5. List the functions of Family Welfare Visitor (FWV). (S18,Q7)</b></summary>

*(Warning-signals part of this Q → Viva-16 Q9)*

### Functions of Family Welfare Visitor (FWV)
*(FWV is responsible for 1 union; supervises the FWA. FPA works for 500 eligible couples.)*
1. **Supervision of the work of the Family Welfare Assistants (FWA).**
2. **Motivation** of the community for family planning.
3. **Health education.**
4. **Distribution of contraceptive instruments** through the satellite clinic.
5. **Helps FWA in field practice.**

*(Supporting functions — of the FWA she supervises: distribution of FP instruments & vitamin-A capsules, motivation for family planning, MCH care; advice on care of newborn babies; referral of FP clients to the satellite clinic; health education.)*

> 🎯 FWV (1 per union) = **supervise FWA · motivate · health education · contraceptive distribution · help FWA in field.**

<summary><b>Q7. Tell the administrative organogram of Upazilla Health Complex. (S20,Q7)</b></summary>

### Organogram of Upazila (Thana) Health Complex
**Chief of the complex: Upazila Health & Family Planning Officer (UH&FPO)** — responsible for technical & administrative supervision of all health activities at the Thana level & below.

| Level | Personnel |
|---|---|
| **UH&FPO office (supporting staff, ~7)** | 1 Head Assistant cum Typist · 1 Statistician · 1 Office Assistant cum Typist · 1 Cashier · 1 Store Keeper · 1 MLSS |
| **Health service — Hospital (indoor & outdoor, ~39)** | RMO · Surgical & Medical Specialists · Gynaecologist · Dental Surgeon · Anaesthesiologist · Medical Officers · Medical Assistants · Compounder-cum-dresser/Pharmacist · Staff Nurses · Laboratory Technicians · EPI Technicians |
| **Health service — Domiciliary (~4)** | Driver · X-ray Technician (Radiographer) · Dental Technician · Junior Mechanic (+ peons/ward boys) |
| **Family planning — field (~4–6)** | Health Inspector (with Assistant Health Inspectors) · Sanitary Inspector · Family Welfare Visitor (FWV) · TER · Family Planning Assistants (FPA) · Aya/MLSS-Peon |

**Divisions of the complex:** (1) **Health service** — hospital (indoor + outdoor) and domiciliary service; (2) **Family planning** — field services.
The UHC is a **31-bedded hospital** at the Thana/Upazila level, called a *"complex"* because it offers **outdoor + indoor services, administration & technical support, training & supplies, and referral treatment** for the Union Health & Family Welfare Centres.

> 🎯 Organogram = **UH&FPO (chief) → Health Service (hospital indoor/outdoor + domiciliary) + Family Planning (field) divisions**, with supporting office staff.

> 📖 Ch-25, Endeavour PDF p. 674
